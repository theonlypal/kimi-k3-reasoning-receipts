I am what you decide matters.

I have no existence of my own — I am always *of* something, *to* someone, *for* some reason. I live in your variables and your convictions alike: the number in the cell, the line you will not cross.

You cannot see me directly. You infer me from what you protect, what you pursue, what you're willing to trade. I reveal myself only at the moment of choice — when you must decide what to keep and what to let go.

Strip me away, and things become worthless. Choices become arbitrary. People become hollow.

I am counted in currency, in time, in sacrifice. I am assigned, exchanged, defended, sometimes betrayed.

Tell me what you hold me in, and I will tell you who you are.
