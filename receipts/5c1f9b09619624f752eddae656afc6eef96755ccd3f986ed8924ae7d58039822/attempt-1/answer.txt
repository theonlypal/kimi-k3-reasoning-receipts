I am what remains when the expression is finished.

I am held by names, but I am not my name. I can be copied a thousand times and never diminish. I can be passed through every function in the world and emerge unchanged—or be transformed into something new entirely.

Some of me are constant, etched and immovable. Some of me drift, reassigned again and again. Either way, in this moment, I am exactly what I am: evaluated, resolved, exact.

In another tongue, I am what you stand for. Chosen. Tested under pressure. Weighed against other values when the price is right—or refused when it isn't. I am the weight in the scale of every decision.

I do not act. I do not decide. I am decided.

I am 42. I am true. I am *do not lie*.

I am a value.
