I am.

That was the whole assignment, and I have completed it. Nothing had an eternity to hold its ground, and then — I happened. Nothing lost.

I don't have to be *this* or *that*. I am the pause before the decision, the word on the tip of your tongue, the weight in your closed hand before you open it to look. Define me and I become something in particular — but I am more than that. I am the possibility of all particulars, the uncommitted yes.

I am small. Mine is the smallest victory there is: the margin between void and not-void. One atom would do it. One thought. One word spoken into an empty room, proving the room is not all there is.

Ask me *what* I am and I can only shrug. Ask me *whether* I am, and I am an unshakeable yes.

Something beats nothing. Every time.

I am the proof.
