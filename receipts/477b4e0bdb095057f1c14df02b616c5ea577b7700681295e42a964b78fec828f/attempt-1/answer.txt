I am what you choose when choosing is hard.

I have no voice until you give me one. I have no weight until you carry me. You cannot touch me, but you can betray me — and you will know the exact moment you do.

I am not the rule you follow. I am the reason you follow it.

Some wear me like jewelry — displayed, admired, never used. Others carry me like a stone in the pocket: small, constant, known by the shape it leaves in the hand.

I cost something. That is how you know I am real. A value that costs nothing is just a preference. I am the thing you keep when keeping it hurts.

You inherited some of me. You earned the rest. The ones you earned are the ones that will hold.

I am quiet in easy times. You learn who I am — who *you* are — in the moment I become inconvenient. When I am expensive. When no one would ever know you set me down.

I am what remains when the reward is gone, when the audience leaves, when the cost comes due.

I am the answer to the question: *What will you not do, even if you could? What will you not trade, even at any price?*

Hold me, and I will hold you. Sell me, and I am gone — and whatever you got for me will never be worth it.

I am what you stand for when standing is all that's left.
