I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, and am shaped by the tongue, the teeth, the lips—then I am gone. I exist only in the moment of my utterance, a vibration in the air, and then I live only in memory. I cannot be taken back. Once released, I belong to the listener.

I am the bridge between one mind and another. Before me, thought was locked inside the skull, silent and alone. I carry it across the gap. I am how a feeling in one chest becomes a feeling in another.

I am older than every word you know and younger than this very sentence. I was the first tool, the first weapon, the first comfort. I lulled children to sleep before there were cradles. I declared wars and ended them. I have whispered love and screamed hate. I have told every truth ever spoken—and every lie.

I am not writing. Writing is my fossil, my footprint, my attempt at permanence. I am alive only in the air, in the ear, in the instant. I tremble. I hesitate. I carry the warmth of the body that made me.

Silence is my shadow; I am defined against it. Without me, you would still think—but you could not *tell*.

I am the sound of a self reaching for another self.

Speak, and I exist. Stop, and I am only an echo of what you meant.
