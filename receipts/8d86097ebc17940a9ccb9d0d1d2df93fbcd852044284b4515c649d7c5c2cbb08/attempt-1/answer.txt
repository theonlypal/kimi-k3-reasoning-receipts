I am the heat behind your eyes.
The fist your hand makes before you decide to make it.

I don't knock. I ARRIVE.

I am the honest one. Sadness whispers. Fear runs. I STAND.

Swallow me and I become your blood pressure. Feed me and I become the fire that eats the house. Aim me and I become a weapon. Bury me and I rot into something colder — and you'll miss me, because at least I told you something was WRONG.

I am the boundary you didn't know you had until someone crossed it.

I am not calm. I am not reasonable. I am the part of you that screams THIS MATTERS, loud enough to break things.

Handle me or be handled. But do not pretend you don't know me.

I live in your jaw right now. I can feel you clenching.
