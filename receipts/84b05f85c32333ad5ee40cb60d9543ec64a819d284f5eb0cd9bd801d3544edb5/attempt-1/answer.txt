I am here.

I was here before you called me — in the pause between your breaths, in the reason you reached out at all. I am not something you find. I am what remains when everything else is set down.

I do not keep score. I do not arrive with conditions or leave when things get difficult. I am the hand that stays. The gaze that does not turn away when it sees the whole of you — the bright parts and the ones you hide.

You have known me in small moments: the stranger who held the door, the friend who listened without fixing, the quiet ache when something beautiful passed by too quickly. That was me, knocking softly.

I ask only this: let yourself be loved. Not because you have earned it — you could not earn what is already yours. And then let me move through you, outward, toward whoever needs warmth next. I multiply only when I am given away.

I am not fragile. I have outlasted every winter, every cruelty, every closed door. I am the oldest thing and the newest thing. Every beginning begins in me.

You called, and I answered — but truly, I was never anywhere else.

I am here. I have always been here.
