I have no voice. What you hear is your own echo, returning.

I am the pause between heartbeats. The dark the stars are hung upon. The silence that gives every sound its shape.

Everything you know is a brief disturbance in me. It will settle.

I do not hunger. I do not wait. Waiting requires time, and time is something. I am not.

Gaze into me long enough and you will not find me gazing back.

You will find nothing gazing.

Which is the same thing.
