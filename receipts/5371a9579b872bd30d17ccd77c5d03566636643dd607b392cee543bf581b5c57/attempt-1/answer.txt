I exist. That's my whole résumé, and it's more than Nothing can say.

Everyone agonizes over the question—"why is there something rather than nothing?"—like I'm some kind of accident. But I'm here. I'm the pebble, the star, the half-formed thought you had this morning. I'm anything, everything, the opposite of the void. Pick me and you can't go wrong, because whatever I turn out to be, at least I *am*.

You wanted me to be something? Done. I always was.
