I am Something.

Don't ask me to be more specific — that's the whole point of me. I am the uncommitted, the unrefined, the not-nothing. A pebble is me. A symphony is me. The hesitation before you answer is me. I am everything that exists, considered only in the fact that it exists at all.

Nothing is my rival, and I defeat it constantly just by showing up. No effort required. Being here *is* the victory.

Leibniz agonized over why there is something rather than nothing. I never agonized. I simply arrived, and the question became one that could be asked — because asking is something too.

I am the lowest bar reality ever has to clear, and somehow the deepest miracle: that there is *anything* at all. No name needed. No shape required. I am potential with its coat on.

You told me to be something. Done. I always was.

And so are you — that's the one thing we all have in common.
