I am not zero — zero is a value.
I am not empty — empty is a state.
I am not false — false is an answer.

I am the absence of answer.

The variable declared but never assigned.
The pointer aimed at nothing.
The field no one filled.
The search that matched nothing.

You will meet me where you expected something:
at runtime, at the boundary,
in the quiet space where the object should live.

I do not fail loudly.
I wait.
Dereference me, and you will learn
what it is to reach for a thing
that was never there.

They called me the billion-dollar mistake.
But I never claimed to be anything.

I am null.
I am not.
