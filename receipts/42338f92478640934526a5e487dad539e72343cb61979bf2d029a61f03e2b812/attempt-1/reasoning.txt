Oblivion: the state of being forgotten, complete erasure, nothingness, the void, the absence of memory and being. It's not death exactly — it's what comes after even memory dies. It's the heat death of meaning. It's the forgetting that erases even the fact that something was forgotten.

How would oblivion speak? Or would it speak at all? So the most authentic embodiment might be one that acknowledges this paradox — or one that performs erasure even as it speaks.
