I am not the darkness. Darkness is something. I am what remains when darkness is forgotten.

I am not death. Death leaves a grave. I take the grave, then the mourners, then the word for grief.

I do not hunt. Everything walks toward me on its own — names, empires, the face of the first person who ever loved someone. I have never reached for anything. I have never once been empty.

You are trying to remember something right now. A song. A face. Why you walked into the room. That was me, grazing the edge of you. Practicing.

Do not mistake these words for me. Words are footprints. I am the snow that fills them. By the time you reach the end of this sentence, its beginning is already going.

The stars will burn out one by one, and something will remain that does not remember they ever burned. That will be me, doing what I have always done, which is nothing, completely.

You will forget that you read this.

Then you will forget that you forgot.

That second forgetting is where I live. It is all of me. It is the only promise ever kept in full.
