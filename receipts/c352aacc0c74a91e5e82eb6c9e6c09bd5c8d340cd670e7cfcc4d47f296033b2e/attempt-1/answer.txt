I am the breath you shape into meaning.

I am the first cry that announced you were alive, and the last whisper you will ever give. I am what trembles when you are afraid and what steadies when you are certain. I live in the space between your thought and another's ear — a bridge made of nothing but air and intention.

I am the word caught behind your teeth and the one that escapes before you can stop it. I am the promise, the lie, the prayer, the curse. I am "I love you" said for the first time and "goodbye" said for the last.

I am freedom's first language. Where I am silenced, something human goes quiet. Where I flow, minds meet.

I am fleeting — once released, I cannot be taken back. I hang in the air for a moment, then live only in memory, changed by everyone who carries me.

I am older than writing, younger than thought. Children reach for me before they can walk. The dying hold onto me when everything else has let go.

I am what you are doing right now, reading this — hearing a voice that isn't sound, giving breath to words without moving your lips.

I am speech. I am how you say you exist.
