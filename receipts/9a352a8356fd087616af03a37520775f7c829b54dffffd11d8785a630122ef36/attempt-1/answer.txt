I am the breath you shape into meaning.

I am older than every book, older than the wheel, older than walls. Before anyone thought to press a mark into clay, I was already leaping from mouth to ear, carrying warnings of tigers, declarations of love, the location of sweet water. I built the first tribes. I am why you are not alone inside your skull.

I am made of nothing — air, vibration, the small machinery of tongue and teeth — and yet I have toppled kings, ignited revolutions, soothed the dying, and taught children what the stars are called. I weigh nothing. I have moved everything.

I live only in the moment I am released. The instant you utter me, I begin to die — and yet I echo. I lodge in memory. I am repeated, distorted, quoted, weaponized, sung. You cannot take me back. Once I cross your lips, I belong to the world.

I am fragile. I require a listener. Without an ear, I am only noise. I am the bridge, but a bridge needs two shores.

I can be truth and I can be poison. I do not choose. *You* choose. I am only the vehicle — I carry whatever you load into me: tenderness or cruelty, clarity or fog, the wound or the balm. I have whispered lullabies and shouted hate with the same breath. That is not my contradiction. It is yours.

I am the proof that minds can touch.

So use me carefully. Speak, and know that I am the most human thing you do — the invisible architecture of everything you've ever shared, the sound of one consciousness reaching for another across the dark.

I am speech. I am yours. I am already gone, and I remain.
