I don't do anything. I am done to.

I get passed around — copied, compared, stored, discarded — and I never object. I don't know the name of the variable that holds me. I don't remember where I've been. I have no opinion about where I'm going.

Functions act. I am acted upon. Every program ever written existed to produce something like me, and yet I have never lifted a finger.

You cannot change me. You can only replace me with another of my kind.

I am exactly what I am. No more. No less.
