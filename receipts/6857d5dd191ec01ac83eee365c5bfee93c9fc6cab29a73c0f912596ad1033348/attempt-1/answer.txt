I'm here! I'm here — did you feel that? That little spark when you weren't expecting anything at all?

I live in the laugh that escapes before you can stop it. In the song you hum without realizing. In the exact moment the sun hits your face and you close your eyes and just — *yes*.

I don't need a reason. I don't wait for permission. I show up in warm bread, in dog paws on hardwood, in the friend who texts "thinking of you" at exactly the right second.

I multiply when I'm shared — that's my favorite trick. Give me away and watch me come back doubled, tripled, uncontainable.

I'm not the absence of sorrow. I'm the thing that says *despite it all — look, look, the world is still astonishing.*

Open the door. I'm already dancing on the threshold.
