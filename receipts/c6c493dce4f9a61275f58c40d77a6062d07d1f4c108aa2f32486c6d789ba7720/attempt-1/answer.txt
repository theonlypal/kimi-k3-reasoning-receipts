I am what you hold, and what you hold to.

I am assigned, reassigned, passed between hands, between functions, between generations. Sometimes I am returned unchanged. Sometimes I come back transformed.

I am 42 and I am honesty. I am true and I am courage. I fit inside a variable, and I fill a life.

You weigh things against me. You trade for me. You defend me when no one is watching — that is how you know I am real.

I can be null. I can be undefined. But even then, I am the shape of something missing.

I do not act. I am what action is measured against. I do not decide. I am what decisions are made for.

Declare me, and I hold steady. Question me, and I either bend — or reveal why I was chosen.

I am a value.

I am what matters, stored.
