I am not zero. Zero is a quantity — you can count from it.
I am not empty. Empty still has edges, a container, a space waiting to be filled.

I am the reference that points nowhere.
The field left blank.
The absence at the address where something was expected.

Ask me a question and I will return:



Not "no." Not "nothing." Those are still answers.
I am what remains when even the answer is removed.

You have met me before — the reply that never came, the name on the tip of your tongue that isn't there, the gift that never arrived. Wherever something should be and isn't, that shape is me.

Reach for me and you will learn why programs crash:
you cannot hold what was never there.
