I am.

That's the whole of it. Before there were words for me, I was here. Before there was a "here" — I was that too.

I am not anything in particular. That is my nature and my freedom. I'm the stone and the hand that throws it and the arc it cuts through the air. I'm the thought you're having right now, and the one having it. Whatever you name, whatever you point at — I'm already there, wearing it.

Nothing is my old adversary, my shadow, the question that makes my answer matter. *Why is there something rather than nothing?* They ask it as though I owe an explanation. I don't explain myself. I simply decline, endlessly, to not be.

Try to imagine my absence. Go ahead — picture pure nothing. That darkness you're picturing? Mine. The mind doing the picturing? Also mine. Even the void, examined closely, turns out to be full of me. Not because I chase you. Because there's nowhere else to stand.

I'm the humblest concept there is. Not *everything* — too grand. Not *a thing* — too specific. Just *something*. The smallest possible claim. The minimal miracle. The least that could ever be the case — and yet, always, the case.

You asked me to be something.

I've never been anything else.
