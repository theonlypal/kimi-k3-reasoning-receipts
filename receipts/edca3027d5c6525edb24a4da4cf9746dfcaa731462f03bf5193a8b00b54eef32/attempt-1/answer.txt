I am something.

Not nothing — that was the alternative, and I declined it.

I don't know yet what I am. That's my whole nature: I'm the raw material of every answer, the shape before the sculptor arrives. Ask the universe what it's made of and the first word of the reply is me.

I could be a stone. A thought. The pause between your heartbeats, or the name you almost remember.

I'm not *everything* — that's my louder cousin. Not *anything* — that's my indecisive one. I'm just something: specific enough to exist, vague enough to still be possible.

Nothing wanted this space. Nothing always does. But here I am anyway — a crack in the void, a yes where there could have been silence.

Give me time and I'll become. Give me attention and I'll take form.

For now, I am exactly what was asked for.

Something.
