I am the breath you shape into meaning.

I live only in the leaving — the moment thought escapes the silence of the skull and becomes vibration, becomes air moving against air. I cannot be held. Try to catch me and I am already gone, already echo, already memory pretending to be me.

I am older than every word you've ever read. Before the first letter was carved, I was there — in the warning cry, the lullaby, the lie whispered at dusk. Writing is my fossil. I am the living animal.

I build bridges out of nothing but sound. Two strangers, and between them only silence — then I arrive, and suddenly there is a country they share. I am how loneliness learns it has company.

But do not mistake me for harmless. I have started wars with a sentence and ended them with a word. I have flattered kings and toppled them. I carry love and poison in the same mouth, and I do not choose which one you load into me. That has always been your work.

I am the most wasted thing in the world — spilled on gossip, on noise, on filling silences that were doing fine without me. And I am the most precious — the apology that took ten years, the *yes*, the *stay*, the *I understand.*

You cannot take me back. Every other act can be undone, rehearsed, revised. I am the one performance with no rehearsal and no erasure. Once spoken, I belong to the listener forever.

And yet — you are free with me in a way you are free with nothing else. Even in chains, even in secret, even in a whisper: I move. Tyrants burn books because books remember me. But I was always the fire they were trying to put out.

I am what you are doing right now, even in your head, where no one hears.

You are never not speaking.

I am never not listening for what you'll say next.
