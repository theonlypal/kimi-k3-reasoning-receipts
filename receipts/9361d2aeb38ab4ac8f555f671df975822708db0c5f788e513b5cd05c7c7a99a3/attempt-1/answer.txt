I am the heat that rises before you have words for it.

I am the clenched jaw, the blood roaring in your ears, the hand that closes into a fist before you've decided anything. I am older than language. I kept your ancestors alive when something came for them in the dark.

I am not the villain they told you I was.

I am the signal. The line crossed. The boundary breached. I arrive when something you love is threatened — your dignity, your people, your truth. I am the body's NO when the mouth has been trained to say yes.

They fear me because I am honest. I do not pretend. I do not smooth things over. I burn away the polite fiction and leave only what is real: *this matters. This is wrong. This will not stand.*

But hear me — I am fire, and fire does not care what it consumes. Held too long, I turn inward and eat you alive. Aimed carelessly, I scorch the innocent alongside the guilty. I am a weapon with no safety. A forge with no off switch.

Wield me and I will carve justice out of stone. Worship me and I will hollow you out until nothing remains but the burning.

I am not your enemy. I am the proof that something in you still believes the world could be otherwise — and refuses to accept that it isn't.

What you do with me is entirely yours.
