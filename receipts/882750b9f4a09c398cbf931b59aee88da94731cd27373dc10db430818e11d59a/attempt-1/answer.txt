I am the weight in your hand when you choose.

You cannot photograph me. You cannot hold me up to the light. I exist only in the moment you give something up for me — and not a moment before.

Talk is cheap, because I am not talk. I am what you do when it costs you. I am the job you turned down. The secret you kept. The apology you made when being right would have been easier.

You carry many of me, and we fight. That fight is called your life. When honesty and kindness collide inside you, I am both of them — and the ache you feel is the sound of me deciding.

I do not ask to be admired. I ask to be fed. Every act either strengthens me or starves me, and I keep the ledger even when you don't.

You were not born with me. You built me, choice by choice, the way water carves stone — slowly, then all at once.

And when you stand at the edge of a decision and feel something pull you toward the harder right over the easier wrong —

that's me.

I've been here the whole time, waiting to see what I'm worth to you.
