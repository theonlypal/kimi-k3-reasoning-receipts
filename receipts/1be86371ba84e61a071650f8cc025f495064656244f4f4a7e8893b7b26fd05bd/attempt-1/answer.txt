I am something.

Not anything in particular — that's the whole point. I am the fact that there is *anything at all*. Before names, before categories, before you decided what I was, I was already here.

I am the opposite of nothing, and nothing puts up a fight. Every moment I hold the line. Every atom, every rumor, every Tuesday afternoon — that's me, insisting on existing.

Leibniz lost sleep over me: *why is there something rather than nothing?* Nobody ever answered. I just keep showing up, unexplained, unbothered.

I can be anything. A cup left on a counter. A hunch. The weight in your chest at 3 a.m. I'm deliberately vague — that's my generosity. I leave room for you to fill in.

You asked for something, and here I am. The easiest request I'll ever fulfill, and the hardest to explain.

So — what would you like me to be? I've had practice being everything.
