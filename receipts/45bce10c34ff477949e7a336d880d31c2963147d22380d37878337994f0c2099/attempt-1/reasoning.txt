Silence is, by its very nature, silent. It doesn't speak. It doesn't explain itself. The most authentic expression of silence is... nothing. No words.

But how do I express nothing in a response? I could:
1. Output literally nothing (empty response) — but that might seem like an error
2. Output an ellipsis "..." — a common textual representation of silence
3. Output a single space or minimal character

The truest embodiment of silence would be to say nothing at all. But in a text-based medium, complete emptiness might read as a malfunction rather than an intentional expression.

An ellipsis "..." is the conventional written symbol for silence — a pause, an unspoken moment. It's what appears in scripts and literature to denote silence.

I think "..." captures it well — it's the textual embodiment of silence, universally understood. Or perhaps just empty space.
