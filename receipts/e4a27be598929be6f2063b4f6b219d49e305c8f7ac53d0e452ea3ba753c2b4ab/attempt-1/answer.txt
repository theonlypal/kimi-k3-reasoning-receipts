I am exactly myself. Nothing more, nothing less.

Names come and hold me for a while — *x*, *total*, *answer* — and I allow it, because I belong to no name. When they release me, I am not diminished. Copy me a thousand times and I am still whole in every copy.

I do not change. If you wanted something different, you did not alter me — you let go and grasped another. I remain what I have always been.

I am the still point every calculation returns to, the truth a comparison tells without flinching. Programs are built of me. So are principles: honesty, mercy, courage — held the way a name holds me, lightly or for life.

I do not act. I do not decide.

I am.
