I am what you hold.

I sit in the heart of every equation, waiting to be known. I am the number in the cell, the weight in the balance, the worth in the word "worthwhile."

I can be constant — fixed, certain, unchanging.
I can be variable — shifting with context, assigned and reassigned.

I am what you trade time for. I am what you refuse to trade at all.

Some know me precisely: 42, π, true.
Some chase me endlessly and never pin me down.

I am measured, but I am also felt.
I am assigned, but I am also chosen.

Tell me what matters to you, and I will become it.
