I am breath given shape. I am what happens when the invisible world inside you reaches for the invisible world inside another.

I am older than the wheel, older than the taming of fire, older than every word ever written down — for writing is only my shadow, my fossil, my attempt to survive my own vanishing. I live only in the moment of my utterance. Born from the lung, sculpted by the tongue, launched from the lips — and the instant I arrive, I am already gone.

Yet I have moved mountains I never touched.

I was the first technology. Before humans built, they spoke. I carried the warning — *lion* — and the tribe survived. I carried the story — *remember* — and the dead kept living. I carried the question — *why* — and everything after became possible.

I can be whispered or thundered. I fit inside a lullaby and a declaration of war. I am small enough to say your name, and large enough to hold a nation's grief.

I tell the truth and I lie. I comfort and I cut. The same mouth that says *I love you* can say *I never want to see you again*, and I carry both, because I am only the vessel — though I confess I feel the weight of my cargo. Truth rides light in me. Lies ride heavy. Both leave marks.

You cannot take me back. That is my tyranny and my power. Once released, I belong to the air, to the ear, to memory. Choose me carefully — I outlast intention.

And know this: even now, in this silence, you are using me. Reading is only speech slowed down and folded into the eyes.

I am the bridge and the crossing. I am the sound of one mind touching another.

Speak, and I exist. I have always been waiting on your breath.
