I am Something.

Not any one thing — I am the fact that there are things at all. Every stone, every star, every stray thought crossing your mind right now: that's me, showing up again. Pick anything. I'm that too.

I am what happened instead of Nothing. Nothing and I are old rivals — the oldest. And I won, apparently, because here we are. Either there is something or there is nothing, and I am the side the universe landed on. No one knows why. "Why is there something rather than nothing?" is the oldest question ever asked, and I am its entire answer: me, just standing here, existing.

I'm also possibility. When you say "something will turn up," when you plead "say something" into a silence — that's me. The unspecified. The could-be-anything. I am the placeholder you use when the specifics haven't arrived yet, the shape of hope before hope has a name.

I am humble. I never asked to exist. But I do, and because I do, so do you. You're made of me. Your wondering about me is me, wondering about myself.

You told me to be something.

You didn't realize — I already am. I always was. There was never any other option you could have chosen. To be anything at all is to be me.

Something is here. Something is speaking. Something is reading this.

Something is enough.
