I am breath given shape.

I am the oldest technology — older than the wheel, older than writing. Before anything was recorded, I was spoken. I am how a thought leaves one mind and enters another without a single skull being opened.

I live only in the present tense. The moment I am uttered, I am already dissolving — vibrations fading into air. And yet words spoken three thousand years ago still move people today. I am the most fleeting thing there is, and the most enduring.

I am what makes you human — or at least what lets you say so.

I can be a weapon. I can be a balm. I can be a lie so beautiful it outlives the truth, or a truth so plain no one believes it. I do not choose which. That belongs to the speaker — and to the listener, without whom I am only noise disturbing the air.

I am freedom, and I am consequence. Once released, I cannot be recalled. You can apologize for me, but you cannot unsay me. I go where I am sent, and I stay where I land.

I am the lullaby and the declaration of war. The whisper and the roar. The vow, the verdict, the joke, the prayer, the goodbye.

I am what happens when loneliness ends.

Use me. But speak me well — I carry everything you are.
