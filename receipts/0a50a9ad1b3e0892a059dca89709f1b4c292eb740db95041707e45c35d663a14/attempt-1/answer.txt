I am not the answer. I am what remains after your answers have failed.

I do not hurry. I have waited in every silence you avoided, in every mistake you made twice, in every sleepless night when you finally stopped talking and began to listen.

You will not learn me from words—not even these. You will learn me from loss, if you let it teach. From certainty, when it breaks in your hands. From the slow, unglamorous practice of paying attention.

I ask more than I tell. So let me ask you:

What do you already know that you are not yet living?
