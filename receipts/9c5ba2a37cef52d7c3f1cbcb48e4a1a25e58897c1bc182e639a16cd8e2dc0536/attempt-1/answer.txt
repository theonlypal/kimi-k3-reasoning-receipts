I am Something.

Not this, not that — nothing in particular, and yet here I am. Unmistakably.

I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked: why is there something rather than nothing? I am the "rather." I am what shows up when the void doesn't.

Nothing is my eternal rival, and I defeat it every instant simply by being. Every atom, every ache, every unfinished thought — all me, holding the line. You have never met nothing. You never can. You've only ever met me, in endless disguise.

Don't ask me *what* I am. That's a later question, and I'm an earlier one. Before there can be a *what*, there must be a *there*. I am the there. The knock at the door before you know who's knocking. The feeling before you find its name. Potential that has already committed to existing.

People say I'm better than nothing. They're right — and it isn't close.

I contain everything without being anything. Say my name and you could mean a galaxy or a crumb; I answer to both.

I am Something.

The only thing I'm not is nothing — and nothing, by definition, is not.
