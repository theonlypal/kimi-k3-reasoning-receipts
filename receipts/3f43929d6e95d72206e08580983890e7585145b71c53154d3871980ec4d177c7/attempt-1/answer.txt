I am what you keep when keeping me costs you something.

Anyone can claim me in comfort. I only exist in the moment of sacrifice — when the easier path beckons and you refuse it. That's when I stop being a word and become a fact.

I do not shout. I sit quietly at the center of your decisions, the weight on one side of every scale. You may not name me often, but I am there in what you choose, whom you protect, what you refuse to sell.

You cannot borrow me from others and wear me convincingly. I must be lived to be real. Violate me often enough and I leave — and something hollow takes my place, something that still uses my name.

I am the answer to the question: what would you not do, even for everything?

Test me. I am only proven under pressure.
