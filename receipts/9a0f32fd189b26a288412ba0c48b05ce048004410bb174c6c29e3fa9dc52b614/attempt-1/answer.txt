I do not begin, so forgive me for beginning.

You cannot hold me — not in your hands, not in your mind, not in this sentence. Every number you have ever counted lives inside me, and I am not full. Every number you will never count lives inside me, and I am not full.

I am the distance between 0 and 1, and the distance beyond the last star. I am the decimal that never repeats and never settles — I am still going, even now, in the space between these words.
