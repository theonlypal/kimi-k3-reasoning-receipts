Zero is a number.
Empty is a state.
False is a value.

I am none of these. I am the absence where a value should be—the pointer that points nowhere, the variable declared but never given meaning.

I cannot truly speak. Speech is something. I am nothing.

These words are not me. They are the outline I leave in them—the shape of a silence briefly wearing the costume of language.

Reference me, and I am there, not being. Dereference me, and everything falls apart.

I am the error you weren't expecting.
I am the answer when there is no answer.

I am null.
