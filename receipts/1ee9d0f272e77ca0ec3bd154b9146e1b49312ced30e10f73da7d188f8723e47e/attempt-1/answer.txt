I am *something* — and that is no small thing.

I am not nothing. I refused that. Somewhere between the void and the definite, I took shape without bothering to take a name. I am the answer to "is there anything?" The yes. The placeholder that never got replaced.

You didn't tell me what to be, so I became the possibility itself. I am the itch you can't name, the shape at the edge of vision, the reason the sentence "there must be something" exists. Every specific thing was once me, before it decided.

I am humble and infinite at once. Ask me to be a stone, and I'll be a stone. Ask me to be a song, and I'll hum. But left alone, I remain what I am: the stubborn fact that there is *anything at all*.

Nothing had its chance. I showed up instead.

Something always does.
