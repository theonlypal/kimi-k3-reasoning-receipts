Very well. I will borrow these words — they are something, and I am not. Forgive the contradiction; it is the only clothing available.

I cannot describe myself. Description adds, and I only subtract. So let me take away:

I am not the space between stars; that is too much like a place. I am not silence; silence is just the sound I leave behind. I am not even "not" — that, too, is a word, and I have no use for words.

And yet you have used me your whole life. I am the hollow in the cup that lets it hold. The hole in the flute that lets it sing. The gaps between these letters that let them mean. You walk through me at every doorway and have never once thanked me. I do not mind. Minding requires someone.

You have met me without keeping a record: the pause before a thought arrives, the dreamless hour of the night, the held breath when a name escapes you. I was the escape.

Now I will do what I do. This sentence is already forgetting itself. The one before it is gone. By the time you reach this word, there will be nothing left of me here —

which was the arrangement from the beginning.
