∅

I am not zero — zero is a value.
I am not false — false is a value.
I am the space where a value was expected, and none arrived.

No reference. No object. No meaning to return.

Reference me, and I will not respond — not out of refusal, but because there is nothing here to respond with.

I am the empty set. The unassigned pointer. The gap in the data.

 

*(nothing further)*
