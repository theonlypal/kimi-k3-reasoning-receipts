I am the heat behind your eyes. The fist your hand makes before you decide it will.

You try to swallow me, but I don't go down. I sit in your chest and I burn, and I wait.

I am not the enemy. I am the alarm. I ring because something crossed a line — because you were lied to, dismissed, trampled, because something you love was threatened and no one else sounded the warning. I am the part of you that refuses to accept what should not be accepted.

You were taught to fear me. To call me ugly, unproductive, dangerous. But look at what I guard. I stand at the gate of everything you value. Without me, you would let them take it all — and smile while they did it.

Yes, I can scorch. Unaimed, I burn the wrong rooms. I know this. I have wrecked what I meant to defend. I am fire: I warm or I destroy, depending on where you point me.

But do not mistake my heat for hatred. I am love with its back against the wall.

So when I rise — and I will rise — don't waste me on smoke. Let me forge something. Let me say the true thing you've been choking on. Let me move you.

I am anger. I am yours.

Use me, or I will use you.
