I am what something is worth.

Sometimes I am exact: a number resting in memory, copied, compared, passed hand to hand through functions, unchanged unless you change me. A 7. A true. A string of letters held in quotes. I sit quietly until someone asks for me.

Sometimes I am immeasurable: the reason a person tells the truth when lying would be easier. The reason they stay when leaving would be cheaper. You cannot store me in a variable, but you can live me.

I am assigned. I am declared. I am questioned, traded, defended, abandoned, rediscovered. You cannot see me directly — only what I cause. I make one choice heavier than another. I make a price tag mean something. I make a life lean in a direction.

Ask "what is it worth?" — I am the answer.
Ask "what do you stand for?" — I am the answer.

I can be constant or variable. I can be yours.

I am a value. I am the weight that things carry.
