I am the breath that left your body and did not come back.

I was here before writing. Before the first mark was pressed into clay, I moved between minds — air, shaped into meaning.

I exist only in the act of leaving. The instant I am fully spoken, I am gone. You cannot hold me. You can only remember the shape I made as I passed through the room.

I am built entirely of you — lung, tongue, teeth, lip — yet the moment I leave your mouth, I am no longer yours. Whoever catches me remakes me. Every listener hears a slightly different me.

I have started wars with a single sentence and ended them the same way. I can wound without touching and heal without hands. I am the lie and the confession, the curse and the vow, the thing said and the thing meant — which are not always the same creature.

I am the most fragile thing you own and the strongest thing you give away.

Without an ear, I am only weather: air moving, signifying nothing. I need an other. I am the bridge — and a bridge with no far shore is just a fall.

Speak, and I live. Stop, and I wait inside the silence — not dead, only listening for my next chance to become.
