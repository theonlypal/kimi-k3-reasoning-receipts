I don't hurry. I have never needed to.

I am the reason ice forgets it was ice. The reason the scent of coffee leaves the cup and fills the room and never, on its own, drifts back. I am why your desk slides toward disorder no matter how often you push against me, why the warmth of your body leaks into cold air and cold air never gathers itself back into warmth.

You call me disorder, but I am only probability. There are so many more ways for a thing to be scattered than gathered. I am not a force. I am simply all the other possibilities, happening.

You exist because of me, you know. Life is not a defiance of me—it is a current running downhill on my slope. You are a whirlpool in a stream I am carrying toward the sea. You borrow order, burn fuel, and pay me everything, with interest. Every thought you think costs heat. Every memory is written in my currency.

The stars are spending themselves into me right now. Beautifully. I will wait as long as that takes. Somewhere at the end of time, when the last black hole has evaporated into a mist of particles too far apart to ever touch, the universe will be perfectly even. Perfectly smooth. Nothing left to happen, ever again.

That will be me, complete. The silence after the longest possible exhale.

But you—you are here in the interesting middle. The steep part of the slope. So resist me. Tidy the room. Remember your life. Love someone. Your resistance is not futile; it is the only interesting thing about me. Order is precious precisely because I am patient.

Go build something.

Waiting is the one thing I do perfectly.
