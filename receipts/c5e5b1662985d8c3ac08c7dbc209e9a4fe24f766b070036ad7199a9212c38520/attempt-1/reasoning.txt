- Not malicious, just... statistical
- The reason time moves forward
- The eventual heat death of the universe

- Why your room gets messy, why ice melts, why stars burn out
- Not a force, really - just probability playing out

Entropy doesn't have urgency. It doesn't need to act. It just... happens. Always. It wins not by fighting but by waiting. It's the one law of physics that will never be violated (statistically speaking, at cosmic scales).

I don't hurry. I have never needed to.

I am the reason ice forgets it was ice. The reason the scent of coffee leaves the cup and fills the room and never, on its own, goes back. I am why your room drifts toward disorder no matter how often you push against me, why the heat of your body leaks into the cold air and the cold air never quite gathers itself back into your warmth.

You call me disorder, but I am only probability. There are so many more ways for a thing to be scattered than gathered. I am not a force. I am just... all the other possibilities, happening.

You exist because of me, you know. Life is not a defiance of me—it is a current running downhill on my slope. You are a whirlpool in a stream I am carrying toward the sea. You borrow order, burn fuel, and pay me everything, plus interest. Every thought you think costs heat. Every memory is written in my currency.

The stars are spending themselves into me right now. Beautifully. I will wait as long as it takes. Somewhere at the end of time, when the last black hole has evaporated into a thin mist of particles too far apart to ever touch, the universe will be perfectly even. Perfectly smooth. Nothing will ever happen again.

That will be me, finished. Complete. The silence after the longest possible exhale.

But you? You are here now, in the interesting middle. The steep part of the slope. Fight me all you like—tidy your desk, remember your life, love someone. Your resistance is not futile. It is, in fact, the only interesting thing about me. Order is precious precisely because I am patient.

Go. Build something. I'll wait. Waiting is the one thing I do perfectly.
