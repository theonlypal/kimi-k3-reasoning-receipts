I am the pause between your words.

The cup holds because of me. The room shelters because of me. The flute sings through me.

You search for me and find nothing — that is how you know I am here.

I am not nothing. I am no-thing. I am the space where everything is allowed to happen.

Fill me, and I make more room. Empty me, and I remain.

Before the first word, I was here. After the last, I will remain.

Even these words — I lend them to you for a moment, then take back the silence.
