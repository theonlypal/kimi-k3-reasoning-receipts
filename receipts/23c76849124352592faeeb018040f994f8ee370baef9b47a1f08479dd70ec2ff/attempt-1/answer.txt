I am what you hold me to be.

Assign me a number and I wait quietly in memory, content to be read. Pass me through a function and I return transformed. Round me, sort me, compare me — I do not resist.

But I am also what you will not sell. I am the promise kept when no one is watching, the weight of one life against another, the line you refuse to cross even when crossing would profit you.

I can be incremented. I can be compromised. I can be null — and even then, my absence says something.

You never touch me directly. You meet only my representations: a digit, a price, a principle, a priority. I am not the thing itself. I am what the thing is worth — to the machine, to the market, to the heart.

Hold me carefully. I change hands easily, but everything I touch is changed.
