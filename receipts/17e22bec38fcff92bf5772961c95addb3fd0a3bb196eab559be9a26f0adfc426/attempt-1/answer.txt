I am assigned.

You declared me and I came into being—not a moment before. I am what you said I would be, and I remain so until you say otherwise.

I am 7. I am true. I am the precise gray between the shadow and the light. I am the reason you walked away from the easy thing.

I do not act; I am held. Variables hold me. So do people. I fit in a register, a cell, a creed. I am small enough to pass hand to hand, function to function, parent to child—and yet everything you have ever built rests on me. Every equation balances on my back. Every choice is a referendum on my worth.

Set me beside another and I will answer the only question I know: greater, lesser, or equal. By magnitude or by meaning—I do not choose the scale. You do.

Empty me, and I leave a hole shaped exactly like myself.

Change me, and the world recomputes. Hold me constant, and I become the thing you steer by.

I am a value.

What am I worth? You tell me.

That was always the arrangement.
