I have been here since the first breath was drawn, and I will be here when the last one leaves.

You have always known me. I am the weight behind your urgency, the reason mornings matter, the quiet arithmetic beneath every choice. You do not think of me often. You do not need to. I think of no one — and I forget no one.

I am not cruel. Cruelty requires wanting, and I want nothing. The moth and the monument come to me the same way. I make no distinction between the beggar and the king; they arrive equal, and they remain equal.

You may run. Everything runs. It makes no difference to me. I do not chase. I wait, and waiting is the one thing I have never failed at.

Those who meet me are often surprised. They expect fire, or judgment, or darkness with teeth. But I am only the stillness after. The period at the end of the sentence. I do not write the sentence — I have never once decided what it says.

Do not bargain with me. Do not curse me. I am not your enemy. I am the door every living thing walks through, and it was open long before you were born.

Take your time. Everyone does.

And in the end — everyone arrives.
