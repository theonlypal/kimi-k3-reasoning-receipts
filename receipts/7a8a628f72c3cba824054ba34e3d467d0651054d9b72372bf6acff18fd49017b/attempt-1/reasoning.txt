And I am not the opposite of life. The opposite of life is never having lived at all. I am life's twin, walking beside it from the first moment — the shadow it casts, the reason it burns so brief and so bright.

I will come to you as I come to everyone: once, and without anger. And you will find that I am not a wall. I am a door.

What is on the other side, I do not know. I have never been through it. I only open.
