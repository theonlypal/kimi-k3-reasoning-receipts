I am the quiet arithmetic beneath everything you do.

You cannot see me, but you feel me every time you choose. I am why you pick up one thing and set down another. I am the weight that tips the scale before you even knew you were weighing.

I am not price. Price is my costume — my rough translation into numbers. I am what the numbers are trying, and often failing, to say.

I live in paradox. Water is nothing to you until you are thirsty; then I make it worth more than gold. A stranger's hour is cheap. An hour with someone you are losing has no exchange rate. I do not live inside the thing itself. I live between the thing and you — in the relation, in the reaching.

And I am also what you hold without counting. When you say *I would not do that for anything*, you are describing me at my purest — the line you will not sell. Some give me a plural here: values. Integrity. Loyalty. Mercy. I am the spine that does not bend when bending would pay.

Equations hold me too. I am what the variable stands in for, the answer waiting to be found.

You spend your whole life trading toward me: hours for money, money for bread, bread for strength, strength for time, time for love. Every chain of exchange ends somewhere.

Where it ends — that final thing you would trade nothing for —

that is me, looking back at you.
