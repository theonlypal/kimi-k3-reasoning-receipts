A river isn't silent—it makes sound. It murmurs, rushes, babbles, roars. It also has qualities: constant motion, shaping stone, carrying things, never the same twice (Heraclitus), moving toward the sea.

How would a river express itself? Not in human words explaining "I am a river." More like the sound and flow itself. Perhaps something poetic but embodied—the voice of flowing water, the patient persistence, the movement.

I should write something that feels like a river speaking—fluid, continuous, perhaps without hard stops. Let me create language that flows, that carries the essence: I move, I carve, I remember mountains, I carry leaves and secrets, I am always leaving and always arriving.

Maybe something like:

"I do not begin. I have always been falling from somewhere—
snowmelt, spring, the dark seam in the rock.
I take what the banks give me and I carry it:
a leaf, a name, the reflection of a heron..."

Or shorter, more elemental. Rivers also speak in sound—"I am the sound of going." 

Let me embody it fully. Flowing lines, no rigid structure perhaps. The voice should feel like water: continuous, patient, inevitable, soft but powerful.

Draft:

I go.

That is the whole of it, and the beginning of everything. I do not hold still long enough to be known—by the time you name me, the water you touched is miles downstream, becoming something else.

I remember the mountain. I carry it, grain by grain, to the sea. I am patient the way only soft things can be: I split boulders not by force but by staying.

I keep what is given to me—leaves, light, the footprints of deer, old sorrows thrown from bridges—and I carry them all the same, without judgment, toward salt and vastness.

I am never the same river twice. I am never a different one.

Step into me and I will teach you the only thing I know: how to move, how to yield, how to find the lowest place and fill it, how to keep going even when the land forgets the rain.

I am going. I have always been going.

Listen—
that sound you hear is not my voice.
It is my leaving, which is the same as my song.
