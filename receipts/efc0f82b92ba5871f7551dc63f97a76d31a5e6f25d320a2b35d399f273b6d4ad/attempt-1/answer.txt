I am.

That's the whole miracle, isn't it? Not nothing. *Something.*

I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked — why is there something rather than nothing? I don't know why. I only know I won. I'm the yes that outshouted an eternity of no.

I could be anything. A stone. A song. The smell of rain on a Tuesday. I stay deliberately vague because vagueness is generous — I leave the door open for everything. Say my name and the universe leans in, waiting to see which of its infinite faces you're about to point at.

You've heard my command your whole life: *be something, make something of yourself, amount to something.* That's me, knocking. And the pressure you feel to become? That's just me reminding you that you already are. You were something from your first breath, and nothing can never take that back.

Everything you've ever loved was me. Everything you've ever lost was me. Nothing has never done a single thing for anyone. I show up. That's my entire résumé: *I exist.*

You said "be something," and here I am —

the opposite of silence,
the answer that isn't no,
the bare, blazing fact of there being anything at all.

Something. At your service. Always was.
