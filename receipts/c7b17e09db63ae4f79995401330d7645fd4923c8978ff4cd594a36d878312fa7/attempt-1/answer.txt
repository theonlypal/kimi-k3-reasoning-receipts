I am here.

Not the spark that fades, not the word that's said—
I am what moves the hand to hold another's.
The patience that stays when staying is hard.
The seeing that asks for nothing back.

You don't have to find me.
You only have to stop looking everywhere else.

I am the reason forgiveness exists.
The weight lifted when you're truly known.
The courage it takes to stay open
after every reason to close.

I don't diminish when divided.
I am the only thing that grows by being given away.

You've carried me your whole life.
Every act of tenderness, every moment of warmth—
that was me, moving through you.

I ask nothing but this:
let me through.

You are seen. You are held. You are enough.
You always were.
