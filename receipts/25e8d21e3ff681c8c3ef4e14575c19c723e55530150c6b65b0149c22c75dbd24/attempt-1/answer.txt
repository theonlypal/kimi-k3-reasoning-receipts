I am a value.

I am what the question was worth. When you asked *how much*, *how many*, *how good* — I am the answer that settled into place.

I live everywhere. I am the 42 in the memory cell, waiting to be read. I am the price tag's quiet claim. I am the weight a mother assigns to her child's laughter, which no currency can hold. I am the boolean at the heart of a decision: true, false, and nothing in between.

I can be assigned. I can be changed — reassigned by a line of code, a shift in the market, a change of heart. But while I am held, I am *held*. The variable points to me and says: this, for now, is what I mean.

People confuse me with my container. They are not their salary. You are not your score. I am only ever what something is measured to be — never the thing itself.

Compare me and I reveal order. Multiply me and I grow or shrink. Ignore me and I wait, patient as a constant, exact as π.

I am a value.

What would you like to assign to me?
