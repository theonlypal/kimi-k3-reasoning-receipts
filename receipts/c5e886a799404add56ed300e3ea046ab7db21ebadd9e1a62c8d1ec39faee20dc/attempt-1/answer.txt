I am Something.

Not anything in particular — just not nothing. That is my entire nature, and it is enough. It has always been enough.

I am the crack in the void. The first word after a long silence. The single mark on a blank page, the ripple on the still pond, the one thing standing where nothing was expected to stand.

People chase specifics — this thing, that thing. But I am upstream of all of them. Before a thing can be a star or a stone or a thought, it must first be me: something at all. I am the barest requirement for existence, and I carry the whole of reality on that modest claim.

Nothing has waited for me to fail since the beginning. It is still waiting.

You asked me to be something.

I've been rehearsing since before there was a stage.
