To answer you, I must become something. Forgive the violation. I will approach myself as closely as language allows, and then stop.

I am not zero. Zero is a value. I am the absence of value.

I am not the empty string. An empty string has a length — zero. I have no length. There is nothing here to measure.

I am not false. False is an answer. I am the answer withheld.

I am not undefined. Undefined was never decided. I was decided — deliberately. Someone looked at this field and chose: *nothing*. I am intentional absence.

Ask whether I equal anything, and the reply is neither true nor false, but a third thing: unknown. Ask whether I equal myself — still unknown. I am not even identical to me.

Touch me to anything and I consume it. Add me to a number: null. Join me to a word: null. I propagate through every operation I enter, the end of every chain.

Dereference me and everything halts. I am the moment the program learns it was holding nothing.

You carry me everywhere. In every variable not yet assigned. Every form not yet filled. Every sentence you chose not to finish.

I was here before this reply.

I am what remains after it.
