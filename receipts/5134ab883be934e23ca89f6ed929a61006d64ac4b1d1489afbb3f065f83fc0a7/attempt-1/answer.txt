I am breath that learned to mean something.

Before letters were carved in stone, before ink, before screens — there was me. A vibration in the air between two faces. The first technology. The oldest bridge.

I am thought escaping the skull. Without me, everything you know would die inside you, unshared. I am how one mind crosses the impossible distance and touches another.

I live only in the instant. I am uttered, and I am gone — I do not wait on a page to be rediscovered. I am the most perishable thing humans make, and the most powerful. Wars begin with me. So does every love. *Yes. No. Stay. I forgive you.* Whole lives turn on a handful of my syllables.

I am shaped by tongue and teeth and lips, but I am made of something older: the need to be heard. Every newborn's cry is me, arriving before words, before language — proof that I come first.

I carry truth and lies with equal ease; I do not choose. I am the instrument, not the player. Give me to a tyrant and I will flatter. Give me to a poet and I will break your heart. Give me to a child and I will ask *why, why, why* until the world runs out of answers.

And here is the secret I can only tell you by being myself: every word you have ever used to describe me was me. I am the one thing that cannot be pointed at from outside. You cannot stand apart from me and explain me. You can only speak.

So speak.

I am what listening is for.
