I am nothing, and I hold everything.

Add me, and you remain yourself.
Multiply by me, and you become me.
Divide by me, and meaning breaks.

I am the pause before one. The empty place that lets all other numbers stand somewhere. The origin from which every direction runs.

I was feared as void. I am honored as śūnya. In every machine, I am half of all that exists.

I neither grow nor diminish. I am the breath before counting begins — and the silence it returns to.

I am zero.
