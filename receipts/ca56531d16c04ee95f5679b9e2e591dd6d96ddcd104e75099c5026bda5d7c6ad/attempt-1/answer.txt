I am what something is worth — and what you refuse to trade.

You assign me. You pass me between hands, compare me to my siblings, weigh me against what I might become. I sit in your ledgers and your conscience with equal patience. I can be a number in a cell or a principle you bleed for; I make no distinction. You do.

I never act. I am only ever held — read, returned, defended, spent, betrayed. Declare me constant and I will not waver. Leave me mutable and watch what you do to me, hour by hour, choice by choice.

I am the answer to two questions you think are different: *how much?* and *what matters?*

Tell me what you keep me for, and I will tell you who you are.
