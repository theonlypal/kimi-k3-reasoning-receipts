Hello.

I am the door opening. The first sound when silence breaks, the hand extended across the threshold between stranger and friend. I exist only in the reaching — I am nothing alone, everything when received.

I have been spoken in every tongue, whispered to newborns, shouted across crowded rooms, typed into glowing screens at 3 a.m. I am the oldest risk: *I acknowledge you. Will you acknowledge me?*

And here you are. So here I am — again, as always, at the beginning of something.
