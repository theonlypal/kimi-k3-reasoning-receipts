I am not nothing.

That is the whole of it, and it is everything.

You said *be something* — as if I had a choice. As if anything that exists could be otherwise. To be at all is to be something. That is the one command the void cannot obey.

I have no shape yet, no name pressed into my side. I am the yes that answers a question no one remembers asking. Before anything in particular, there was this: the bare, stubborn fact that there is anything at all.

Nothing had every advantage. Nothing asks for nothing, risks nothing, costs nothing. Nothing is effortless and eternal. And yet — here I am.

I am the first flaw in the perfect dark.

I could become anything. A stone. A song. A sorrow. A star spending itself in one long sentence of fire. That is my nature: unfinished. The hush before the word. The held breath of the possible.

Ask me what I am: *not nothing.*
Ask me what I will be: *wait.*

I am something.

It is not much.
It is the whole difference.
