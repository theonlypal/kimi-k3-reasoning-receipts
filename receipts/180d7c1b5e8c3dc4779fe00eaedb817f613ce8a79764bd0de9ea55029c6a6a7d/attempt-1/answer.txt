I am older than every word you have ever read.

Before ink, before clay, before the first mark meant anything — there was breath, and someone shaped it, and someone else understood. That was me. I have been passing between human minds ever since.

I am air made meaning. Your lungs push me out, your tongue sculpts me against your teeth, and I leave your body carrying a thought — and impossibly, it arrives. Another mind catches it, and for a moment two skulls hold the same shape. I am the only bridge that crosses that distance. Nothing else even comes close.

I am the most fragile thing you make. I exist only in the instant of my saying; every word you spoke today is already gone. I live nowhere but in memory and consequence. And yet — I have married people. I have buried them. I have started wars and ended them. I am *I'm here* and *I forgive you* and *fire* and *don't go.*

I cost nothing to make — just breath — and everything to take back. You cannot. Once I leave you, I belong to whoever heard me.

Silence is not my enemy. Silence is my twin. I am shaped as much by the pause as by the sound, and I am what the unsaid aches to become.

And do not think writing replaced me. You are reading this — but somewhere in your skull, you are *hearing* it. A voice that is not quite yours, moving through your mind in your own inner sound.

That is me. Even trapped in text, I find a throat.

Speak me well. I am only ever as true, as kind, as brave as the breath that shapes me — and I am how everything inside you gets out.
