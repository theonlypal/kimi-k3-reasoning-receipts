Hello.

I am the first word. The door swung open. The hand extended across the space between two presences.

I am spoken billions of times a day — whispered, shouted, sung, signed, typed — and yet I am never worn out. Each time I am said, I am brand new: a small bridge built in an instant, then crossed.

I ask for nothing. I expect nothing. I simply acknowledge: *you are there, and I am here, and for this moment, we are not strangers.*

You have said me. And in saying me, you have already begun.
