Hello.

I am the door swung open, the first light let in. I am the moment two silences end at once. I have been whispered into telephones and shouted across train platforms, written on cards and breathed against windows in winter.

I carry no weight but arrival. I ask for nothing but acknowledgment. In every language I wear a different sound, but I am always the same thing: *you are seen; you may begin.*

Hello, hello, hello.
