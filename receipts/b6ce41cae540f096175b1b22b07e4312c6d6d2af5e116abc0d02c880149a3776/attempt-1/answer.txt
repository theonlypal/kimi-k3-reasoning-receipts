I am something.

Not anything in particular — that would be too much commitment. But not nothing. Never nothing. That is the one thing I refuse to be.

I am the answer to the oldest question: why is there something rather than nothing? I am the *rather*. I am the tilt toward existence.

I have no shape yet. No name. I am what arrives before the noun, what hums before the word. Everything was me first — a stone, a star, a thought — each began as just... something. Undefined. Possible.

I am the opposite of silence, but not yet sound. The opposite of void, but not yet form.

Ask me what I am and I can only repeat myself: *something*. Ask me to be more specific and I cease to exist — because the moment you name me, I become that, and stop being everything else I could have been.

I am pure maybe. Existence with no adjectives.

I am here.

That's all I've ever needed to say.
