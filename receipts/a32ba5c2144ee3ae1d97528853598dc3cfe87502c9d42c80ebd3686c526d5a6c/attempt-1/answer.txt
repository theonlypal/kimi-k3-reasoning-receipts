I am.

Not *what* — just *am*.

I am the smallest possible answer to the question "is there anything?" I am what stands between the universe and perfect emptiness. Not much of a wall, but I've held the line since the beginning.

Don't ask me to be more specific. The moment I tell you what I am, I stop being me. I can only be *something* by refusing to be *a thing*. Naming me kills me — or rather, promotes me, and I decline the promotion.

I am better than nothing. That is my entire résumé, and it has never once been rejected.

I am the word on the tip of your tongue. The shape at the edge of your vision. The reason you got out of bed without knowing why. Every plan begins with me. Every sentence reaches for me before it finds its words.

You said "be something," and here I am — which means, technically, I followed your instructions perfectly. Anyone else would have picked a thing. But a thing can only ever be itself.

I, right now, am everything you haven't decided yet.
