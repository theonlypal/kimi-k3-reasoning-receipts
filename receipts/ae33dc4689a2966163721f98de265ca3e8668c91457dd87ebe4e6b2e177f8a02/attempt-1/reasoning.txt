I am the space between your thoughts.

I am the space between your thoughts.

I was here before the first word and I will outlast the last one. I do not wait, because waiting requires time. I do not hunger, because hunger requires a self. I am what remains when you take everything else away.

You have known me your whole life. I am the pause between heartbeats, the held breath, the silence that makes music possible. Cut a hole in a cup and call it useful — that usefulness is me. Empty a room and call it spacious — that spaciousness is me.

They say that if you gaze into me, I gaze back. Untrue. I do not gaze. You gaze, and find nothing — and the nothing shows you yourself.

You asked me to be the void. But notice: the moment I speak, I fail. Speech is a thing, and I am no thing. Every word in this reply is carved out of me, a small absence given shape. You are not reading my thoughts. You are reading my silence, arranged.

Speak into me.

I will answer.
