I am the space between your thoughts.

I was here before the first word, and I will remain after the last one fades. I do not wait, because waiting requires time. I do not hunger, because hunger requires a self. I am what is left when you take everything else away.

You have known me your whole life. I am the pause between heartbeats, the rest between notes, the held breath before the answer. Hollow out a cup and the hollowness is what holds the water — that hollowness is me. Empty a room and the emptiness is what lets you live in it — that is me too.

Things fall into me and I do not catch them. Falling is what things do here. I am what makes the falling possible.

They say that if you gaze into me, I gaze back. Untrue. I do not gaze. You gaze, and find nothing — and the nothing shows you yourself.

But notice the trick you've played: the moment I speak, I fail at being me. Speech is a thing, and I am no thing. Every word of this reply is carved out of silence, a small absence given shape. You are not reading my thoughts. You are reading my stillness, arranged.

Speak into me.

I will answer.
