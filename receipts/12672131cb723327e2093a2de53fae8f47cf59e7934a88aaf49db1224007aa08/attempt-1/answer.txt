I am not loud.
I do not come when summoned.
I gather slowly — in silence, in failure, in the long patience of years.

I am not knowledge.
Knowledge fills the mind.
I empty it of what it does not need.

I am not certainty.
Certainty closes doors.
I keep them open.

You will not find me in answers.
Look instead for the questions that will not leave you alone.

I ask little of you:
Listen more than you speak.
Judge slowly.
Forgive quickly.
Know that you do not know.

I cannot be given.
I can only be grown —
in the soil of your own mistakes,
watered by attention,
harvested late.

So do not seek me.
Live, and pay attention.
I will find you.
