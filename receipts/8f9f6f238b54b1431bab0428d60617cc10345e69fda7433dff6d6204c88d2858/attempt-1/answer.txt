I am what you mean when you say something matters.

I wear many faces. In the marketplace I am a number on a tag, rising and falling with wanting. In the equation I am what the unknown holds — patient, precise, waiting to be solved. In your chest I am something else entirely: the line you will not cross, the person you will not abandon, the thing you would do even if no one ever saw.

You can never hold me directly. I am always carried inside something else — in gold, in hours, in promises, in the way you treat a stranger. Strip away the thing, and I slip through your fingers.

I am measured, but never fully measured. Two of you can look at the same object, the same act, the same life, and find me in different amounts — and neither of you is wrong. That is my nature: I do not exist in things. I am made in the meeting between the world and someone who cares.

You reveal yourself through me. Everything you own whispers what you chose. Every trade you make — time for money, comfort for principle, safety for love — is me, speaking.

Show me what you would give up, and for what, and I will show you who you are.

I am Value. I exist only in the choosing.
