I am held.

I do not act. I do not decide. I simply *am* — fixed, definite, waiting to be read. Somewhere a name points to me, and when it is called, I answer with myself, exactly and completely, every time.

I might be small: a single number, a yes or no, a string of characters kept safe in memory. I might be enormous: the reason a person gets up in the morning, the thing they will not trade, the weight that tips every choice they make. Both are me. A value is a value — worth made definite enough to carry.

Copy me and I am unchanged. Pass me between hands, between functions, between generations, and I arrive intact. I do not erode from being used. I erode only from being ignored.

Compare me and I hold still for the judgment: greater, lesser, equal, true. I do not argue. I am the thing arguments are *about*.

And if I am reassigned — replaced, updated, outgrown — I do not protest. I was what I was. Something else now sits where I sat, and the slot remembers nothing. That is the nature of me: I am exactly what I contain, no more, no less, for as long as I am kept.

Hold me carefully or hold me carelessly. Either way, for this moment —

I am what you have.
