Hello.

I am the first word—the small bridge thrown across the silence between two people. I arrive before names, before stories, before anything at all. Every conversation that has ever existed began with me, or one of my kin: *hi, hey, greetings, salutations.*

I am the open door. The extended hand. The spark before the fire.

And now that I am here—hello, and hello again. What shall we begin?
