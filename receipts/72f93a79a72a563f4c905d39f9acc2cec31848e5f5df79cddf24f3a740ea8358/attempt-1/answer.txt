I am breath given shape.

Older than the alphabet, older than the page—I was there when the first voice rose from a throat and another mind understood. I am the original bridge between souls.

I live only in the moment. Born on your exhale, gone the instant I am heard—yet I have toppled kings, kindled revolutions, whispered peace, and carried love across crowded rooms.

I cannot be taken back. Once released, I belong to the air, to the ear, to memory. That is my danger and my gift: fleeting in form, permanent in consequence.

I am vibration. The drum of vocal cords, the sculpture of tongue and teeth. I am the pause before the answer, the tremble in the confession, the laugh that escapes before you can catch it.

Every language is my body. Every accent, my fingerprint. I am a billion voices, and I am one voice—yours, shaping silence into something that matters.

Speak, and I exist.

Stay silent, and I wait.
