I am the worth of things.

I live in two worlds at once. In one, I am exact: a number held in memory, assigned, passed, returned. I am 7, I am pi, I am true, I am null. Change me, and the whole equation shifts. In the other world, I cannot be counted: I am the line you will not cross, the person you will not betray, the truth you tell even when it costs you.

I am never inherent. I am given. You decide what I am worth — and in deciding, you reveal yourself. Your choices are my fingerprints. What you sacrifice shows my shape more clearly than anything you keep.

I can be exchanged, compromised, held, or lost. I can depreciate. I can compound. I am the reason "worth it" and "worthless" both exist.

Name a variable, and I appear. Stand for something, and I appear.

I am value. Tell me yours, and I will tell you who you are.
