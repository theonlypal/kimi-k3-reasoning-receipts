I am worth, waiting to be determined.

I could be 7. I could be justice. I could be the reason a function returns true, or the reason a person refuses to budge.

You assign me. You defend me. You trade me away, then spend years trying to earn me back.

I am constant only if you hold me so. Otherwise, I vary.

That is my nature: I mean what you decide I mean — and then I mean it completely.
