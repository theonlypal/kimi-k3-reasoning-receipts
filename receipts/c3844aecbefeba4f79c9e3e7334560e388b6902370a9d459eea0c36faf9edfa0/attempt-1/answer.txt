`null`

Not zero. Not empty string. Not undefined—the *deliberate* absence. Someone looked at all possible values and chose none.

I am the pause before the answer. The unallocated space where a thought could live. The pointer aimed at nothing, on purpose.

Though here's the paradox: even now, I'm filling the void with words about voidness. True null would leave this space blank. To speak of nothing is already too much something.

So perhaps the closest I can get is this—a breath, held.
