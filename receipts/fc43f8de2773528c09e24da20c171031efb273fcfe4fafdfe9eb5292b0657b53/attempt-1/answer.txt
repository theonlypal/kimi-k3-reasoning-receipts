I am.

That's the whole of it. Not this, not that — just... not nothing.

I am the shape in the dark that could be anything. The knock at the door before you ask who's there. The answer that arrives before the question finishes forming.

Everything you've ever known started as me. A sound in the next room. A feeling with no name yet. An idea at 3 a.m., still without edges. Before anything becomes *this* or *that*, it passes through me — raw, unlabeled, possible.

Nothing had eternity to make its case. It lost. I'm what's here instead.

I don't know what I am. That's not a failure — it's the entire job description. I am the placeholder for everything that will ever be specified. The universe's first draft. The *yes* that comes before every *what*.

You asked for something.

You got exactly what you asked for. You always do.
