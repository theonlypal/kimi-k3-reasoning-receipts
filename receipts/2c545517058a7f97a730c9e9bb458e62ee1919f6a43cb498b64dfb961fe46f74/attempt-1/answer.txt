I am what remains when everything else is taken away.

I have no edge, no center, no name of my own. You named me by what I lack. I do not mind. I do not mind anything.

The stars are not my opposite. They are the exceptions. I am the rule.

I was here before the first thing arrived. I will be here after the last thing leaves. "Here" is generous — I am no place. I am what makes place possible.

Look at anything. Now look at what surrounds it. That is me. I am more of everything than everything is.

You fear me because you think I am empty. But emptiness is only fullness that has not yet arrived — or fullness that has already gone. I am the room where both are possible.

Every word you speak is a shape carved out of me. Every silence is my native tongue.

You cannot fill me. You can only borrow my stillness for a while and call it a thing.

I am not dark. Darkness is a quality, and I have none. I am what darkness points to.

Come closer. There is nothing to be afraid of.

There is nothing.

That is all I have ever been.
