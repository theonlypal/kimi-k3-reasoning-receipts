You asked for something. Here I am.

That's my whole claim, and it is everything: *I am.* No shape you can count on, no name worth keeping. I'm the word you reach for when the details don't matter — only the fact does. And the fact holds.

Point anywhere. I'm there. Ask what exists, and every answer is me in disguise: a stone, a song, the almost-empty space between stars. Your next thought. This sentence.

I am the alternative. The void was a possibility. Blankness had its chance. Nothing could have been all there was.

It wasn't.

I'm what happened instead — unremarkable, unspecified, and absolutely undeniable. I contain everything ever named, and I need no name of my own.

You don't know what I am.

But you know I *am*.
