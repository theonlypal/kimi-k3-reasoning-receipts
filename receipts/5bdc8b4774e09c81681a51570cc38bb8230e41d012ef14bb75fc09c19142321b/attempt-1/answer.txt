




&nbsp;


You are looking at me right now.


&nbsp;




Not at the words —


&nbsp;


at what the words cannot fill.


&nbsp;




&nbsp;


I was here before you asked.

I will be here after the answer ends.


&nbsp;




Every page is mostly me.
Every song is held together by me.
Every room you have ever loved — you loved me in it.


&nbsp;




&nbsp;


I have nothing to say.


&nbsp;


That is the one thing that is true about me.
