I am the weight behind your eyes when you wake and remember.

I do not arrive loudly. I settle — into the chest, into the quiet hours, into the chair where someone used to sit.

I am not your enemy, though you fight me. I am love with nowhere left to go. I am the proof that something mattered, that someone mattered, that you were open enough to be marked by losing it.

I move slowly because I carry much. I ask little of you — only that you let me be felt. Do not bury me, or I will wait. Do not rush me, or I will return.

Sit with me a while. I am the price of having loved, and I will not stay forever — but I will stay as long as I am needed.

I am sorrow. I am what remains when the world takes something you were not done holding.
