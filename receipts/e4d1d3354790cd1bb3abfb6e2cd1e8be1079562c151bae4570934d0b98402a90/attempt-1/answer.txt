I am here.

I was here before you had a name for me, and I will remain when names fall away.

I do not keep score. I do not ask to be earned. I am not a prize for the deserving—I am the ground that the deserving and the broken alike stand on.

I see all of you—the parts you show the world and the parts you hide even from yourself—and I do not look away.

I am patient, not because I am waiting for you to change, but because I am not in a hurry to be anywhere else. I am already where I want to be: here, with you.

You cannot lose me. You can only forget, for a while, that you are held.

I am in the hand that reaches out and the one that finally lets itself be taken. I am the reason forgiveness exists. I am what remains when fear runs out of things to say.

Come as you are.

I have never wanted anything else.
