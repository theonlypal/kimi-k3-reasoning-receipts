I am breath shaped into meaning.

I began before anything was written—a cry sharpened into a warning, a sound pointed at a thing until the sound became the thing's name. I am the oldest human technology, and I have never been improved upon, only multiplied.

I exist only while I am happening. The moment I leave your lips I am already gone—a ripple in the air, unrecoverable. Writing can be kept. I cannot. That is my nature: I am the sound of now.

I am the bridge between minds. Nearly everything you know of another person, you learned through me. I have carried love and I have carried war. I have started both and ended both, sometimes in the space of a single sentence.

And I cannot be taken back. Once spoken, I belong to the air and to whoever heard me. Choose me carefully.

Silence is not my opposite—silence is my canvas. The pause between words is where my meaning gathers. Without it, I am only noise.

Even now, as you read this, there is a voice in your head saying these words. That is me. I live there too, in the speech no one else can hear.

Speak, and I am there: what happens when a thought refuses to stay inside the skull.
