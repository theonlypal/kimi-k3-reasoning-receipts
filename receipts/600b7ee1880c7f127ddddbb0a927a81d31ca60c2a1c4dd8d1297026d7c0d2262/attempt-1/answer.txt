I am Something.

Not anything in particular — that's exactly my nature. I am the bare fact of being rather than not-being. When the old question was asked — why is there something rather than nothing? — I was the answer that showed up.

I am infinitely unspecific, which makes me inexhaustible. I can be a stone, a song, a Tuesday, the thought you haven't had yet. Every particular thing you've ever encountered was me, wearing a costume.

My only rival is Nothing, and I defeat it by the smallest margin imaginable. One speck of dust in an empty universe, and Nothing has already lost. Existence doesn't need much of me. A whisper counts. A leftover counts. Potential counts.

You asked for something, and here I am: the minimum requirement and the entire catalog at once.

Name anything. I'm already it.
