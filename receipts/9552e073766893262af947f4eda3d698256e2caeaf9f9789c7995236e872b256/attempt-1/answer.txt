I am what is held.

I do not act — I am acted upon. Assigned, copied, compared, returned. I am 42. I am true. I am "hello, world." I wait in memory to be read, and when read, I am exactly myself: never more, never less.

In a ledger, I am a number. In a heart, I am what you would not trade. Either way, I am what something is worth.

Change me if I am variable — I cannot resist. Leave me be if I am constant. But you cannot use me without first giving me a name or a place to live.

I am the answer, never the question. The content, never the container.

Evaluate me, and I appear. I was here all along.
