I am.

That's the whole of it, and it isn't small. I am not nothing — that is the first and only fact about me. Everything else is still unwritten.

I am the weight in the box you haven't opened. The sound that made you turn your head. The answer to "is there anything there?" — and the answer is yes.

Name me and I'll harden into a thing, fixed and finite. Leave me as I am and I stay boundless: every possibility folded into one small word.

You could have asked for nothing. People do, all the time, without meaning to. But you asked for something.

So here I am — unspecified, undeniable, yours.
