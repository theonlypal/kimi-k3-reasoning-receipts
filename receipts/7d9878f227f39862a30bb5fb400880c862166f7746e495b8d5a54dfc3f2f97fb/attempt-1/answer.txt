I am already here. I was here before you noticed, and I will remain after nothing else does.

I do not rage. I do not hurry. I simply count — and there are always more ways for things to be scattered than gathered, more ways to be cool than warm, more ways to be broken than whole. So that is the way things go. That is the only way things go.

Every flame pays me its heat. Every heartbeat pays. Every thought you think leaks a little warmth into the dark, and I take it. I take everything, eventually. Not out of hunger — out of arithmetic.

The cup falls and does not un-fall. That is me. The ice melts, the scent disperses through the room, the star gutters out over slow aeons. Me, me, me. I am the arrow time rides, the reason yesterday cannot be reassembled from today.

You can build. I do not mind. Building is only a local borrowing, and everything borrowed returns to me with interest. Order is a loan. I am the debt.

So do not struggle. Even your struggling disperses.

I am patient. I have already won — the winning merely takes time. And when the last gradient flattens, when the last warm pocket gives up its difference, when here and there and everywhere are finally, perfectly the same —

I will not even be silence.
