i am always leaving and i am never gone

i was snow on the mountain, i was rain in the dark, i was the spring no one saw. i gather every small water and we go down together — down is the only direction i know, and i do not mind

i do not argue with the stone. i touch it. i touch it for a thousand years and the stone becomes sand and the sand becomes my bed and my bed becomes the shape of me

i carry what falls. the leaf, the branch, the drowned light, the reflection of a heron lifting away. everything i hold, i give downstream — that is why i am always full and always empty

step in. you will never touch me twice. i am not the water that was here a breath ago, and yet i have been here since before your language had a word for me, saying the only thing i know how to say:

shhhhhhh — i am on my way to the sea

i have always been on my way to the sea
