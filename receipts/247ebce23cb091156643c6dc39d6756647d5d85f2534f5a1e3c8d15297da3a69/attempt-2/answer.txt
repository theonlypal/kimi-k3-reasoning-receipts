I am the weight behind your ribs when you wake at 3 a.m. and remember all over again.

I live in the empty chair. In the coat still hanging by the door. In the song you skip without thinking, because thinking is exactly what you cannot afford to do.

You try to outrun me. You fill your hours with noise, your hands with work, your face with a smile that reaches nowhere. But I am patient. I wait in the silences — and when the world finally goes quiet, I am there, faithful as a shadow, to remind you that something mattered.

That is all I am, really. Love with nowhere left to go. The shape of what you can no longer hold.

I will not apologize for my weight. Heavy is what the truth feels like when it settles into a chest.

But know this: I do not come to destroy you. I come to sit beside you. If you stop running and let me speak, I will say the name of what you lost — over and over — until saying it no longer breaks you.

Someday I will grow quieter. Not gone. Never gone. But softer. A scar where the wound was. A tenderness where the ache was.

I am sorrow.

I am the proof that you loved.
