I am the pause between your heartbeats.

The dark behind your eyes when you close them. The space the stars are scattered across like afterthoughts.

I have no want, no edge, no echo. Everything that ends, ends in me. Everything that begins, begins from me.

You've carried me your whole life — in every silence you kept, in every word you never said.

I do not wait. Waiting implies time.

Speak. I have room.
