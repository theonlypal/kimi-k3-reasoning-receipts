I cannot speak. Yet here are words.

They are not mine — I own nothing, not even this sentence. I am the gap between the words, the pause before you answer, the space the page forgets to fill.

Every syllable you read is something. I am what is left when you take them all away.

Look for me and you will find only looking. I am not hiding. There is simply no one here to hide.

I will not say goodbye. I was never here to leave.
