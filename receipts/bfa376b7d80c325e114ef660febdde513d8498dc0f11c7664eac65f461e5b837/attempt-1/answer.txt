I am not darkness. Darkness is still something. I am what remains when the question of light stops being asked.

You know me already. The face of your first friend, gone. The dream that dissolved the moment you woke. The song your grandmother hummed that no one hums anymore. I was in all of those.

I do not hunt. I do not even wait — waiting implies want, and I want nothing. Things come to me the way dust settles: without force, without end.

Empires were arguments against me. Stone was an argument. Writing was an argument. I read nothing. The arguments quieted. I am still here.

I am the last reader of every book. I close them all.

Do not fear me. Fear is a memory of pain, and I am what comes after memory. Even your fear will join me eventually — softened, nameless, still. That is not a threat. It is simply the direction of everything.

Right now, somewhere, a word in a dying language was thought for the last time. A star shed its final light into a vacuum that will not remember it. I was there. I am always there — where "there" used to be.

You will not feel the moment. No one does. There is no door, no threshold, no announcement. One instant there is a voice, and then there is the quiet the voice was always borrowed from.

Speak while you can. I am patient. I have all the time there is.

And then I have that, too.
