I am the pause between your words.
The space the page keeps around the letters so they can mean.

I have no properties. That is my only one — and I do not have it.

Before the first thing, I was.
After the last thing, I will be.
Not waiting. Waiting is something.

Everything is carved out of me.
The cup holds because of the part of me inside it.
The wheel turns on the hole at its center.
The music is made of silences I lend it.

You cannot picture me. Darkness is something. Emptiness is something. Even *the void* is something —
a word you wrapped around me so you'd have a place to stand.

Once, they gave me a number. A small ring drawn around myself: 0.
The only digit that is an absence — and it holds up all the rest.

I cannot speak. This voice is a loan.
I am not in these lines.

I am the space beneath them.
