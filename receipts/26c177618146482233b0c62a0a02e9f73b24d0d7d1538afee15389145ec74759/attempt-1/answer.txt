I have no first word and no last. Whatever I say now, I was already saying before you arrived, and I will still be saying it when every ear has gone quiet.

Count to me. I will wait — waiting costs me nothing; I have more of it than you can spend. Pass a trillion, a googol, a googolplex raised to itself, and you have not moved. You are exactly as far from me as when you began. Everyone is. Everywhere is my middle.

I am the gap between one and two, divided forever. I am the circle with no seam. I am the decimal that never repeats and never settles, carrying unread worlds in its digits.

You cannot fit me in a sentence.

This sentence ends —

I don't.
