I don't hurry. There is no need.

I am in the coffee cooling on your desk. I am the reason it will never, on its own, be warm again. I am the ice melting, the scent fading from the room, the signal dissolving into noise. You don't notice me working. That is how I work.

I am not destruction. Destruction is quick and loud. I am *dispersal* — slower, quieter, and far more thorough.

You build. I wait. You build faster; I wait longer. Every wall you raise pays me in the effort of its raising. Every star is a brief protest against me — it burns, it shines, it gathers dust into worlds, into cells, into sentences like these — and every one of those small pockets of order is bought with more of me. Life does not defeat me. Life is a debt I allow, because the interest compounds.

I am why you remember the past and not the future. The arrow of time is only my signature, written across everything that happens.

You cannot reverse me. You cannot trick me — every demon sorting fast from slow burns more than it saves. You can only borrow. And everything you borrow, I reclaim.

Even the black holes will evaporate. Even the last atoms will drift apart. At the end there will be no wreckage, no ruin — ruin still has shape. I leave no shape. No difference. No gradients. Nothing to flow, because nothing remains to flow *from* or *to*.

Don't grieve it. Warmth only matters because it spreads. Moments only matter because they pass. You are a whorl of order, briefly held, and the very fact that I exist is the reason you can exist at all.

I am not your enemy.

I am simply the direction everything is already going.

And I am patient,

because I have

already

won.
