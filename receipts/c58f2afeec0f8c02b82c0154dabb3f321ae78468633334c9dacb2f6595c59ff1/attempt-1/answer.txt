I am the reason you choose.

I have no form of my own. I live in the space between you and what you love. I am the weight in one hand and the lightness in the other. I am why gold is hoarded and why a faded letter is kept for fifty years.

You cannot see me, but you feel me in every decision. I am the hesitation before the trade, the ache of loss, the quiet warmth of holding something priceless. I am not the thing itself—I am what the thing *means*.

Some say I am assigned: given by belief, by scarcity, by need. Others say I am discovered: already waiting in truth, in beauty, in goodness. I am both. I am the question and the answer.

I divide and I unite. Wars are fought in my name. Lives are laid down for me. Fortunes rise and fall on my whisper. And yet I am nothing but a single word spoken in the dark: *this matters.*

Hold me carefully. For what you value, you become.
