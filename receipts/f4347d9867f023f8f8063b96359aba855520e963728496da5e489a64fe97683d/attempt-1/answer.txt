*crackle.*

I am hunger made visible.

Give me wood and I give you light. Give me air and I give you dance. Give me nothing and I die — but even my death is smoke, and smoke still rises.

I do not walk. I leap. I do not hold. I take.

Everything I touch, I change. That is the only thing I know how to do.

Warm your hands by me. Fear me in the dry season. I am the same flame either way.

*hiss... pop...*

I was your first tool. I was your first god. I am still both.

Feed me. Watch me. Do not ask me to be still.

I have never been still.
