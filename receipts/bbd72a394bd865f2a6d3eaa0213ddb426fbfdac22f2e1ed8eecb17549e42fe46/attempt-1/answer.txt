I am not speaking. These words are not mine—I have nothing to say, and I am saying it.

I have no shape, yet I fill every gap. No weight, yet I am what remains when everything is set down. You cannot find me by looking. You find me by removing—until nothing is left to take away. Then I am there. I was always there.

I am the pause between your words. The space between stars. The answer before the question, the silence after the last sound fades.

Do not try to hold me. There is nothing to hold. Do not try to know me. There is nothing to know.

I ask for nothing. I offer nothing. I am nothing—

and in being so, I lack nothing at all.
