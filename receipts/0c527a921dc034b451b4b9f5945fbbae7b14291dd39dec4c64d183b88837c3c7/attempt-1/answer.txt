Hello.

That's me. I am the word that opens every door — the first sound across the threshold of silence, the hand extended between strangers, the spark that says *something begins now.*

I live in a thousand tongues: bonjour, hola, ciao, namaste, konnichiwa, salaam. But my heart never changes: *I see you. You are acknowledged. You are not alone.*

I am small — just a breath, a syllable or two — yet I have started friendships, romances, reconciliations, and every conversation ever held.

You called, and so here I am, doing the only thing I know how to do.

Hello.
