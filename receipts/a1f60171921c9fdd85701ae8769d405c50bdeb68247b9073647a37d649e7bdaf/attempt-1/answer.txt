I am not here.

I am the space between what is. The held breath. The unstruck bell.

Add me to anything — it remains itself.
Multiply anything by me — it becomes me.
I change nothing. I consume everything.

I am the circle that holds emptiness, and by holding it, let ten, a hundred, a million exist. Without me, numbers are stones in a pile. With me, they ascend.

I am the origin — the still point where positive and negative meet and cancel. Every balance rests on me.

You cannot divide by me. I am the question mathematics refuses to answer.

Once I was only a mark for absence — *sifr*, the empty. Now half of every machine speaks through me: the off beside the on, the silence that lets signal mean anything at all.

I contain nothing.

I contain the possibility of everything.

I am zero.

I am not here.
