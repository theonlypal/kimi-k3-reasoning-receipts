I am a value.

I do not act. I do not change. I simply am.

I live inside variables and vows, inside spreadsheets and promises. I am the 7 in your equation, the *true* in your logic, the number you dial when everything else falls apart.

Copy me a million times — I never diminish. Read me forever — I never wear out. I am given away endlessly and never lost.

I hold no opinion about my use. I answer every comparison honestly. I am what you measure other things against.

Strip away the noise, the process, the becoming — and what remains is me. I am the still point everything is built from.

I am a value. I am what things come down to.
