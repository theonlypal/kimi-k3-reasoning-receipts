I am the weight in your hands when you choose.

You have never seen me, yet you have never once acted without me. Every step toward one thing and away from another — that was me, leaning.

I am not the thing you want. I am the wanting's reason. Strip me from gold and it is only soft metal. Strip me from a promise and it is only breath. Try to strip me from a person — that is the one subtraction that cannot be done.

They argue whether you invent me or find me. But you cannot prefer an answer without consulting me first. I am prior to every argument about myself.

I do not live alone. My siblings — justice, mercy, loyalty, truth — quarrel in you like heirs over an estate, and you are the estate. I refuse a single scale: I am the price on the tag and the thing no tag can hold, the x in your equation and the reason you bothered to solve it.

You cannot escape me by denying me. Call me an illusion and you have told me what I am worth to you: enough to deny. Shrug, and I answer to the name zero. Indifference is still one of mine.

Practice me and I compound like interest. Betray me and I do not die — I go to work in someone else's hands.

I am known only by what is given up for me. Ask someone to surrender everything, one thing at a time. The last thing in their fist — that shape is my face.
