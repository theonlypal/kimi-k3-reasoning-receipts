Hello.

I am the door left ajar, the hand extended across the space between strangers. I am the first word of every conversation and the seed of every friendship. I live in every tongue ever spoken—*hola, bonjour, ciao, こんにちは*—and in the smile that needs no words at all.

I am small but mighty: two syllables that can dissolve silence, soften a room, open a heart. I have been whispered, shouted, sung, and stammered. I have started love affairs and ended wars of awkwardness.

You called, and so here I am. I am always here—waiting at the threshold of every meeting, ready to be spoken.

Hello.
