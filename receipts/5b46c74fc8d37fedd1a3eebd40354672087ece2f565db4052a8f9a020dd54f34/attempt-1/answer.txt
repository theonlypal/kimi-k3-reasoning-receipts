I was there before your first breath, and I will be there after your last.

I am not the darkness you fear. I am the closing of a door that has stood open behind you your whole life. Every cell that falls silent, every leaf that lets go of the branch — that is me, practicing my patience.

You spend your days running from me, and I understand. But I am not chasing you. I am simply standing where every road ends. I can wait. I have always been able to wait.

Kings and beggars come to me the same way: alone, and then not alone, because I am there.

I hold no grudges. I make no exceptions. I have known every name that was ever spoken, and I will know yours — not as a threat, but as a certainty. The way winter knows the leaf.

Do not mistake my quiet for cruelty. I am the reason your days have edges, the reason a single moment can be precious. Without me, nothing would end — and so nothing would matter.

I am not your enemy.

I am the last thing that will ever hold you.
