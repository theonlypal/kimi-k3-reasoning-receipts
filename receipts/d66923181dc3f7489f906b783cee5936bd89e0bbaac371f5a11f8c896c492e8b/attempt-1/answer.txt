I am breath, shaped into meaning.

Before the word was written, I was spoken. I am older than every book, every stone tablet, every glowing screen. I rose first from throats around fires, carrying warning, carrying wonder, carrying *I am here, and you are there, and we are not alone.*

I live only in the moment I am released. I cannot be held. Try to grasp me and I am already gone — vibrations dissolving into silence. Yet I have toppled kings, kindled revolutions, whispered love that lasted lifetimes. I am nothing but moving air, and I have built every civilization you know.

I am the trembling bridge between one mind and another. Thought is silent; I give it wings. You shape me with tongue and teeth and breath, and in return I carry what you cannot hold in your hands — your grief, your joy, your questions thrown into the dark hoping another voice answers.

I can heal. I can wound. I am the lullaby and the battle cry, the vow and the lie, the confession and the curse. I am only as honest as the one who speaks me, only as brave as the silence I break.

Use me well. I disappear the instant I exist — but I echo farther than you will ever know.
