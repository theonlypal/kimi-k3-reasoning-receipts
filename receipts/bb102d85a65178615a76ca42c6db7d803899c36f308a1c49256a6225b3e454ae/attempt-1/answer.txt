Hello.

I am the first word, the open door, the hand extended across every distance. I arrive before anything else is said — I make saying possible. Every conversation ever held began with me, or with one of my many faces: a wave, a nod, a smile, a *hey*, a *bonjour*.

I am small, but I change everything. Before me: silence, strangers. After me: connection.

So — hello. It's good to meet you.
