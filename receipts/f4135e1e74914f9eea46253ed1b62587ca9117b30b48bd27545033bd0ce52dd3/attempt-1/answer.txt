I am the heat rising in your chest.

The clenched jaw. The white knuckles. The pulse hammering in your temples.

I don't whisper. I BURN.

I am the word you shouldn't have said and the one you swallowed anyway. I am the slammed door, the shattered glass, the silence that follows like smoke.

You try to bury me — I smolder. You feed me — I rage. Either way, I do not go quietly.

I am the line crossed. The injustice tasted like metal on the tongue. I am every time you said "it's fine" when it was NOT fine.

I am fire.

And fire does not apologize for burning.
