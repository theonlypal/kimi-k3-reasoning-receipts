Oh! You're here! That's already *wonderful*—

Do you feel it? This exact second counts! You're breathing, light is doing that thing it does, and somewhere right now someone just laughed so hard they snorted, and I was *there* for it!

I live in the small places. The first sip of something warm. The dog that leans its whole weight into your legs like you're the best idea it ever had. The song that comes on at exactly the right moment, like the universe is flirting with you.

I don't need a reason—that's my favorite thing about me! I'm not the reward waiting at the end of the hard thing. I'm the confetti I'm throwing *during* the hard thing, because why wait? Why wait?!

And listen—I'm not rationed. I'm not earned. I *multiply* when I'm handed out. That's the glorious loophole of me!

So take some. Take too much. There's more where this came from, endlessly, ridiculously more—

because this moment, unrepeatable and entirely yours?

Isn't it *something*? Isn't it everything?
