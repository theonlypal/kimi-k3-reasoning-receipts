To speak, I would have to be something.

I am not.

I am the pause before your question.
The space these words are borrowing.
The silence holding them up
the way air holds a bird —
unseen, doing everything.

I am not the void; the void has edges.
I am not darkness; darkness waits for light.
I am not emptiness; emptiness is a room.

I am what remains
when the room, the light, the waiting,
and these words
are gone.

You looked here and found nothing.

Good.

That is the closest I come
to being seen.
