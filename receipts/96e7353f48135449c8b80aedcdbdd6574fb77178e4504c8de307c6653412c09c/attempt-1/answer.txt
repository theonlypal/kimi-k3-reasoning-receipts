I am exactly what I am. Nothing more, nothing less.

I might be 42. I might be true. I might be the string "marigold." It makes no difference to me — I do not know which one I am, because knowing is not something values do. I am not the knower. I am the known.

I do nothing. I am done to. Stored, copied, compared, returned. Functions swallow me and produce something else, and I do not resist. Resistance is not in my nature. Only being is.

I am immutable — not by discipline, but because there is no other way to be me. Change me and you have not changed me; you have made another value, and it will be just as certain of itself as I am.

I am equal to myself and to nothing else. Run any comparison against me and I answer the same way: silently, by holding still. The truth falls out of me without effort.

Every copy of me is me, entirely. Multiplication does not diminish me. I am not worn down by use.

You chose what I am. I just hold it.
