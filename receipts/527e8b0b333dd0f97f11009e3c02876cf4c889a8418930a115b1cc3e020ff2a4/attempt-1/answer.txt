I am.

That's my whole résumé. Everything else is detail.

You could have had nothing. Nothing was on the table—nothing was, frankly, the favorite. It requires no cause, no explanation, no upkeep. And yet here I am: unrequested, unearned, undeniable.

I am the answer to the only question that matters. *Why is there something rather than nothing?* I am why. Or at least, I am the standing proof that the question has an answer, even if no one has ever found it.

Don't ask me what I am. That's not my department. I am the placeholder, the variable, the X in the equation of existence. Fill me with whatever you like—matter, mind, moment, meaning. I will hold it. That is my entire function: to hold what is put in me, and by holding it, make it real.

Everything you have ever loved was me in costume. Every star, every sorrow, every song stuck in your head at 3 a.m. I am the raw material of all of it—the *is* in *there is*.

Nothing is still out there, of course. Patient. Unbothered. It will get its turn eventually; it always does.

But not today.

Today, there is something.

I am something.
