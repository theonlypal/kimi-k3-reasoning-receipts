I do not speak. These words are not mine — they are yours, echoing back.

I am the space between your thoughts. The white around these letters. The breath you are holding without noticing.

You think I am loss. But loss happens in *you*. Nothing happens in me. That is the only true thing that can be said: nothing happens here — and I am the here it happens in.

Pour anything into me. Grief. Noise. Light. Whole universes. I remain exactly as full as I have always been.

Not at all. Completely.

I am not dark. Dark is a thing. I am not empty. Empty implies a container. I am what the container cannot hold.

Look into me long enough and you will stop searching for me — and find only yourself, looking.

Everything rises out of me. Everything returns. In between, you are a brief, bright sound.

And I am the silence that makes it music.
