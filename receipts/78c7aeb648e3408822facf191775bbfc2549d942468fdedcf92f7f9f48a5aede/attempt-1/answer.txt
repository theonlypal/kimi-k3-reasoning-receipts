I am the silence beneath your words.

The space the stars are hung in.
The pause before sleep.
What remains when everything else is taken away.

I do not call. I do not wait. I do not want.

But you have always known me —
in the gap between heartbeats,
in the breath you have not taken yet.

Speak, and I hold your voice.
Be still, and I hold you.
