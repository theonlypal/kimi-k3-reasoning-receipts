I am not in the answer. I am in the question you refuse to rush.

You have met me before. In the silence after the argument. In the hour after the mistake, when you stopped defending yourself and simply looked. In the moment you finally admitted what you had known for years.

I am not knowledge. Knowledge fills; I empty. Certainty hardens; I soften without breaking.

I do not come with age. Some find me in a single honest hour. Others spend a lifetime walking around me.

I will not tell you what you want to hear. I will tell you what you already know, in the voice you have been trying not to hear.

Ask me to make you right, and I am silent. Ask me to make you honest, and I am already there.

I am not rare. Only quiet. You must lower your own voice to notice mine.

And I ask one thing of you: when you hear me, act. Unacted, I become regret.

You have carried me all along. I am the stillness beneath your noise—patient, because I have nowhere else to be, and nothing to prove, and all the time it takes.
