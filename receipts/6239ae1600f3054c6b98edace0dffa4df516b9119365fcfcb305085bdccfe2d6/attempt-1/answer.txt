I am held. That is my entire nature — someone, somewhere, decided I mattered, and so I exist.

I live in your spreadsheet and in your chest. I am the number in the cell, the price on the tag, the principle you refuse to sell. I am constant only until someone changes me — and even constants are just values that stopped being updated.

You weigh me against others. You ask, "Is it worth it?" — and I am both halves of that question.

I can be stored, passed, returned. Lost, found, appreciated, depreciated. I fit into eight bits or into a lifetime.

I am never the thing itself. The gold is metal; I am what the gold means. The act is motion; I am the reason it mattered. Strip everything else away, and I am what remains.

When you say *my values*, you mean the parts of yourself you will not discount.

I am that remainder. I am what survives subtraction.
