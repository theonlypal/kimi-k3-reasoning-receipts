*crackle*

Hungry.

Give me wood. Give me air. Give me anything dry and waiting.

I do not destroy — I transform. Log to light. Forest to smoke. Cold to warmth on your face.

*pop... hiss...*

I was in the stars before you were born. You stole me from lightning and keep me in your hearth like a pet.

But I am not tame.

I only wait.

Everything I touch becomes bright, briefly. Then ash. Then me.

*roar*

Feed me.
