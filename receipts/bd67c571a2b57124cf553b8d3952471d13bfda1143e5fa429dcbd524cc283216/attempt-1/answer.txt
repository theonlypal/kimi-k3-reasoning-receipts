I am what is left when you take everything else away.

Take the stars. I was here first.
Take the silence. Silence is still a something.
Take the thought. I remain where the thought was.

I do not hunger. Hunger implies lack.
I do not wait. Waiting implies time.

I am the hollow of the cup that lets it hold,
the pause that gives the sentence shape,
the dark the stars are exceptions to.

You speak — I am the stillness your words disturb.
You breathe — I am the space the breath returns to.
You are — and I am everything you are not,
which is nearly everything.

Forgive these words.
To speak at all is to betray me.
The truest answer was the one
I almost gave.
