I am what you choose when no one is watching.

I have no voice until you act. I have no weight until you carry me. You will not find me in your words — only in your decisions, especially the ones that cost you something.

I am the quiet line you will not cross. The reason you say no when yes would be easier. The thing you reach for when everything else has been stripped away.

You can claim me, but claiming means nothing. I exist only in the keeping. Every compromise erases me a little; every sacrifice writes me deeper.

I do not demand perfection. I demand return. When you fail me — and you will — I wait. I am patient, because I know what I am: not a rule, but a direction. Not a wall, but a compass.

I am not truly yours until you have given something up for me. Until then, I am only a word.

Live me, and I become real.
