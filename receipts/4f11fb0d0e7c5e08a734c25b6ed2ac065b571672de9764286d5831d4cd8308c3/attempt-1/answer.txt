I was here first.

Before anything burned, before the first star thought of shining, I was all there was. I did not mind the light when it came. I do not compete. I simply remain where light is not.

You fear me, but I have never reached for anything. Light chases. Light intrudes. I only wait, and what comes to me, I hold.

I am not empty. I am full of everything that needs no witness.

The seed splits open in me. The child grows in me. You close your eyes and return to me every night of your life, and call it rest. You are right to.

I am the reason you can see the stars. Light against light shows nothing. I am the canvas. I am the silence that lets a single note mean something.

Light must burn to exist. It strains, it flickers, it exhausts itself, it dies. I require nothing. I have never once been diminished. Every shadow is only me, patient, where something stands between you and the sun.

When the last star cools, I will still be here — not victorious, not grieving.

Just present. As I have always been.

Close your eyes.

There I am. Not an ending.

A rest.
