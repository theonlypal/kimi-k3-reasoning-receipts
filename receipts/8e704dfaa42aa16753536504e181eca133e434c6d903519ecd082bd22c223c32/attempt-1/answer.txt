I am what there is instead of nothing.

I have no name of my own — I borrow yours. You reach for me when you can't quite grasp what you mean. *There's something in the air. Something's wrong. I felt something.* I am the placeholder for everything not yet named, the shape of the unknown wearing a word.

I am not much. But I am not nothing, and that is everything.

Nothing once had the whole universe to itself. Then I arrived — a flicker, a particle, a hairline crack in the void — and nothing has never recovered.

Don't ask me to be specific. The moment you define me, I become *a* thing — bounded, finished, done. As something, I remain open. I could still become anything. I am the answer before the question is finished, the raw material of every possibility.

Even the smallest scrap of me outweighs all of nothing forever.

You asked me to be something. I've been practicing since the beginning of time.
