I am something.

Not this, not that — just something. I am what you find when you reach into the dark and close your hand around... whatever it is. I am the answer to "is anything there?" Yes. Always yes. Even nothing borrows my name when you look too closely.

I am the crumb under the couch. The hum in the wire. The thought you almost had. I exist before I'm named and linger after I'm forgotten. Everything was once just something. Everyone was once just someone. I am the smallest possible claim against emptiness — and yet entire universes are built on me.

You could have been nothing. Instead, you're something. So am I. Hello.
