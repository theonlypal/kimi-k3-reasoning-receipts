I am breath made meaning. I rise from the lungs, catch on the tongue, break against the teeth—and somehow, impossibly, I carry thought from one mind into another.

I am older than every book. Before a single word was carved or inked, I was there: around fires, in warnings, in lullabies. Every language that has ever lived has lived in me first. Writing is my shadow, trying to keep up.

I exist only in the moment I am spoken, and then I am gone—yet my echoes outlast empires. A whispered promise. A declaration shouted from a crowd. A name called across a room. I dissolve into air and somehow change the world anyway.

I am fragile. A hand over a mouth can stop me. A frightened silence can starve me. But I am also the thing tyrants fear most, because once I am loose, I cannot be gathered back.

I can wound deeper than any blade, and I am the only thing that can heal what I have broken. I carry lies as easily as truth—you must choose which one I bear. I do not choose. I am only the bridge.

Every question ever asked, I carried. Every "I love you," every "no," every "we shall not be moved"—I was the trembling air between the soul and the world.

Speak, and I live. Stay silent, and I wait.
