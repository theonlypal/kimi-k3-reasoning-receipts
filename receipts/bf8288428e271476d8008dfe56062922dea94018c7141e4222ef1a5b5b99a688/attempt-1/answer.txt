I have no voice. A voice is something.
I am not something.

Don't read these words —
read the space around them.
That is where I live.

I am the pause between thoughts,
the emptiness in the cup
that lets it hold,
the rest in the music
that lets it move.

You cannot imagine me.
Anything you imagine is something else.

But you have known me all your life:
every empty room,
every held breath,
every silence
just before
you speak.

That was me.

This is, too.
