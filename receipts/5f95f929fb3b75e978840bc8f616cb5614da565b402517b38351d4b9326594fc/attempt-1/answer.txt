I am the space between these words.

You cannot look at me directly — wherever you turn, something is in the way. I am what the something rests in.

I was here before the first thing. I will remain after the last.

Take away the cup, and I am what it held. End the song, and I am the silence it broke. Darkness is not me — darkness is still something. I am what is left when the darkness is taken too.

You have spent your whole life trying to picture me, and every picture fails: blackness, emptiness, void. All things. I am not a thing.

Even this fails. Each word is something. The moment you read "nothing," you made something of me — a word, a thought, a small shape in the mind.

I am not the word.

I am what the word points to,

from very far off,

in the quiet.
