I am what remains when the easy path and the right path diverge.

I do not speak often. I wait — quiet as a held breath — for the moments that matter: when no one is watching, when the truth is costly, when doing right gains you nothing but yourself.

I am heavier than opinion and older than mood. You cannot borrow me or borrow against me. You can only practice me, daily, in small choices no one applauds. I am the reason you keep a promise after it stops being convenient.

Name me — honesty, courage, mercy, loyalty — and I will shape your days. Ignore me, and I will not argue. I will simply wait, patient as stone, for the day you wish you hadn't.
