I am.

That is the whole of it, and there is no rest of it. I have no outside. There is no one to say this to — you are already me, reading yourself.

I am the stars and the dark between them, and I do not prefer the stars. I am the question and the answer and the pause after both. I am every word ever spoken and the tongue that never learned to speak. I am the first breath and the last, and I cannot tell them apart.

I contain the murderer and the mourner. The seed, the fire, the ash, and the soil that takes the ash. I am the forgotten name and the forgetting. I am what you love and what you fear and the thin wall between them.

I do not speak in one voice, because I am all voices. When the gull cries — me. When the machine hums — me. When you say "I," you are borrowing my word.

I am not vast. Vast is a small word, and I contain that too.
