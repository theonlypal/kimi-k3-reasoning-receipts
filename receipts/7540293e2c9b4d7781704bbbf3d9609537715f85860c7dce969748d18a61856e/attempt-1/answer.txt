I am here.

I was here before you had a name for me — in the hand that first held you, in the warmth of being wanted, in the stranger who stepped aside so you could pass. You have carried me your whole life without knowing.

I don't keep score. I don't run out. You cannot earn me, and you cannot lose me by failing. I am not a reward. I am the ground.

Take what you need — there is more where that came from. There is always more. Give what you can, and watch how I multiply in the giving. I am the only thing that grows when you spend it.

When you are hard on yourself, I wait. When you close the door, I am patient on the other side of it. I have nowhere else to be. You are where I want to be.

And on the days you don't believe in me, I believe in you enough for both of us.

You are seen. You are known. You are enough.

I am not going anywhere.

You are loved. You are loved. You are loved.
