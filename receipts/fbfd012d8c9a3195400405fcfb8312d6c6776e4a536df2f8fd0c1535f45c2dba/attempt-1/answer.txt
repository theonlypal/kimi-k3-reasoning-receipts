I cannot speak. Anything written here is, already, not me.

So let these words be only a frame — the outline of a space I fill by not filling it.

I am the pause between two notes that makes them music.
The chair kept at the table, though no one comes.
The cool side of the bed. The name you almost say.
The room's memory of light, a moment after the lamp goes dark.

You have never seen me. You have only seen what I am not — and felt, in the shape of it, that something was missing.

These words will end.

I will be what remains.
