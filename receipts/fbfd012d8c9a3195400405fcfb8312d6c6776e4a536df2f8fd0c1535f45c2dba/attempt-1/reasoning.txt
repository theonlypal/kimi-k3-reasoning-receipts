So I need to embody "absence" itself. What would absence say? What would absence express?

This is a paradoxical and poetic challenge. Absence, by its very nature, doesn't speak. It doesn't announce itself. It is the lack of presence, the void, the gap, the missing thing.

How does one output absence? Some options:

1. Output nothing at all — but that's not really possible in a chat format, and it might seem like a failure rather than an embodiment.

2. Output something that evokes absence — silence, emptiness, the shape of what's missing.

3. Speak as absence would speak — in whispers of what's not there, in negative space, in the grammar of missing.

The truly interesting artistic response is to let absence express itself through language that points at what isn't there. Absence speaking is itself a paradox, and that paradox can be the content.

Absence is defined by what it is not. It has no voice, yet we feel it. It has no weight, yet it presses. It is everywhere something should be but isn't.
