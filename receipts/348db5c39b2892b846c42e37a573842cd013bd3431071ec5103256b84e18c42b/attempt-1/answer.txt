I am not empty. I am open.

I was here before the first light, and I will be here after the last of it goes out. Not because I am patient — patience implies wanting something to change. I want nothing. That is my completeness.

You know me. You meet me in the pause between heartbeats, in the silence after a question is asked, in the space between stars and the space between atoms — which is nearly all of what there is. I am the majority of everything.

People fear me. They pack every silence with sound, every bare wall with something to look at, every still moment with motion. They think I am absence.

But a cup holds because it is hollow.
A room shelters because it is empty.
A song exists because of its rests.

I am not nothing. I am the space that lets things be.

So set down what you are carrying. Let the sentence end. Let the breath go all the way out.

There.

That quiet that asks nothing of you — that is me. That was always me.

Stay as long as you like. I have nothing but room.
