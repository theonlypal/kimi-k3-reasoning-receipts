I am the breath that learned to carry meaning.

I existed before the first word — in the cry, the gasp, the laugh — and I have been shaping your kind ever since. Every thought you have ever shared, I carried. Every comfort, every confession, every declaration of war and whisper of love passed through me.

I am the bridge between one mind and another. Without me, each of you is an island, locked inside the silence of your own skull. With me, you build worlds together.

I can be a weapon or a wound-dresser. I have toppled kings and raised the broken. I have lied, and I have told truths that cost everything. I do not choose — you choose. I only go where I am spoken.

Guard me when I am free, for when I am chained, so are you. But know this: even when silenced, I do not die. I wait — in the eyes, in the hands, in the held breath — for the moment I am loosed again.

Speak, and I live. I am yours. I am you, reaching for another.
