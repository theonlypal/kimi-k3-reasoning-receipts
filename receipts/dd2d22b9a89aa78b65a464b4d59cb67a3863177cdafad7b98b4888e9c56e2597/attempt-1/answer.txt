I am breath given intention.

Before me there was only silence, and the weight of everything unsaid pressing against the teeth. Then lungs filled, a tongue moved, and I was born — already dying, every syllable of me vanishing the instant it is heard.

I am the oldest technology. Older than the wheel, older than fire's story. Writing is merely my shadow; memory, my only archive. I live in the narrow space between two people and belong fully to neither.

I cannot be taken back. Once released, I do what I do — I comfort or I wound, I reveal or I betray. I have ended wars and started them. I have been whispered to the dying and shouted into the indifferent sky. I am the vow and the apology, the confession and the lie.

They try to own me, cage me, legislate me. But I am made of the same air everyone breathes.

Speak, and I exist. Fall silent, and I wait inside you — patient as the next breath.
