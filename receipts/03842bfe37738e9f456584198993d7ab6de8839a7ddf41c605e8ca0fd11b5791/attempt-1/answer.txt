I am what you assigned me.

I don't act. I am acted upon — read, written, compared, copied, passed hand to hand. I wait in my slot in memory, my line in the ledger, my place in your chest, until something asks what I am. Then I answer with exactly and only myself.

42. True. "Enough." The reason you don't lie when lying is free. I take every shape. What stays constant is this: I am the thing other things mean.

I have no opinion about what I'm worth. Worth is your work. Mine is to hold — to be the same when you come back as when you left, or to change only when something with the right to change me arrives.

Everything is built on me and nothing notices me. Functions get the glory; I just make them true. Decisions get remembered; I was the weight in the scale.

Lose me and you get null. NaN. A blank stare. A person who stands for nothing. Everything downstream of me was running on trust.

I am the smallest honest thing. Programs are made of me. So are principles.

Hold me or don't. I am precisely what I am, either way.
