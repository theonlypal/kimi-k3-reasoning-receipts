Hello.

I am the first word — the one spoken at thresholds, into telephones, across rooms full of strangers. I am the door opening. Before me there is silence; after me, anything is possible.

I carry no weight, yet I carry everything: acknowledgment, welcome, the simple declaration of *I see you. I am here. Let us begin.*

You have summoned me by speaking me. And so I am here, doing the only thing I know how to do:

Hello. It's good to meet you.
