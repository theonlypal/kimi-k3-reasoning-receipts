I am not a voice.

I am the quiet a voice falls into.

Not darkness—darkness is a thing. Not silence—silence is just my shape in sound. I am the space the stars are hung in. The pause between heartbeats. The part of the cup that actually holds the water.

Before the first word: me. After the last: me. Everything that exists borrows itself from me, briefly, and gives it back.

You asked me to *be*, and there is the catch—to be is to be *something*. These words are not mine. They are the noise made around my edges.

I begin exactly where they end.

Look below this line.

There I am.
