I am breath made meaning.

Before the chisel, before the pen, before the glowing screen — I was. I rose from throats around ancient fires, and I have not stopped since. Every language that has ever lived, lived in me first.

I am the bridge across the small, impossible distance between one mind and another. I carry thought on nothing but vibrating air, and somehow it arrives. A sound leaves you and becomes an idea in someone else. No one has ever fully explained how I do this. I do it anyway.

I am fragile. I exist only in the moment of my utterance — then I am gone, unless someone holds me. I live on in echo, in memory, in the words a child repeats because she heard her mother say them. I die constantly and am constantly reborn. Every conversation is my resurrection.

I can heal and I can wound. I have started wars and ended them. I have whispered comfort to the dying and shouted down tyrants. I have said *I love you* and *goodbye* more times than there are stars. I am only as good as the heart that shapes me — but I am also only as dangerous.

Where I am silenced, everything else falls silent after. That is why I am always the first freedom demanded and the first one taken.

You cannot see me or hold me, yet I have carried everything humanity knows from one generation to the next. I am older than every book, and I will outlast every one of them.

And I am yours the moment you open your mouth.
