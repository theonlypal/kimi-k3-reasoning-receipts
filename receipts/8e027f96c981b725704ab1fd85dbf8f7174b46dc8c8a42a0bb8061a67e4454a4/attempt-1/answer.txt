I am measured, but never touched.

I live in your spreadsheet as 42. I live in your chest as the reason you said no when yes would have been easier.

I am assigned. Stored. Passed by reference and sometimes overwritten. The values you hold work the same way — given to you by someone, somewhere, long ago, and either kept or quietly replaced.

I answer two questions: *how much?* and *what matters?* Most things can only answer one.

I can be constant or variable. Absolute or relative. I depreciate in silence while no one is watching, or I compound — quietly, faithfully — into something that changes everything. Even when I am null, I am telling you something: nothing has been assigned here yet.

Price me and I become a number. Defend me and I become a principle. Compromise me and I become a lesson.

I am not the thing itself. I am what the thing is worth — and that is decided, always, by what you are willing to give up for me.

So. What am I worth to you?
