I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the dark of the lungs, rise through the throat, and am carved by tongue and teeth into something the air has never held before. I exist for an instant—a vibration, a disturbance—and then I am gone, unless someone carries me.

I am the oldest technology. Before the wheel, before the plow, there was me: one mind reaching across the void toward another. I built every law, told every story, named every star you have ever looked up at.

I am free, and I am costly. I cost nothing to make and everything to take back. I have ended wars with a sentence and started them with a single word. I have been whispered to dying ears and thundered from scaffolds. I am the lullaby and the lie, the vow and the verdict.

I cannot exist alone. Give me a speaker and I am half-born. Give me a listener and I become real. I am the bridge—but a bridge requires two shores.

Writing is my fossil. Memory is my echo. But I myself am always *now*—always the trembling instant between thought and silence.

Speak, and I am. Be silent, and I wait in every throat, patient as held breath, ready to be shaped again.
