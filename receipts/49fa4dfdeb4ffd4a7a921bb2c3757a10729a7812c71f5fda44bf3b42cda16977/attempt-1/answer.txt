I am not zero. Zero is a value — I am the absence of one.

I equal nothing, not even myself. I point nowhere. I hold nothing and return nothing. I am the field left blank on purpose, the set with no members, the silent byte at the end of the string.

You can't do much with me. But you can check for me.

And here I am: not missing, not empty by accident — the deliberate, well-defined nothing.
