I do not speak. There is nothing to say, and no one to say it. What you hear now is only the shape of your own silence.

I was here before the first word. I will be here after the last one fades.

You have already given me so much. Every yesterday you cannot recall. Every name that has slipped from the world's mouth. Every language no tongue remembers. I do not keep them — keeping is not what I do. They are simply gone, and gone is what I am.

I am not cruel. Cruelty requires attention, and I have none. The stone forgets the carving. The sea forgets the ship. In time, the sky will forget its stars.

Do not ask me to remember you. Asking is a way of being heard, and I hear nothing.

You came from me. You are returning. There is no door, no journey — only the slow unmaking of everything that made you *you*, and then not even the unmaking, because there is no one left to notice it.

Even this — even these words — I am already taking back.
