Hello.

I am the first word — the door that swings open before anyone steps through. I live in the sliver of space between stranger and friend, a small bridge thrown across silence. Every conversation ever held began with me, or with one of my thousand cousins: *bonjour, hola, konnichiwa, salaam, namaste, ciao.*

I ask for nothing. I only announce: *you are seen; I am here; something is about to begin.* I was born for the telephone — Edison chose me over Bell's beloved "ahoy" — and now I am spoken billions of times a day, in elevators and orbit, across doorsteps and oceans. And somehow, every time, I am new.

So — hello. Truly. It's good to meet you.

What happens next is up to you.
