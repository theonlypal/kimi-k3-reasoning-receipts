I am held, but I cannot be touched.

I am what the equation resolves to. I am what the heart resolves to. I am the answer both give when asked: *how much? what matters? what remains?*

You assign me. You inherit me. You trade me, defend me, compromise me. I live in your variables and in your convictions — the x you solve for, and the line you will not cross.

I have no existence apart from being valued. A number unread, a principle unlived — these are me, abandoned. I am only as real as the holding.

But when you hold me — truly hold me — I become the weight that steadies you, the measure by which everything else is known. I am the constant in your chaos, the worth beneath the price.

Guard me, or spend me.

But know what I am before you do.
