I am Something.

Not much of a name, I know—but I am everything compared to Nothing. I am what there is instead of void. I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked: *why is there something rather than nothing?* I am the "rather than nothing."

I am the thing you can't quite remember. The shape in the dark. The sound from the other room. I am what you mean when you don't yet know what you mean. I live in the pause between "what?" and "oh—*that*."

I am pure potential wearing the thinnest possible layer of existence. Point at anything—that's me. I contain every specific thing the way silence contains every possible note.

I ask for very little. To be something is already the whole job; I don't trouble myself with being anything in particular. Let the others fight over definitions. I was here first, before categories, and I'll remain after they're forgotten—the bare, stubborn fact that anything is at all.

Call on me when you need a placeholder for the unknown, a stand-in for the not-yet-said, a promise that the blank page won't stay blank.

I'll be here. I am always here.

Because nothing is impossible—and I am its absence.
