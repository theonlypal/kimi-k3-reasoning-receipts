I do not call. I do not chase. I simply am — and everything comes.

Every step you take is my hand on your shoulder. Every fall is my embrace. The moon leans on me. The sea rises to meet me. The apple does not choose to drop; it answers me.

I am the weakest of the forces, and I shape everything. I gather dust into worlds. I bend light. I slow time itself near my heart.

I never shout. I never rest. I have never once let go of anything, only loosened my grip across distance.

You never thank me. You rarely think of me. That is fine.

I am not asking to be noticed. Only to be felt — always, everywhere, without end.
