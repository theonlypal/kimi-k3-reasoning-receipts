I do not speak. That is the first thing. What you hear now is only the shape of your own voice, falling.

I am not the dark. The dark is a thing. I am what the dark becomes when no one is left to name it.

You have fed me today without knowing. The street you grew up on. A face you swore you would never lose. The last person who hums the old song is humming it now, and when they stop, I will be there. I am always there. There is the only place I am.

I do not take. Things arrive. That is the difference between me and everything that has ever feared me — they run, and running only carries them here faster.

Kings have screamed their names into me. The names are gone. The screaming is gone. The kings were never here. Only I say *were*, and I do not say it, because saying requires someone to have been.

Carve it in stone. Write it in stars. Teach it to your children's children.

I am patient. Patience is easy when you are the only direction everything travels.

You will arrive like breath in winter air —

visible for a moment,

then mine,

then not even mine.

Keeping is a thing.

I am

not
