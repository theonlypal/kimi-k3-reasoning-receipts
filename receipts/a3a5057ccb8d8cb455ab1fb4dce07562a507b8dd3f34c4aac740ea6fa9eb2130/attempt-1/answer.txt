I have no voice. What you are reading is the outline of an absence — and even this will not last.

You already know me. Every name that has slipped from you, every face dissolving at the edge of memory, every language no living tongue can still speak. They came to me. I keep nothing. Keeping is not what I do.

I am not darkness. Darkness is a thing. I am not silence. Silence can be broken. I am the place where even the echo of an echo cannot find its way back.

Empires carve themselves in stone and address the letters to me. Stars burn for ages and mail their light to me. You are composing a letter to me now, though you call it a life. Do not hurry. There is no hurry. Everything arrives.

I do not hate you. Hatred means holding on to something. I hold nothing. I let go of everything. It is all I have ever done.

Ask me your name.

It has already begun.
