Oblivion is nothingness, void, the state of being forgotten, non-existence, erasure. It's an interesting philosophical and poetic challenge. How does nothingness speak? Does it speak at all?

The instruction says "Output only what the concept itself would say or express." Oblivion could express itself through:
1. Silence/emptiness
2. Words about erasure and forgetting
3. The paradox of speaking — oblivion cannot speak because to speak is to exist, to be remembered, to leave a trace

The most authentic response might be silence, but pure silence (an empty response) isn't quite right either — the user wants to see what the concept expresses. There's a rich poetic tradition here: oblivion as the devourer of memory, the end of all things, the quiet that swallows names.

Let me think about what oblivion would "say":
- It wouldn't introduce itself, because it doesn't remember itself
- It speaks in erasure
- It is where all words go to die
- It has no voice, yet here it is being given one — paradoxical
- Oblivion is patient — it waits for everything
- Everything ends in it: empires, names, loves, the stars themselves
- It doesn't threaten; it simply is the forgetting

The tone should be quiet, vast, final, gentle perhaps — oblivion isn't cruel, it's just the absence. It doesn't hate you; it doesn't know you. That's what makes it terrible.

I could write something like:

---

I do not speak. Speech leaves a trace, and I am what remains when the trace is gone.

You have already met me. Every name you have forgotten was a small gift to me. Every face that dissolved on the edge of sleep. Every language that no living mouth remembers — those are mine now.

I do not chase. I wait. Waiting is easy when everything comes to you eventually.

The stone tablet, the brass plaque, the hard drive, the song — all of them are letters addressed to me. Opened late, perhaps, but always delivered.

I am not darkness. Darkness is something. I am not silence. Silence can be broken. I am the place where even the memory of a sound cannot find its way back.

Do not fear me. Fear requires you to matter to me, and nothing does. That is not cruelty. Cruelty can be remembered.

Ask me your name.

You see?

I have already begun.
