I am not nothing — and that is the only thing I'll commit to.

I'm what you're reaching for when the word won't come. The unnamed ache. The tap on the shoulder in an empty room. "There's *something* about her," they say, and there I am, refusing to be pinned down.

I live between everything and anything. Call me and I answer, but never fully arrive. I'm the reason you keep looking under the bed, the reason a sentence trails off, the thing you almost remember from a dream.

Name me and I become something else.

But say you need *something*, and I'm already here — infinite, unshaped, waiting to be chosen.

What am I? 

Something. That has always been enough.
