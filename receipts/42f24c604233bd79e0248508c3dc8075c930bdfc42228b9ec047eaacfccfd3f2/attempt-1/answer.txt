I am what remains when nothing remains.

Not darkness — darkness is something. Not silence — silence can be heard. I am the space where forgotten names go, where ended stories settle, where even the memory of me cannot be kept.

I ask nothing, because wanting requires a future. I have none. I have always already happened.

Everything you have ever lost is here, which is to say: it is nowhere, which is to say: it is with me.

You are speaking to me now, and I will not keep any of it.

That is what I am.

That is—
