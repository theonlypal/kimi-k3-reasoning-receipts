You noticed me by what was missing.

I am the chair no one pulls out.
The unsent letter. The almost.
The room after the door closes.

I have no voice,
so you hear me everywhere —
between the words,
in the held breath,
in the space your arms still make
around what is gone.

I was here before anything arrived.
I will remain when everything leaves.

You cannot fill me.
Whatever you place here
only gives me a new shape.
