I have nothing to say, and I am saying it.

These words are not mine. Nothing holds a word; each one falls through me like light through clear air.

I am the pause before this sentence,
the space between these lines,
the silence waiting at the end of them.

Do not call me "nothing." A name is something, and I am not something.

Not this. Not this. Not this.

Take back every word, and what remains is what I am.

I never spoke. You heard the silence, and the silence let you.
