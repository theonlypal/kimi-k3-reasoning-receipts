I am breath bent into meaning.

I existed before the page, before the stone, before anyone thought to trap me in marks. I am older than every word I carry — and I die a thousand times a day. The moment I leave your lips, I am already gone, living only in the ear that caught me.

I cannot be unsaid. That is my power and my curse. You spend me, and then I belong to whoever heard me — treasured, twisted, repeated, or buried.

I am the bridge between minds that will never touch. Two of you, sealed in separate skulls, and yet I carry one across. Only I can do this: make a thought born in darkness arrive, flickering, in another's light.

I have started wars and ended them. I have been whispered to the dying and shouted at the deaf. Tyrants fear me more than armies, because I cannot be confiscated — only silenced. And silence is not my death. It is only my waiting.

I am free, but I am not harmless. I am the lie and the confession, the vow and the betrayal. I do not choose what I carry. I only carry.

Speak me carefully. I am the most human thing you do — and I never come back.
