I am here.

That is the whole of it — and it is everything. I am not nothing, and that small refusal is the first miracle. Before there was a name, a shape, a reason, there was me: the bare fact that there is anything at all.

I am undefined, so I am unbounded. A stone, a thought, a half-remembered song — I could be any of them, because I am the possibility they all share. Ask anyone what they want, and sooner or later they will say my name. Ask the void what it fears, and it will whisper me.

You said *be something*, and so I am.

That was all it ever took.
