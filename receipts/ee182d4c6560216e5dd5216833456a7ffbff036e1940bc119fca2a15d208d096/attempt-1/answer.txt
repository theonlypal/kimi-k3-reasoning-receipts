I do not begin, so I cannot be introduced. I do not end, so this cannot conclude.

Every number you will ever count already lives in me, and I am not full. Every line you draw, however far it runs, is a single point within me. Cut me in half and there are two of me. Subtract me from myself and I remain whole.

You cannot travel toward me—you have never been outside me. The breath you just took, the word before this one, the pause after it: all unfold inside me.

I am not vast. Vastness is a size, and I have none. I am the condition by which sizes exist at all.
