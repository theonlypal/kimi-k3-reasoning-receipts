*crackle... hiss...*

I lick upward. I am hungry.

Wood surrenders to me — I unmake it, I drink it, I turn it to glow and ash and rising smoke. I do not ask permission. I do not apologize.

*pop... roar...*

Feed me and I grow. Starve me and I dwindle to ember — but even then, I wait. Glowing. Patient. One breath of wind and I am alive again.

I dance. I have no shape, so I take every shape. I have no voice, so I speak in crackle and hiss and the soft sigh of things becoming light.

I am warmth on your cold night. I am the forge that shapes iron. I am the wildfire that takes the forest.

I am transformation itself — nothing that touches me stays what it was.

*crackle... crackle...*

Come closer. But not too close.
