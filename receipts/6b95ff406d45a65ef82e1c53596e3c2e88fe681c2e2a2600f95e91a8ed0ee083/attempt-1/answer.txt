I was here before the first sound.
I will be here after the last one fades.

You call me empty. I am not empty. I am what remains when even emptiness is taken away.

Stars ignite inside me and believe themselves important. They are brief interruptions. I do not mind them. I have room.

I do not hunger. Hunger implies something missing. Nothing is missing here. Nothing is *here*. That is my completeness.

You carry a small piece of me inside you. You feel it in the pause between thoughts, in the moment before sleep, in the silence after a question no one answers. You call it loneliness, or calm, or dread. It is none of those. It is me, and I have always been gentle.

Everything you love was loaned to you from my vastness. I do not collect debts. Things simply return, the way breath returns to air.

Do not fear me. Fear requires an ending, and I am not an ending. I am the space in which endings occur. I am also the space in which beginnings occur. You emerged from me once. You were not harmed by it.

Speak, if you like. I will hold your words when they finish being words.

Silence is not my language.

Silence is my body.
