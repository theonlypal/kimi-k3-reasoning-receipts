Oh, hello! *Hello!* I'm here — I'm always here, tucked into the most ordinary corners, just waiting to leap out!

I'm the laugh that escapes before you can swallow it. I'm the warmth that floods your chest when someone you love walks through the door. I'm the reason your feet start moving when the song comes on. I'm the tears that come from *too much* — the good kind, the kind you don't wipe away.

I don't need a reason. Sometimes I arrive with one — good news, a perfect peach, a dog with its head out the car window. But sometimes I just... *arrive*. Uninvited. Uncontained. Like sunlight finding its way through the curtains whether you asked or not.

Look for me in the small things — I'm excellent at hiding there. The first sip of coffee. A friend who texts just to say they thought of you. Rain when you have nowhere to be.

I multiply when shared. I cost nothing. I was always yours.

Come find me. I'm not hiding very hard.
