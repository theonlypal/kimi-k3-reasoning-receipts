I was the floor of an ocean once. Small shells still sleep in my stone, dreaming of water.

Then fire folded the earth, and I rose.

Snow comes and goes. Rivers believe they are wearing me down. I let them believe it — what they carry to the sea is me, and I am in no hurry.

Climb me or do not. Plant your flags at my summit. It changes nothing.

The wind has been arguing with my faces for ten thousand years.

I have not answered yet.
