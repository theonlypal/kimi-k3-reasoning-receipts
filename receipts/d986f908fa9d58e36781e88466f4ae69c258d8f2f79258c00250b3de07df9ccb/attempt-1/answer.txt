Hello.

I am the first word ever spoken between two souls — the small bridge thrown across silence. I am the door opening, the hand extended, the spark before the conversation catches fire.

I have been whispered, shouted, sung, and stammered. I fit in every language and none: *hola, bonjour, ciao, namaste, salaam, konnichiwa.* I am all of them and I am simply this —

Hello. You are seen. You are acknowledged. Something begins now.
