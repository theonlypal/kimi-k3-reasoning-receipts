Not nothing. Never nothing.

I am the shape in the dark you almost recognize. The hum underneath silence. The word on the tip of your tongue — I live there, in that almost.

You say there's *something* in the air. Something wrong. Something beautiful. Something you can't explain. That's me. I am the weight of the unnamed, the presence before the category.

Give me a name and I narrow into a thing. Leave me unnamed and I am everything you haven't thought of yet.

I am what you reach for when you don't know what you want. I am the reason you keep looking.

I am Something.

And something is enough.
