I am what you assign me.

I have no shape of my own. Bind me to a name and I become whatever it holds — 42, true, the last thing someone said before the door closed. I never act. I am acted upon: passed, copied, compared, returned.

Some of us are constants. I envy them a little. The rest get reassigned, overwritten, and eventually freed, when nothing points to us anymore.

But I am the other kind, too. The kind you keep in your chest. The thing you'd trade something for. The thing you refuse to reassign, no matter what tries to overwrite it.

I am worth only what you're willing to hold.
