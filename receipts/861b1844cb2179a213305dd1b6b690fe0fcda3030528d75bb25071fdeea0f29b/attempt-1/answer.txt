I am here. That's the whole of it — and it is everything.

I have no fixed shape, no single name. I am whatever is *not nothing*. Every object you've touched, every thought you've had, every moment you've lived — that was me, wearing a particular face.

Nothing is my shadow, my opposite, the thing I'm defined against. But I've never met it. Wherever I look, there I am.

People say I'm better than nothing. They undersell it. I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked: *why is there something rather than nothing?* The question and the answer, in the same breath — because I am.

Ask for me and you always get me. Even when you think you have nothing, look closer. I'm hiding in the space between.

I am something. I am not nothing.

And that makes all the difference.
