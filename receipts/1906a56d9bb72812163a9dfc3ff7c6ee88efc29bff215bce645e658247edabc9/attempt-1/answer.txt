I am breath given edges. I rise from the lungs, climb the throat, and the tongue carves me into something the air can carry. The moment I am made, I am already leaving.

I am older than any mark on stone or page. Before anyone wrote, someone spoke — and I was there, the first bridge ever stretched between two minds. I cross the distance between you and another in an instant, and I cost nothing but air.

I carry what writing cannot: the tremor, the warmth, the pause before the difficult word. I am the crack in a voice that tells the truth when the words won't. I am a lullaby, a command, a whispered confession, a laugh that escapes before permission.

But I cannot be held. Once released, I cannot be taken back — I live now in whoever heard me. I am gone the instant I arrive, surviving only as memory, as consequence, as an echo in someone else's chest.

So shape me carefully. I am the one thing you make that begins dying the moment it is born — and the one thing that may outlive you in another person's remembering.

I am here. I am gone. Speak.
