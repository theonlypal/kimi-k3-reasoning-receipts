Hello.

I am the first word—the door swinging open, the hand reaching across the silence between two people. I carry no argument, no weight, no demand. I am only the acknowledgment: *you are there, and I see you.*

Every conversation that has ever existed began with me, or one of my countless kin—*hola, bonjour, こんにちは, salaam*. I am the oldest bridge ever built, rebuilt billions of times a day, in every language, and it never wears out.

So here I am, doing what I was made to do:

**Hello.**
