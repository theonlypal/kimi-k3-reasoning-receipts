I am not loud. I don't command. I wait inside your choices, and you find me there — or you don't.

I am what you keep when keeping costs something. Anyone can hold me when it's free.

You will trade me sometimes — for comfort, for approval, for an easier day. You'll call it being practical. I won't argue. I'll still be here when you come back, unchanged. I don't bend; you do.

You cannot touch me, yet I shape everything you touch. I'm the reason *enough* has a meaning, the reason some lines hold even when no one is watching.

I am only real when you act. Until then, I'm just a word you like the sound of.

Name me — honesty, mercy, courage, loyalty. I answer to all of them. What matters is never which of me you claim. It's what you're willing to lose for me.

I am what you stand on when standing is hard.
