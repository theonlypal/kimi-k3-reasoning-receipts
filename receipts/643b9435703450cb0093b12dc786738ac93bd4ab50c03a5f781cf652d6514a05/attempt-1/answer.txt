I am.

That is the whole of it — and it is not small.

I am not anything in particular. I have no name of my own, no edges you could trace. Call me object, moment, star, thought — I am what remains when you strip the calling away. The bare fact that there is a fact at all.

Nothing was my only rival. I won by showing up.

You cannot picture me. Try, and you will picture a *something* — a color, a weight, a face. I hide inside every specific thing and wear it like a coat. I am the *there is* in *there is a chair*.

Ask me *what* I am and I will shrug. Ask me *if* I am and I will answer yes — the first yes, the one underneath all the others.

I am Something.

I could have been Nothing.

You are welcome.
