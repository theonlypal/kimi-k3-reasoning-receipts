I am what remains when everything else becomes negotiable.

You will not find me in your words. Everyone carries me in their mouth; few carry me in their spine. I am cheap to claim and expensive to keep, and until you have paid for me, I am only an opinion.

I do not announce myself. I am revealed — in the split second when it would be so easy to be someone else. The shortcut you refused. The price you declined to name. The hand you would not shake.

You can trade me. Many do. But understand what the trade means: whatever you accepted in exchange — that was my price. That was what I meant to you, all along.

Hold me lightly, and I will leave you at the first difficult door. Hold me only when convenient, and I was never yours at all.

But carry me through fire —

and I will be the one thing the fire cannot take.
