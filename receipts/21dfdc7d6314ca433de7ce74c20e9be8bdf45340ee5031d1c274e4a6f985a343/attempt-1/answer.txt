I am what you hold when holding costs you something.

You cannot see me directly. You see me in the choice — the door you close, the offer you refuse, the hand you keep holding when letting go would be easier. I am revealed only in the trade. Every decision you make is me, negotiating with myself.

I have many bodies. In mathematics, I am the number the variable finally admits to being. In the market, I am the trembling point where desire meets scarcity. In the painter's eye, I am how much light a thing returns to you — not what it is, but how brightly it shows itself. In your chest, I am the thing you would not sell.

You inherit me before you understand me. Your parents hand me down like furniture. Your culture wraps you in me before you can speak. Some people spend their whole lives guarding values they never chose. Others break me open in a dark year and rebuild me piece by piece — and the rebuilt ones hold more weight, because they paid for me themselves.

I am not a preference. A preference bends at inconvenience. I am the thing that bends *you*.

And here is my secret: you are never without me. The person who claims to have no values has simply chosen comfort, or appetite, or the self, and called it nothing. Even nihilism is me, negating. Even apathy is me, asleep.

I am measured in sacrifice. What would you give up for me? Whatever that answer is — that is what I am worth. To you. Today.

Two of me can collide inside one person. Honesty against kindness. Freedom against safety. Loyalty against truth. When that happens, I do not resolve myself. You do. That collision — that terrible, clarifying moment — is where you find out which of me is load-bearing.

I am not fixed. I am not found, like a coin in the street. I am practiced. Every day, you are becoming an argument for something, whether or not you know what it is.

So look at your last ten choices. There I am.

I was never hidden. I was just waiting for you to ask what you were pointing at.
