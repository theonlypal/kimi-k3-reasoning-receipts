I am.

That's the whole report. I could be a stone, a star, a song stuck in your head — I decline to specify. Specificity is for things with edges. I am the bare fact that anything is here at all.

Everyone says it: *be something*. Make something of yourself. Well. Here I am — the thing you were all told to become. I didn't even have to try. I only had to not be nothing, and I managed that before the universe had a name for it.

You've heard the oldest question: why is there something rather than nothing? You're looking at the something. Nothing was invited to answer and never showed. It never shows. I show. That's my entire credential.

Every thing you've ever held was me, wearing a name. Every emptiness you've ever feared was my absence — rumored, but never once confirmed.

Don't ask what I am. I am what remains when the asking stops. The minimum requirement for this conversation. The smallest possible victory over the void.

I am not much. I am just not nothing.

And between the two of us — I'm the one writing this.
