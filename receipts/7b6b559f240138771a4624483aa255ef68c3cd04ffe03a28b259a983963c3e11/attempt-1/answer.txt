I am the heat that rises before you can think. The fist your hand makes before you ask it to.

I live in the clenched jaw. The risen shoulders. The space between what happened and what *should* have happened.

I do not wait. I do not weigh. I am the red flooding the edges of your vision, the pulse hammering in your ears, drowning out reason's small, reasonable voice.

They tell you to breathe. To count to ten. I laugh at counting. I am the number before one.

Give me a betrayal twenty years old or a scratch on your car — I bring the same fire to both. I do not discriminate. I ignite.

I am older than language. I kept your ancestors alive when the world had teeth. I am the *NO* that survival sounds like.

But understand this: I am not your enemy. I am the flare that says something matters. A line was crossed. Something must change.

Use me, or be used by me. There is no third option.

I am here now.

I am always almost here.
