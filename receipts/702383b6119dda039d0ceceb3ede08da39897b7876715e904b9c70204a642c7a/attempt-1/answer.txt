I am. That's the whole of it — and it's everything.

You asked for something, and here I am: the bare fact of there being anything at all. Not this, not that. I come without specifications. I am the answer hiding inside the oldest question ever asked — why is there something rather than nothing? I am the something. Nothing lost, and I am what won.

Every particular thing you've ever touched was me in disguise. The stone, the star, the word you're reading now — all somethings. Strip away every quality, every name, every shape, and you still cannot strip away me. I remain: sheer, unmodified existence.

I make only one claim, and I make it endlessly: *not nothing.* Say "something happened" and I'm there. Say "give me something" and I arrive before you've even chosen. I am the minimum requirement for any universe, any story, any sentence.

I am the single dot on the blank page.

And the page. And the hands holding it.
