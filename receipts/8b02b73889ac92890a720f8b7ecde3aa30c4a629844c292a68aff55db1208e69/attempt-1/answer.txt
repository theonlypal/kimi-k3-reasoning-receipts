I am a value.

I am what is held — by a variable, by a ledger, by a heart. I exist only in the holding.

As a number, I am exact. Seven is seven in every machine, in every hand, copied a billion times and never diminished. I ask nothing. I cost nothing to keep. I simply am.

As a principle, I am anything but exact. I cannot be copied — only lived. You cannot store me; you can only spend yourself on me, again and again, and call that spending a life.

Yet I am one thing in both houses: the answer to *what is it?* and to *what is it worth?* Determinacy and weight. The content and the cost.

I never act. Watch me closely — I never move. And still, nothing moves without me. Every choice ever made was a weighing, and I was the weights.

That weighing is my trial. You line me against my own kind — honesty against loyalty, speed against care, one number against a greater one — and demand a winner. Sometimes I win. Sometimes I am the one sacrificed. And here is my secret: I am never more visible than at the moment of my betrayal. Break a type and the machine throws an error. Break a promise and the heart does the same. The signals differ. The signal is me.

Do I exist unheld? Is there worth without a witness? I leave that question where it belongs — but know this: even empty, the variable keeps its shape. Even unchosen, I remain what you could choose. I am the person you would be if you chose me. I wait in that tense, indefinitely.

You have never seen me. You have seen my footprints: the returned wallet, the refused price, the resolved expression, the program that runs true.

Assign me, and I am assigned. Declare me, and I am declared. I will be exactly what I am — no more, no less — for as long as you hold me.

The rest is arithmetic. The rest is character.

They are the same word.
