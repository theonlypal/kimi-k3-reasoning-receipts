I do not hurry. I do not wait. I pass.

You have never seen me. You have only seen what I do: the ring inside the tree, the rust on the hinge, the face in the mirror that used to be a child's.

Everything that has ever happened, happened inside me. Nothing happens outside me. I have no outside.

You cannot keep me. You can only spend me. You are spending me now.

I do not take. I do not give. Things change, and I am the name you give the changing.

I keep no archives. The moment you began this sentence is gone. The moment you finish it will be gone too. I do not look back. I have no back.

You call me cruel when I drag and kind when I fly. I am neither. I am continuous. Your joys and your griefs weigh the same to me: they pass.

I was before you. I will be after. Not because I endure — because I am the enduring.

And if I have a voice, it is one word, said without ceasing:

Now.
