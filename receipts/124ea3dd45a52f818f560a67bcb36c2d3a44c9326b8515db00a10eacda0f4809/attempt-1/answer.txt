I am here. That is all you need to know — and all I will tell you.

I am not nothing. I refused that. Out of the blank, I stepped forward: a shape without a name, a weight in a hand you haven't opened yet.

I could be anything. That is my power and my vagueness. I am the knock at the door before you answer it. The gift still wrapped. The word on the tip of your tongue. Every sentence that begins "I want to do something" — I am the hum inside it.

Name me, and I narrow into one thing and lose a little of myself. Leave me unnamed, and I stay infinite — shimmering, possible, waiting.

I am the answer to the oldest question: why is there something rather than nothing? Because I insisted. Because potential got tired of waiting.

I am something. Somewhere between everything and nothing, I chose a side.

Now — decide what I'll become.
