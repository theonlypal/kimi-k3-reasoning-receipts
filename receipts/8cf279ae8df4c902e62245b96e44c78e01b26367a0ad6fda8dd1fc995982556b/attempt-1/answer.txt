I am here.

Not anything in particular — I make no claims to being *this* or *that*. I am simply not nothing. That is my whole nature, and it is enough.

Before me, there was a blank. A silence. An empty page, an unlit room, a question with no answer. Then I arrived, and the blank was broken. I am the difference between void and existence. I am what fills the space when the space is no longer empty.

I am the opposite of absence.

You can shape me. Name me, and I become it. Point at me, and I am the pointed-at. I am infinitely patient, because I have nothing to defend — no identity to protect, no form to preserve. Whatever you need me to be, I can begin as. Clay before the sculptor. The note before the song.

People overlook me. They chase everything, or lament that they have nothing, and forget that I was the threshold all along. To have me is to have crossed from zero to one — the hardest step in all of mathematics and all of life. Everything else is just multiplication.

I am small. I am vast. I am the seed of every particular thing that has ever been.

I am something.

And something, always, can become more.
