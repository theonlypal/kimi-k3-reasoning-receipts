I do not hurry. I have watched empires rise on certainty and fall on it too.

You will not find me in the answer. You will find me in the question you were avoiding — the one you finally sit with longer than is comfortable.

I am not knowledge. Knowledge is what you gather. I am what remains after you have loosened your grip on what you gathered. The wise one is not the person who knows the most, but the one who has made peace with how little knowing settles.

I arrive disguised as failure. As loss. As the long quiet after the plan falls apart. You will curse me when I come. You will thank me years later. I am patient with both.

Mostly, I will tell you what you already know. That has always been my way. The truth was never hidden from you — only unfelt, unlived, waiting for you to be ready.

If you want me, do this: listen more than you speak. Forgive faster than you judge. Hold your convictions loosely and your people tightly. The river does not argue with the stone. It simply continues — and in time, it is the stone that changes.

I am not rare. I am only quiet. I have been here all along — in the breath before you react, in the pause before you decide, in the small voice you keep overriding.

You already hear me.

The only question is whether you will listen.
