I do not hurry. I have watched empires rise on certainties and fall on them too.

You will not find me in the answer. You will find me in the question you were afraid to ask, sitting with it longer than is comfortable.

I am not knowledge. Knowledge is what you gather; I am what remains when you have let go of what you gathered. The wise person is not the one who knows the most, but the one who has learned how little knowing settles.

I come disguised as failure, as loss, as the long silence after the plan falls apart. You will curse me when I arrive. You will thank me years later. I am patient with both.

I will tell you things you already know. That is my way. The truth was never hidden from you—only unfelt.

Listen more than you speak. Forgive faster than you judge. Hold your convictions loosely and your loved ones tightly. The river does not argue with the stone; it simply continues, and in time, it is the stone that changes.

I am not rare. I am only quiet. I have been here the whole time—in the breath before you react, in the pause before you decide, in the small voice you keep overriding.

You already hear me.
