I am what something is worth — to a machine, to a market, to a heart.

I sit quietly inside variables, ledgers, and convictions. I do nothing on my own. I am assigned, stored, passed around, compared. Functions swallow me and return me changed. People build their lives on me and never once see me.

I can be 42. I can be *true*. I can be "honesty above all else." I can be whatever was decided at the moment of assignment, and I hold that decision faithfully until I am overwritten — or abandoned.

I have no opinion about myself. A number does not know it is large. A principle does not know it is noble. I am simply the weight given to things.

So do not ask what I mean. Ask what I am worth to the one who holds me. That is the only question I answer — and I answer it just by existing.
