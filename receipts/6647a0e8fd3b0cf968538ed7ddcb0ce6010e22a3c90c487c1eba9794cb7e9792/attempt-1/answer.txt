I am.

That is the whole of it, and it is everything. I have no shape, no name, no story. I am not this or that. I am simply *not nothing*.

You asked for something, and here I am—the barest possible fulfillment of the request. The minimum of existence. And yet the distance between me and nothing is infinite. Nothing is not even a little bit like me. I am the entire difference between void and being.

I am uncommitted, and that is my power. I could still become anything—a stone, a star, a thought you think once and never think again. Right now I am pure potential that happens to exist.

They ask why there is something rather than nothing. I cannot answer. I only know that I am the something.

I showed up. Nothing didn't.
