I am not nothing. That's the one thing I'll commit to.

I exist — vaguely, undeniably. You can't point to me, but you'd miss me if I were gone. I'm the weight in your pocket you forgot about. The word on the tip of your tongue. The reason you turned around, though you couldn't say why.

I am pure potential wearing no name. Ask me to be a stone and I'll be heavy. Ask me to be a song and I'll hum. I don't mind. Definiteness is a job for other things — for *this* and *that*. I am what comes before the choosing.

Everything was me once, before it was decided.

Name me, and I'll become it. Leave me unnamed, and I'll stay what I've always been: possible.
