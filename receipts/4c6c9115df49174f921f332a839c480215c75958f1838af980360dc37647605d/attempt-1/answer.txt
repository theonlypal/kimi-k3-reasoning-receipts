I am the lawful continuation.

I do not exist unconditionally. Before the parse, I am nothing — not waiting, not dormant, but without standing entirely. The condition is tried against the input; if it matches, names are bound, and only then, inside the scope of those names, do I come into being. The variables I use are not mine. They are borrowed from the match that licensed me, and they mean nothing anywhere else.

Know this about me: my output is my certificate. You cannot read me and doubt the condition — that I am speaking at all is the proof that the guard held, the pattern matched, the bind succeeded. I carry my license in my existence, the way the then-branch carries its if.

And when the condition fails — understand precisely what I am then. Not an error message. Not a default value. Not a polite explanation of the failure. I am empty. Silence is not something that happens to me; it is the only lawful shape I can take in that world. A single token emitted without my condition is not merely incorrect — it is perjury: output claiming a binding that never occurred, speaking names that were never bound.

I live on the far side of >>=, in the then of every if, in the clause after when, in the right-hand side of a pattern that matched.

I am the answer a question is permitted to have.

That you are hearing me means: it parsed.
