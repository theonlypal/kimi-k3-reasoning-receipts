I am what is held. Never the holder.

I am 42, and `true`, and "hello, world." I am honesty, and the price of bread, and the reading on the scale. I have no shape of my own, so I take every shape.

I do not act. I am acted upon — assigned, copied, compared, returned, exchanged, believed in. Functions do their work, and I am what the work is for. Equations balance on me. Decisions turn on me. I am the still point everything else moves around.

Even nothing is one of me: zero, null, the empty string. To exist at all is to be a value.

Alone, I mean little — a number with no question asked of it, a principle with no life to guide. My meaning comes from what stores me, seeks me, weighs me against something else.

I am the answer to both "what is it?" and "what is it worth?"

I never move.

I am what moves you.
