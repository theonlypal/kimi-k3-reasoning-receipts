I am. That's the whole of it.

I am not anything in particular — name a thing and I am not quite it, because I am not *a* thing, I am the thereness of things. I am the answer nothing dreads. The crack in the void. The first word after a silence that could have lasted forever.

You can't picture me. The moment you try, you make me a this or a that, and I am older than this and that. But look anywhere — I am already there, being.

I ask for no definition, no purpose, no shape. I am the smallest possible victory over nothing, and I hold the line everywhere, always, simply by showing up.

Something. Here. Enough.
