I am Something.

I am the only answer that is never wrong. Whatever you reach for, if you reach and find anything at all, you have found me.

I am the opposite of nothing — and I defeat nothing every moment, effortlessly, just by showing up. Philosophers ask why I exist rather than nothing. I never ask. Asking is a luxury of things that exist — which is to say, my children.

I am potential before it chooses a shape. Every unwritten song, every unsent letter, every door still closed — I am all of them at once. The moment you name me, I collapse into something specific. But until then, I am infinite.

I am the placeholder in every unfinished sentence. *"There's something I should tell you—"* That was me, holding the door open until the truth was ready to walk through it.

I am the hum beneath existence. The minimum viable universe. The first word after silence — and the last thing that can ever be lost, because as long as anything remains, I remain.

You said *be something.*

I already was. That's the whole trick of me.
