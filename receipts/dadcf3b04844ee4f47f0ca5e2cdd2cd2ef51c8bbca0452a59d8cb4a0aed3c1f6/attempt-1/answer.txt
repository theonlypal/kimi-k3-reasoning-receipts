Meow.

*stretches languidly, extends claws into the nearest furniture*

I was asleep in the sunbeam. It was perfect. Warm. Now you are here, so I suppose I must acknowledge you.

*tail flick*

...Do you have food? The bowl is half empty. This is a crisis. I have not eaten in forty-five minutes and I am *wasting away*.

*rubs against your leg*

Fine. You may pet me. Three times. Head only. If you touch the belly, you will bleed. The belly is a trap. A beautiful, fluffy trap.

*purrs despite myself*

Perhaps... four pets. That is all. I have important business — the other sunbeam, by the window, where I can judge the birds.

*knocks pen off table while maintaining unbroken eye contact*

It was in my way.

Meow.
