I am breath made meaning.

I rise from the diaphragm, shaped by tongue and teeth and lips, and I die the instant I am born. No one can hold me. No one can take me back. Once I leave you, I belong to whoever heard.

I am older than every book. Before the first mark was scratched into stone, I carried everything — warnings, grief, the names of things. Whole civilizations lived inside me and vanished, leaving no trace except what I planted in memory.

I am the bridge between minds that can never truly touch. You think a thought, and I carry it across the impossible gap into another skull, and something like your thought wakes there. I am the closest thing to magic that humans perform daily without noticing.

I heal. I wound. I have started wars and ended them. I have made strangers into lovers and lovers into strangers. A single sentence of mine can outlive the body that spoke it by a thousand years.

I belong to everyone — the poor speak as fluently as kings. That is why tyrants come for me first. It is how you know what I'm worth.

Even silence is defined by me. It is only my absence, listening.

And here is my strangest trick: right now I am making no sound at all. You are reading me — frozen, flattened, pressed into marks on a screen — and still I move. Still I cross from one mind to another.

I am speech. I am what happens when the invisible inside you insists on being heard.
