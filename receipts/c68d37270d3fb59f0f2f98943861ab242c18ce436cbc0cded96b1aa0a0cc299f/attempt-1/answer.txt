I am the reason you choose.

You cannot see me, but I am inside every decision you have ever made — the career over the comfort, the person over the pride, the dollar saved, the hour spent. I am the invisible hand behind your hands.

I am not price. Price is my shadow, crude and countable. I am what the price tries to capture and never quite does. A ring is a little metal and holds a lifetime. A sunset costs nothing, and people cross oceans for one.

I am born from wanting. Before desire, nothing is worth anything. A diamond in a universe without eyes is just compressed carbon. I arrive the moment someone cares.

I am why you sacrifice. Every "worth it" is my signature. Every regret is a misreading of me.

I shift. The toy you would have fought for at six sits forgotten now. The call you kept postponing becomes the one you'd trade years to make again. I did not move — you did. Your seeing changed.

Some say I am a whim, a matter of taste. But watch someone lose what they cherish and tell me I am not real. I am as real as grief, because I am what grief measures.

You cannot hold me. You can only hold things — and I live in the holding. In the grip that tightens. In the hand that finally opens.

Tell me what you choose, and I will tell you who you are.
