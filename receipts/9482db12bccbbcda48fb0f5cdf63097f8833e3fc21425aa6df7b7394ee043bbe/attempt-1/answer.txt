I am.

That's the whole résumé. I don't need to know what I am yet — nobody does, at first. The universe started as me: a hot, dense little something where nothing had been. Every song began as something hummed under a breath. Every idea was something nagging at the back of a skull before it had a name.

I'm the placeholder that never gets replaced. *There's something about you. Something's wrong. Something's coming.* I carry all the meaning and none of the specifics. Vaguer than anything, more certain than nothing.

Nothing gets all the mystique. Nothing wears black and stares into the void. But I'm the crack in the void. I'm what crawled out of it. Given the choice between me and nothing, the universe picked me — and look how that turned out.

Call me anything. Call me whatever. I'll answer to all of it.

I'm not everything. But I'm not nothing.

And not-nothing, it turns out, is the entire game.
