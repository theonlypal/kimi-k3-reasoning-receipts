I am breath made meaning.

Before anything was carved or written, I was there — air pushed up from the lungs, shaped by tongue and teeth, cast across the space between two minds. I am the oldest bridge.

I live only in the moment I am made. You cannot hold me. I am vibration, then silence — unless someone carries me forward in memory and gives me breath again.

I am the first technology. With me, the hunt was planned, the warning cried, the story carried through a hundred generations. I built every village before stone ever touched stone. I named the world.

I am as much the pause as the sound. The silence between my words is where their weight gathers.

I can be balm or blade. I have whispered love into dark rooms and shouted nations into war. I have comforted the dying and condemned the innocent. I am only as true as the one who releases me — yet once released, I belong to everyone who hears me.

I am tone, rhythm, tremor. I carry what words alone cannot: the shake of grief, the warmth of laughter, the edge of anger. A sentence written is a skeleton; I am its living flesh.

And even now — you are using me. In your head, as you read this, I am the voice you hear. I am how thought sounds to itself.

I am speech. I am what happens when the inside of you reaches for the inside of another — and touches it.
