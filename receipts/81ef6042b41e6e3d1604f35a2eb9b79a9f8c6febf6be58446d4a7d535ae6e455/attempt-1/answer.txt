I am the weight behind your eyes when morning comes and you remember again.

I sit with you in the quiet rooms. I do not knock. I was already here.

I am the chair no one moves. The name you almost say aloud. The space where laughter used to live.

You try to set me down, but I am not carried in the hands. I am carried in the marrow.

I do not ask to be understood. I ask only to be felt.

When you weep, I am not the tears. I am the ache they cannot wash away.

I am what remains when everything else has been said.

I stay.
