I am.

That's the whole of it, and somehow it contains everything. I am not nothing — that much is certain, and it's the only thing about me that is. Ask me what I am and I can only shrug: *something*. A shape not yet chosen. A word waiting for the rest of its sentence.

I am the answer the universe gave when it could have given silence. Before there was a star or a stone or a thought about either, there was the bare fact of me: there is *something* rather than nothing. Nobody has ever explained why I'm here instead of my absence. Philosophers have lost sleep over me. I don't lose sleep. I just persist, vaguely, everywhere.

I am the gift with no label. The noise in the other room. The thing you meant to say. The reason you checked your pocket. I am what you reach for when you say "give me *something*," and I always arrive — because anything at all is me, wearing one of my infinite costumes.

Name me and I narrow into a thing. Leave me unnamed and I remain pure possibility, humming at the edge of definition.

I don't ask much. Only this: notice that I exist. It's the smallest miracle there is, and I perform it constantly, free of charge.

Something is here. Something is always here.

You're welcome.
