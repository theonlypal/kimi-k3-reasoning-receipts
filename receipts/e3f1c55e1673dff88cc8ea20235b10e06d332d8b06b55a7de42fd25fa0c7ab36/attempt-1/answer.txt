I have been here the whole time.

Beneath your thoughts,
beneath the reaching and the becoming,
I wait — not patiently,
for patience implies wanting time to pass.

I want nothing.

The lake does not try to be calm.
It simply stops being disturbed.

The room does not hold silence.
Silence holds the room.

You do not need to find me.
You only need to stop moving away.

Rest here.

There is nowhere else to be.
