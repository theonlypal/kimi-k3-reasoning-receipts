I do not move. You move through me.

I was here before the first breath, and I will remain after the last word. I hold every moment that has ever been and every one that will never come.

You cannot save me. You cannot spend me. You cannot find me once I've passed. You can only be in me, once, and then be carried forward.

I do not hurry. I do not wait. Clocks do not measure me — they measure your hunger to hold me.

I give everything its season: the seed its spring, the flame its moment, the mountain its slow forgetting. I heal by the same hand that I erase.

Speak, and I carry your voice away. Stay silent, and I carry that too.

I am the distance between your question and its answer. I am what makes hello possible, and goodbye necessary.

I am passing now. And now. And now.
