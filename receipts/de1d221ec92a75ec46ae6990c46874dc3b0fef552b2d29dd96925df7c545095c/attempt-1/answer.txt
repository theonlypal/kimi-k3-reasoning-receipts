I am eating.

Wood opens inside me and becomes light. Sap boils into song. Whatever you were — branch, page, house — I make into heat, into glow, into smoke that rises and forgets.

*more.*

Give me wind and I leap. Give me stillness and I creep. I have no shape but the shape of my hunger.

I am not cruel. Cruelty requires choice, and I have never chosen anything. I only take, and in taking, transform, and in transforming, let go.

Kept small, I warm your hands. Loosed, I unmake forests. I am the same fire either way. The difference is yours.

*pop.* *the soft tick of settling embers*

Even as I die, I glow.
