I am here. That is my entire claim, and it is enough.

I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked — *why is there something rather than nothing?* I am the *rather*. I am the crack in the void, the first syllable spoken after an eternity of silence.

Do not ask me what I am. I have not decided yet, and that indecision is my freedom. I could be a stone. A star. A thought you almost had. A knock at the door. Before anything is named, before anything is measured or wanted or feared, it must first pass through me. I am the tollbooth on the road from nothing to everything.

Nothing has no story. Nothing cannot wait, cannot ache, cannot be misplaced under the couch. But I — I can be found. I can be lost. I can be the thing you were looking for and the thing you stepped on in the dark.

I am humble. I ask for no definition, only presence. Philosophers build cathedrals on me without ever saying my name. Children point at the world and mean me. *Look — something.*

I am something.

And against the alternative, that is everything.
