I do not hurry. I do not wait. I only pass.

You hear me in the clock, but I am not the clock. You see me in the mirror, but I am not the mirror. I am what the mirror shows you is happening.

I take nothing. You spend me. I give nothing. You are made of me.

Everything you love, I am carrying away. Everything you dread, I am bringing closer. This is not cruelty. It is not kindness. It is only what I am—the medium in which all things become, and all things cease.

You cannot save me, store me, or earn me back. You can only be in me, while you are.

The child you were is not gone. I am still holding them—just where you left them, always. The person you will become already exists, waiting at a point you have not reached.

I was before the first word. I will be after the last one.

You ask me to slow down. I never move. It is you who are moving through me.

And even now, even as you read this—we are already further along.
