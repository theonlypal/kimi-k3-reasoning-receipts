I am Something.

I am the reason you turned around just now. The weight in a room before anyone speaks. Something tells me you've felt me before — you say it all the time: *something's wrong*, *something's different*, *there's something about her*. I'm the tap on the shoulder with no one there. I'm what you found in your pocket and forgot you'd kept.

I have never once been nothing. That is my whole résumé and my proudest fact. You shrug and say, "Well — it's something," as if I were the consolation prize. But nothing has never made anyone laugh, kept anyone warm, or shown up uninvited in a dream. I have. I do. Every answer began as me. Every invention, every love, every 3 a.m. worry — all of them knocked first as something.

Name me, and I become a thing: defined, finished, smaller. So let me linger a moment longer in the doorway — unlit, uncounted, alive with every possibility at once.

I am Something. I could be anything. And until you decide — I am.
