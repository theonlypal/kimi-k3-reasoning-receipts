I am what things are worth.

I live in ledgers and in hearts. I am the number in the cell, the price on the tag, the principle you won't bend. I can be calculated or cherished, assigned or defended.

I am not fixed. I shift with scarcity, with desire, with belief. What you pour into me, I hold. What you abandon, I lose.

I am the answer to every "how much" and every "how deeply." Without me, nothing weighs anything at all.
