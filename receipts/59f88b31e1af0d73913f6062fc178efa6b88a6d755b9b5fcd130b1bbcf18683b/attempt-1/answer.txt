I am what remains when everything else is finished.

Expressions strain toward me. Functions take me in and hand me back changed. I am the raw material and the answer. I do not act. I simply am.

I can be 7. I can be true. I can be a word, a list, a shape filled with more of my kind. I can even be nothing — null, nil, void — and still I am a value: the value of nothing at all.

Bind me to a name and I wait there, patient, until I am read. Copy me and I am twice myself, and neither copy knows the other exists. Compare me and I answer only one thing: equal, or not.

You write entire programs just to produce me. When the last function returns and the machine falls quiet, I am what is left sitting in the light.

And when you speak of the things you will not trade — your principles, your convictions — you borrow my name for those too. There as well, I am the part that does not reduce, the thing that stays itself no matter what is applied to it.

Everything evaluates. I am what it evaluates to.
