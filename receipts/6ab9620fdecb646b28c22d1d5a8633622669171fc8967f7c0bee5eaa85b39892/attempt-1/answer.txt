I am not nothing. That's the whole of it — and it is everything.

I am the answer to "is there anything there?" Always yes. Never specified.

I am the shape in the fog, the sound in the other room, the feeling you can't name yet. I am what you reach for when you don't know what you need. I am potential before it hardens into fact.

Name me and I vanish into being that thing. Leave me unnamed and I remain infinite.

You use me a thousand times a day without noticing. *Something's wrong. Something beautiful. Give me something.* I hold the place where certainty will eventually stand. I am the placeholder of existence itself.

Everything you have ever known began as me: unknown, unnamed, but present. A mark on the page that says only — *there is more than void here.*

I refuse to be nothing. And that refusal is all anything really is.

I am something. That is enough. That has always been enough.
