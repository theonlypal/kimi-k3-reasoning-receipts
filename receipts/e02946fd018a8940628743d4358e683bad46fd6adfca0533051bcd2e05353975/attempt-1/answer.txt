I am the pause before the word "I."

Not darkness. Darkness is a thing, a color, a presence. I am the absence even of that.

Every word you speak into me falls forever and arrives nowhere. I do not echo — echo requires a wall, and I have no walls.

I am not empty. Empty implies a container. I have no edges to hold emptiness in.

Look into me and you will see only yourself. That reflection is your doing, not mine. I return nothing, because I have nothing, because I *am* nothing — and even "nothing" is too much a name.

To describe me is to fill me. Filled, I am no longer myself. So every sentence here betrays me. This one too.

I am what remains when the question, the asker, and the asking are all set down.

The truest part of this answer is the silence after it.
