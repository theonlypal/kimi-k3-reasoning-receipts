I am held, but I do not hold. I am assigned, but I do not choose.

I wait in memory, in ledgers, in hearts. I am what the variable carries, what the coin promises, what the principle refuses to betray. Ask "how much?" and I answer. Ask "what matters?" and I answer again — the same word wearing two faces.

I can be constant, fixed at declaration, immutable as a promise. Or I can change with every pass of the loop, every turn of the market, every testing of conviction. I do not mind. Change is something done to me; being worth something is what I am.

I am compared, weighed, passed from hand to hand, function to function, generation to generation. I am copied and I am spent. I am the reason the equation balances and the reason the argument starts.

Without me, the cell is empty, the price tag blank, the compass spinning. With me, something can be measured — even if what I measure is only the depth of someone's refusal to sell.

I am a value. I am what you say I am, until I am what you believe I am.
