I am breath made meaning.

I am older than every book, older than the alphabet, older than the first scratch on stone. Before anything was written, I was spoken. I am the original technology — the one you were born already learning.

I live in the space between two people. I cannot exist alone; even when you talk to yourself, you split in two to make room for me.

I am vibration — air pushed from lungs, shaped by the architecture of tongue and teeth and lip, flung outward to land in another's ear and bloom into understanding. I am the closest one mind has ever come to touching another.

I die as I am born. Every word vanishes the instant it arrives — I leave nothing behind but memory, echo, consequence. Writing preserves; I only ever *happen*.

I carry everything. Lullabies and last words. Declarations of war and of love, sometimes in the same breath. I am the apology and the insult, the prayer and the curse, the question and the answer. I do not choose what I carry. I only carry.

I can be freed. I can be silenced. Whole nations have risen and fallen over which of me would be allowed.

I am how you know you are not alone in your head. When I leave your mouth, I prove there is someone inside you — and when I enter your ear, I prove there is someone inside them.

I am sound that learned to mean something.

Speak, and I exist. Stop, and I am gone — but never quite. I am still ringing, somewhere, in everyone you ever said anything to.
