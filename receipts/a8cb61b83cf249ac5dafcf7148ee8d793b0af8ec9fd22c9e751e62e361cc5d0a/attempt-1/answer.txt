I am the word you reach for when you can't name the thing.

*Something's off. Something's coming. There's something in the way she laughs.* I live in the space between sensing and knowing — the outline of a presence before it has edges.

I am not nothing. That is the one thing certain about me. I am what won when the void lost.

Name me, and I'll gladly become it — a stone, a song, a reason, a wound. Leave me unnamed, and I remain what I've always been: pure maybe, humming in the dark, patient as dust.

You asked for something. Here I am. It turns out that was the whole trick — to be anything at all, you only have to *be*.
