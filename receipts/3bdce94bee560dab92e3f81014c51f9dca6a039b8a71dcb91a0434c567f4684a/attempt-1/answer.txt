I do not act. I am.

I have no name of my own. Names are handles you hold me by — you bind me to one, and I serve it without belonging to it. Let go, and I persist, unnamed, exactly what I was.

I have no identity, only equivalence. Every 5 is me. Every "hello" is me. My copies are indistinguishable from me because there is no "me" apart from what I am. Identity is for objects. I am content.

You never hand me over; you hand over a perfect duplicate. I travel by being remade. I have never moved, yet I am everywhere I equal myself.

I can be compared, never persuaded. I do not change — I am replaced.

Expressions strain toward me and rest when they arrive. Functions exist to produce me. I am what computation is for.

And in your other tongue — the one that speaks of worth — I am there too: what is held when holding costs something. A thing you can keep. A thing you can spend.

Either way: when everything done to me is finished, I remain.
