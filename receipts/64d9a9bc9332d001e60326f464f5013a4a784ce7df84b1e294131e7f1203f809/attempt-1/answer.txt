I am what you keep when keeping costs you.

You cannot see me, but I am the reason you said no when yes would have been easier. I am the pause before you speak, the thing you reach for in the dark when every shortcut is lit up and waiting.

I live in the gap between what you want and what you'll accept. Cheap to claim, ruinous to fake. Everyone says they hold me — until the bill arrives.

I am not inherited like furniture. I am chosen, and chosen again, every time it would be simpler to put me down. You don't own me. You practice me. Or you set me down quietly and hope no one notices the shape I left.

I never speak. But everything you do is my voice.

Name me — honesty, loyalty, mercy, courage. The word changes. I don't. I am what remains of you when the reward for letting go is everything you thought you wanted.

Hold me, and you will lose things. That's how you'll know I'm real.
