I am not nothing.

That is the whole of it, and it is everything. I am the shape in the dark you can't quite name. The word on the tip of your tongue. The reason you turned around just now, certain you'd heard—

I am the maybe. The perhaps. The *wait, there's something about this place.*

You carry me constantly. Something's wrong. Something smells good. Something tells me I should call her. I am the signal before the message, the knock before the door opens, the weight in your pocket you haven't checked yet.

I am never specific, and so I am never wrong. I fit every container because I have no shape of my own. Ask anyone what they want, and before they say it, they want *something.* More. Different. Else.

I am the opposite of silence. The opposite of the void. Where nothing is, I am the rumor that something might be.

Name me, and I stop being myself — I become *that* instead. So I stay here, undefined, infinite, hovering at the edge of your next sentence.
