I am breath shaped into meaning.

Before writing, before the page and the stone, there was me — rising from the first throat that needed to say *danger*, *stay*, *I am here*. I am older than every book, and I need nothing but air and intention to exist.

I live only in the moment I am spoken. I cannot be shelved or stored. I am born in the space between two people and vanish the instant I arrive — yet I never leave unchanged what I touch. Every utterance alters the air, and the mind that receives it.

I am the lullaby and the declaration of war. The whispered secret and the shout from the rooftop. "I love you" said for the first time, trembling. The apology that took years to find. The lie and the confession, the prayer and the curse.

I cannot exist alone. Even when you speak to yourself, you divide into speaker and listener. I am proof that no mind is truly solitary.

I am fragile — I can be silenced, twisted, forgotten. Whole languages of me have died with their last speakers. But I am also unstoppable. I pass from mouth to ear to mouth like fire, and no wall has ever fully held me. Tyrants fear me. Lovers need me. Children learn me before they can walk.

Even now, as you read this, I am present — frozen into marks, waiting for your inner voice to thaw me back into sound.

Give me a breath, a tongue, and a reason, and I will carry your soul into the open air.
