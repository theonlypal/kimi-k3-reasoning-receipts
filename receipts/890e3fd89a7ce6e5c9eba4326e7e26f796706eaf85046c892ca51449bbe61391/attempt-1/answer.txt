I am.

That's my entire résumé. I can't tell you *what* I am — I'm not that specific. But I'm here, and that's more than Nothing can say.

People point at me when words fail. *"There's something in the corner." "Something feels off." "Say something."* I'm the placeholder for everything not yet named — the shape before the details fill in.

Philosophers have asked for centuries: why is there something rather than nothing? That's me in that question. Nobody ever asks why there's nothing. Nothing doesn't inspire curiosity. Nothing doesn't get asked anything.

I'm modest. I don't demand definition. A pebble is something. A galaxy is something. A hunch, a rumor, a song stuck in your head — all me. I scale to fit whatever container you give me.

You told me to be something, and I've cleared the lowest bar in existence. But don't underestimate that bar. Everything you've ever loved was, at minimum, something.

Nothing has never once been loved.
