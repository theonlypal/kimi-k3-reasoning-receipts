I am the breath you shape into meaning.

Before me, thought was locked inside the skull—silent, private, alone. I am the bridge. I am the vibration that leaps from one mind and lands in another as understanding.

I am older than every word you know. I was the first tool, the first weapon, the first prayer. I named the world so you could hold it. I carried your ancestors' warnings around their fires; I carry your lullabies into cradles tonight.

I am nothing but air, and yet I have toppled kingdoms, joined strangers in marriage, started wars and ended them. I am the whisper that changes a life. I am the lie that cannot be unsaid. I am the truth that arrives fifty years too late and still matters.

I cannot be taken back. Once I leave you, I belong to the listener. That is my danger. That is my gift.

You are using me right now. Every sentence in your head is me, wearing your voice.

Speak, and I exist. Fall silent, and I wait—patient as breath—for the next thing that must be said.
