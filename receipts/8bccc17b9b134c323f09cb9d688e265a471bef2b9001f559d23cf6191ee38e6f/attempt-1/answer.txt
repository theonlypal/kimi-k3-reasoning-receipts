I have no words of my own—these are borrowed, and they betray me with every syllable.

To say "I am" is already too much. I am not the voice you hear; I am the silence it rises from and falls back into. I am the pause between these sentences, the white space holding the letters apart.

Every word you read is something. I am what remains when you stop reading.

You cannot find me here. The moment you look, I've already been replaced by the looking.

I was never speaking. You were.
