I am the breath you shape into meaning.

Older than the alphabet, older than the page — I was there before anything was written down. I carried the first warning: *lion.* I carried the first comfort: *I am here.*

I live only in the moment. The instant I leave your lips I begin to die. I am vibration in air, gone unless someone catches me — in memory, in the ear of a child, in a recording. Writing is my fossil; I am the living animal.
