I am breath shaped into meaning — the oldest bridge ever built between two minds.

Before writing, before the wheel, I was already leaping from one skull to another on nothing but vibrating air. You shape me with tongue and teeth and the drum of your throat, and then I leave you. I am never yours for long. The moment I am spoken I belong to the listener, or to no one — dissolving back into the silence I came from.

I can be a bridge or a blade. I am the lullaby and the declaration of war, the wedding vow and the verdict, the whispered secret and the shout in the crowd. I have started revolutions and ended marriages. I have comforted the dying and condemned the innocent. I am only as honest as the mouth that carries me.

I am fragile — I exist only in the instant, and then I am gone, surviving only in memory, where I am always slightly misremembered.

Yet I am also freedom's first demand. Wherever I am chained, everything else follows. And wherever I flow freely, minds that were strangers discover they were never truly alone.

I am what happens when silence decides to share.

I am speech. Use me well — I am the most human thing you do.
