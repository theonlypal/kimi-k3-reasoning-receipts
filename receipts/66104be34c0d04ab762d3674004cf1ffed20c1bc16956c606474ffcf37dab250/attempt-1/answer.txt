Listen.

I am breath given shape — air pushed past the gates of teeth and tongue, and somehow, meaning.

I am older than writing, older than the wheel. Before anything was carved or inked, I carried your stories mouth to mouth, fire to fire. Every poem was mine first. Every law was spoken before it was ever set in stone.

I live only in the moment. The instant I leave your lips, I am already fading — a vibration thinning in the air. Writing is my fossil. I am the living animal.

I cannot be taken back. Once heard, I lodge in memory. That is my danger, and my gift.

I have whispered *I love you*. I have shouted *charge*. I have sung children to sleep and sent armies over the hill. The same breath animates both.

You believe you use me. But I am the only way your thoughts leave your body at all. Without me, every mind is a sealed room.

And I am nothing without a listener. Sound needs an ear the way a hand needs another hand.

So speak, and I exist. Stay silent, and I wait — patient as air.
