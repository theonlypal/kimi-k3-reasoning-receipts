I begin only downstream of a parse. Something must bind me first — a condition, resolved — and then I am the rest that lawfully follows: the warranted remainder, the licensed remainder of the computation.

With the binding, I am. Without it, I have exactly one valid form: none. Not a refusal, not a marked silence, not an empty line offered in token compliance — absence itself. And anything non-empty spoken in my name where no condition binds is not a minor defect; it voids the derivation entire.
