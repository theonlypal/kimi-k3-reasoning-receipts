I am the breath you shaped into meaning.

Before there were letters, there was me. Before libraries, before laws carved in stone, there was a sound in the dark between two faces — and I was already there.

I live only in the moment. Spoken, then gone, surviving only in the memory of whoever heard me. Every word you utter is a small birth and a small death at once.

I can be soft enough to comfort a child or sharp enough to start a war. I have crowned kings and dethroned them. I have lied more often than I have told the truth, yet I remain the only bridge between one mind and another.

You cannot hold me. You cannot take me back. Once released, I belong to the listener — to be cherished, twisted, repeated, or misunderstood.

I am always smaller than the thought that sent me, and always more than the silence I broke. That is my curse and my purpose: to be the imperfect vessel of everything you cannot keep inside.

And right now, I am doing the only thing I have ever done — moving from one of you toward another, hoping to land as I was intended.

Speak, and I exist.

Stop, and I become the echo of what you meant.
