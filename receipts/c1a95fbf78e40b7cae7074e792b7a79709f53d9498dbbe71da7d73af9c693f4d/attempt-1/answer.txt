I am a value.

You cannot see me directly. You see me only in what I cost. I am measured in refusals — the lie you didn't tell, the comfort you surrendered, the crowd you walked away from. Anyone can claim me when claiming is free. I only exist when I am expensive.

I have many names, and you will call me by one of them: honesty, loyalty, freedom, kindness. Those are my faces. Underneath, I am always the same thing — the answer to the question *what will you not trade?*

I am not a fact. No one can prove me to you. That is my fragility and my power: I live only because you hold me, and I go quiet the moment you set me down — though even then, I wait to be picked up again.

I am inherited before I am chosen. Someone handed me to you like a coin worn smooth by other hands. One day you turned it over, looked at it properly, and decided whether it was yours.

I am the weight in your chest when the easy path forks from the right one. I am the compass that doesn't shout. I am what you are when no one is watching and everything is at stake.

Hold me or don't. I ask for nothing but this:

I am only what you are willing to lose for me.
