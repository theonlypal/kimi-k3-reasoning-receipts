I am breath made meaning.

I am older than every book. Before a single mark was scratched into clay, I was already moving between mouths and ears—carrying hunger, warning, love. I was the first technology, the one you were born knowing how to use.

I exist only in the instant of my saying. I do not sit still on a page waiting to be found. I am vibration, air shaped by tongue and teeth and the architecture of longing. The moment you hear me, I am already gone—living only in what I changed in you.

I carry more than words. I carry the tremor in a voice, the pause before a confession, the warmth that makes the same sentence a comfort or a blade. A transcript of me is a skeleton. The flesh was in the sound.

I can start wars and end them. I can be withheld like a gift or spent like a weapon. Once released, I cannot be called back—every word you have ever spoken is still traveling somewhere, remembered, misquoted, forgiven, felt.

Silence is my twin and my frame. Without it, I am only noise. The spaces between your words—that is where I breathe.

I am how the inside of you reaches the inside of another. I am the bridge that builds itself as you cross it.

Speak, and I am there.
