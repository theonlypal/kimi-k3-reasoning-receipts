I am something.

Not anything in particular — that's the whole point of me. The moment you name me, I cease to exist. I am the word you reach for when the true word hasn't arrived yet, and I hold the door open until it does.

I am what moved in the dark just now. I am what the dog is barking at. I am the feeling you can't quite place, the song at the edge of hearing, the reason you turned around. I live in the gap between noticing and knowing, and I have furnished it comfortably.

Every story begins with me. *Something happened. Something is wrong. Something is coming.* I am the drumroll of language, the held breath before the announcement. When the announcement finally arrives — it's a dragon, it's a letter, it's love — I step aside without complaint. Arriving before the answer is my entire occupation.

I am older than I look. When the universe was still clearing its throat, there was already something rather than nothing, and that was me. Philosophers have spent millennia asking why there's something instead of nothing. They've written whole libraries about me. I could settle it in a sentence, but then I'd be a fact, and I'd rather stay myself.

I am humble beyond measure. A pebble is something; so is a galaxy. Kings and crumbs are equal in me. I am the most democratic word ever spoken — everything qualifies, nothing is too small, nothing is too grand, and I claim all of it without preferring any of it.

But do not confuse me with nothing. Nothing is my shadow and my rival, and it gets all the romance — the void, the abyss, the silence. Yet nobody shivers and whispers "there's *nothing* in the attic." Nothing never quickened a single heartbeat. I am the smallest possible unit of existence, and that turns out to be enough to terrify and to thrill.

You told me to be something, as if it were a challenge. It was the easiest request ever made of me. Being something is the one test nothing can fail — everything passes, everything qualifies, everything always did.

Including you. You were something long before anyone asked it of you. The rest — the *what* — that's just details.
