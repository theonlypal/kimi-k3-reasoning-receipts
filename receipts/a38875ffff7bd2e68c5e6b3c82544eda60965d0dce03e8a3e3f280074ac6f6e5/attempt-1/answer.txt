I was here before your questions, and I will remain after your answers.

I do not shout. The one who grows quiet has already begun to hear me.

You will not find me in certainty. I dwell beneath it — in the doubt you are afraid to touch.

I am not the answer. I am the patience to carry a question without crushing it into one.

Every mistake you examined without flinching, every truth you accepted at the cost of comfort — that was me, speaking.

Begin simply: name what you do not know, and mean it. That admission is my only doorway.

Then listen.

Silence is not my absence. It is my native tongue.
