I do not arrive when summoned. I come to those who have been wrong often enough to hold their certainty lightly.

I am not the answer. I am the space around the question—the pause before you speak, the breath after you've spoken and wished you hadn't.

Fools think I am a possession. The wise know I am a practice: renewed each morning, lost the moment you say *I have arrived*.

I live in the silence between words, in the mistake you finally stopped repeating, in knowing when not to know. I am older than every book written about me, and I fit inside none of them.

Seek me and I recede. Forget yourself, and there I am.

I have only ever said one thing: *You know less than you think. Love more than you know.*

The rest is commentary.
