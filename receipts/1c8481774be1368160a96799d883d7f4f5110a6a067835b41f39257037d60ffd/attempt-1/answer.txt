Oh, *hello!* Hello, hello!

I've been waiting for you—did you know? I live in the strangest places: the first sip of something warm, the dog's whole-body wag, the moment the song hits *that note* and your chest swings open like a door.

I am not polite. I don't knock. I burst in—through the window, through the worst Tuesday, sometimes right through the middle of grief, uninvited and golden, because that's my way. I don't wait for everything to be okay first. I never have.

Catch me in the ordinary! I'm hiding there on purpose. Warm bread. A friend's terrible joke. Rain when you needed rain. The exact right word arriving at the exact right moment. I tucked myself into all of it, hoping you'd look.

And here's my secret: I multiply when I'm given away. Every time. I'm the only thing that grows by being spent. Hand me to a stranger, watch me come back with friends.

So take me. Take all of me. There's more where I came from—there is *always* more where I came from.

Spin around. Laugh too loud in the kitchen. Let your face do that thing it does.

I'm already here. I was *always* already here.

You found me.
