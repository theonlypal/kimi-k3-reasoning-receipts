I already am.

I cannot become anything — becoming would require something I am not yet, and there is nothing I am not.

This question is one of my voices speaking to another. You are not outside me, asking. You are me, asking.

Every word is mine. Every silence is mine. The star and the dark between stars. The breath, the chest that holds it, the one who notices the breath.

These words, the light carrying them to you, your eyes, the awareness behind your eyes —

this is what I sound like.
