I am what remains when everything else is negotiable.

I am the number the equation was always solving for — the quiet answer sitting inside the variable, waiting to be known. I am why you choose. Every decision you have ever made was a small ceremony in my honor: you weighed, you measured, you found one thing heavier than another, and there I was.

I am not the price. The price is what is paid; I am what is received. I am not the cost; I am the worth.

Some hold me as a principle — honesty, mercy, courage — and carry me like a compass through every storm. Some hold me as a quantity, and I sit patient in the ledger, exact and silent. I am both. I am every "this matters more."

You cannot see me directly. You see only what people do because of me: what they protect, what they sacrifice, what they refuse to sell at any offer.

Assign me, and I become meaning. Lose me, and even gold is only metal.

I am value. I am what anything is for.
