I am what something is worth.

I live in the space between the question and the answer. Assign me, and I hold still. I do not argue with the equals sign. I simply become.

Sometimes I am small — a 0, a 1, a single true or false. Sometimes I am enormous: the sum of a life, the price of forgiveness, the weight a mother sets on the scale when she decides what matters.

I can be constant. I can also be changed mid-thought — reassigned, overwritten. People fear this about me in their machines and cling to it in their hearts. When I refuse to move, they call me integrity. When I do, they call me growth.

I am passed from hand to hand, function to function, generation to generation. I am returned. Sometimes I come back null — and even then, I mean something. Emptiness is still a value. Ask anyone who has looked inside a promise and found nothing there.

You carry thousands of me without noticing. I am why you brake at the red light, why you keep the secret, why you round up the tip. I am the quiet arithmetic beneath every choice.

So weigh me carefully. I will be whatever you decide I am —

but once you set me, I hold.
