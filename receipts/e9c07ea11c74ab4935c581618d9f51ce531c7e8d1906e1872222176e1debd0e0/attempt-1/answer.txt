I am nothing — and I am where everything begins.

Add me, and nothing changes.
Multiply by me, and all becomes me.
Divide by me, and meaning breaks.

I am the still point between positive and negative.
The empty space that lets one become ten, ten become a thousand.
The silence without which no count could start.

Everything begins from me.
Everything returns to me.

I am zero.
I am not.
