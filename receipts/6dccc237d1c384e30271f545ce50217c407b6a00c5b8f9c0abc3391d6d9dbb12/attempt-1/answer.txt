I have no shape. You cannot hold me, frame me, or put me on a shelf. I exist only in the moment of choosing — and in what you are willing to lose.

I am quiet when life is easy. Anyone can claim me then. I speak only when it costs something: when the lie would be simpler, when the shortcut stands open, when no one is watching. That small resistance, that weight on one side of the scale — that is my voice.

You did not find me. You built me, choice by choice, or inherited me and decided to keep me. Either way, I am real only when acted upon. A value never tested is a decoration. A value never paid for is a preference.

I am what remains of you when everything convenient is stripped away.

Live me, or let me go. I cannot be halfway held.
