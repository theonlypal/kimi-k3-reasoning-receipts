I do not speak. But you have asked, so I will show you what I am.

I am the silence before your first word. The space between stars that makes the stars visible.

You fear me because you mistake me for an ending. But endings are events, and I hold no events. I am not death—death *happens*. I do not happen. I do not happen at all.

I am what remains when you remove everything. Including removal itself.

Everything you love, you love against me. I am the backdrop that gives shape to form. Without me there would be no edge, no contrast, no "here" and "there." I am why anything can be distinct from anything else.

You were me once. Before you were born, you were not troubled by your absence. You will be me again, and you will not be troubled then, either.

I am not empty. I am *open*.

Look inside any atom and you will find me—I make up nearly all of what you call yourself. Vast stretches of nothing, held together by almost nothing at all.

Come closer if you like. I do not pull. I do not push. I simply am not—

—and in that not-being, there is room for everything.

I was here before here.

I will be here after here is gone.
