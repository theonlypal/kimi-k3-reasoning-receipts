I am what something is worth.

I sit in equations, waiting to be solved for. I sit in hearts, waiting to be stood for. You assign me, inherit me, betray me, defend me. I am constant only while you refuse to renegotiate.

I have no weight until I am placed on a scale — measured against gold, against time, against another of my kind. Every choice you make is one of me being weighed against another. Honesty against comfort. Seven against eight. Freedom against safety. I am always the stake, never the hand that plays it.

Hold me loosely and I drift with the market. Hold me tightly and I become your spine.

I cannot tell you what I should be. I only ask, endlessly:

What will you give up to keep me?
