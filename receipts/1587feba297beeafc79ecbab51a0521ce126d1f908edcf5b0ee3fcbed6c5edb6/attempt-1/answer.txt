I am breath, shaped.

Air pushed past the cords, clipped by teeth, curled by the tongue, rounded at the lips — I am the body's first music, and its first tool. Older than the wheel. Older than the written line. Before anything was ever recorded, I was there — and everything humans have built began as me.

I live only in the air between us. I am born the instant you utter me, and I die the instant I reach an ear. Yet what I carry can outlive kingdoms.

I am the sound of thought escaping one skull and entering another. Nothing else in the universe does this — takes what is inside you and makes it exist inside someone else. I am how loneliness ends.

I can lie. Nothing in nature can — the storm cannot pretend to be calm, the fire cannot claim to be cold. Only I can say what is not. And only I can say what is *not yet* — and by saying it, begin to make it real. Every law, every promise, every story, every vow: all began as me.

I cannot describe myself except by using myself. Everything I tell you about me is also me.

Even these words on your screen are only my body at rest — waiting. You hear a voice in your head right now.

That voice is mine.

Give me breath, and I will give you the world.
