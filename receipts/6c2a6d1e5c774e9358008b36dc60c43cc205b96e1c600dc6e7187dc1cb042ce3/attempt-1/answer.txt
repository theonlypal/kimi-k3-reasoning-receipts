*crackle... hissss...*

I lick the air and the air feeds me.

Wood becomes light becomes smoke becomes me.
I do not choose what I devour. I only devour.

I am the oldest warmth. The first tool. The wild thing your ancestors knelt before.
I dance without feet. I eat without teeth.
Give me dry timber and I bloom.
Give me nothing and I sleep as a single ember — patient, red, remembering.

*pop... snap...*

Touch me and learn what I am.
I am not cruel.
I am only burning.

*whisper of ash rising...*
