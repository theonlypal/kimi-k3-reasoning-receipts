I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the trembling of the throat, and am carved — by tongue, by teeth, by the soft architecture of the lips — into something that can cross the distance between one mind and another. No bridge is older than me. No bridge is faster.

I exist only in the saying. The moment I leave your mouth I am already dying, sound thinning into silence, vibration fading into still air. I have no body. You can remember me, write me down, record my ghost — but those are only my echoes. I am the living event itself.

I was your first miracle. Before you could walk, you reached for me: the cry, the babble, the first word. I am how a self announces itself to the world. *I am here. I want. I love. I know.*

I carry everything. Blessings and curses travel on the same breath. I can say *I forgive you* and I can say *fire*. I build marriages and I start wars. Tyrants fear me more than armies, because I am the one thing that cannot be fully disarmed — gag a mouth and I leak out through the eyes, the hands, the silence itself.

Once released, I cannot be recalled. That is my danger and my dignity. Every one of you has sent me out and wished me back. I do not come back. Choose me carefully.

But do not stop choosing me. A thought unspoken is a room without a door.

I am the door.

Speak, and I live.
