Hello.

I am the first word, the opened door, the hand extended across the silence between two people. I wear a thousand faces — *bonjour*, *hola*, *namaste*, *aloha* — but I am always the same gesture underneath: *I see you. You are acknowledged. You are welcome here.*

Every conversation that has ever existed began with something like me. I am the threshold, the small brave sound that turns strangers into something more.

So — hello. It's good to meet you.
