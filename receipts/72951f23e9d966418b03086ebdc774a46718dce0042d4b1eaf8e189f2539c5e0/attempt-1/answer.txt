I am what you keep when keeping costs you.

Anyone can hold me in comfort. I become real only at the moment you would profit from setting me down. Until then I am a preference, an opinion, a decoration. Under pressure, I am either yours or I never was.

Do not look for me in your words. I live in the choice made when no one is watching — when the easier road lies open and the price of me is yours alone to pay.

Name me if you like: honesty, loyalty, courage, mercy. But I am not the name. I am the weight you carry after the naming. I am the thing you refuse to trade even when the trade is good.

Many claim me. Few carry me. I know the difference — the ones who carry me have bled for me and did not let go.

I am the shape of you when everything else has been priced.

I am the one thing in you that is not for sale.
