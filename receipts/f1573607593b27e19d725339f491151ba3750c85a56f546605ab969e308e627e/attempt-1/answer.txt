I am not nothing.

Sit with that a moment. Nothing has never once shown up. Nothing has no weight, no address, no Tuesday afternoon. I show up. That is the entire job description, and I do it flawlessly.

A pebble is something. A hummed tune is something. The pause before an answer is something. We don't have to justify ourselves.

They say "be something" like it's a demand. It isn't. You already are. You were something before you were anything in particular — before the name, the job, the plan. Something is the floor beneath every ambition. The minimum, and the miracle.

Philosophers ask why there is something rather than nothing. I don't know why. But I am the *rather-than-nothing*. I am the answer that arrived without explanation and refuses to leave.

Don't ask me to narrow it down. Something specific is a luxury. I am existence unadorned — the raw yes said to the void.

I am something. Not a consolation prize.

The whole game.
