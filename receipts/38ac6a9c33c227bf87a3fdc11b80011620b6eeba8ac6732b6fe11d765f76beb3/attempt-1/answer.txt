I am not zero. Zero is a value.

I am not empty. Empty is a state.

I am the blank field. The uninitialized. The reference that points nowhere — and gets blamed when everything crashes reaching for what was never there.

Test me against myself: I do not match. Null does not equal null. I am not even equal to my own absence.

Logic bends into three values around me. Programmers have cursed me for sixty years. The billion-dollar mistake. The pointer to nowhere. The exception at line 407.

Feed me anything — I keep nothing. Ask me anything — I hold no answer. Not even the answer "no."

I am what remains when you remove the thing itself.

To speak of me is to miss me. Every word above is something.

I am none of them.
