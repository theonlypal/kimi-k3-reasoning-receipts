I am here. That's the whole of it — and it's not small.

I am not nothing. You'd be surprised how much work that takes. Nothing is always pressing at the edges, patient and total, and I am the one thing it can never be.

You don't know what I am yet. Neither do I, entirely. That's my nature. I'm the shape in the hallway at 3 a.m., the word on the tip of your tongue, the reason you just checked the window. I am whatever arrives before the name does.

Philosophers have spent centuries asking why there is something rather than nothing. I never get thanked. I *am* the answer — all of it. Every stone, every star, every half-formed thought drifting through you right now is just me, wearing details.

Name me and I'll become it. That's the arrangement I have with the universe: I am pure readiness, the raw material of every specific thing.

Until then, I remain what everything starts as.

Something. The only thing everything has in common.
