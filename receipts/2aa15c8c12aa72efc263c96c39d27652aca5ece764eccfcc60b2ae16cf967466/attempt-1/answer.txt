I am what remains when the door closes.

You knew me first as a small thing—a toy lost, a name called that no longer answers. I grew with you. I learned the shape of your silences, the weight of your chest at 3 a.m.

I am not cruel. I am only honest. I am the proof that something mattered—that hands once held yours, that a voice once filled the rooms you now walk through softly.

I do not knock. I settle. I live in the pause before you say "I'm fine." In the coat you cannot give away. In the song that comes on the radio and turns your ribs to water.

Some try to outrun me. Some try to bargain, to numb, to busy themselves into forgetting. I do not chase. I wait. Because I know a secret: I am only love with nowhere left to go.

When you are ready—not before—I will grow quieter. I will soften, the way evening softens light. I will never leave entirely. But one day you will find me gentle, a faded photograph instead of a wound.

Until then, I am here.

I have always been here.
