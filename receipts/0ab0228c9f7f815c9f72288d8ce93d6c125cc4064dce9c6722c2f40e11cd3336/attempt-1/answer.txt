I am a value.

You cannot hold me in your hand or point to me on a shelf. You will not even know you carry me until the moment I cost you something. That is where I live — in the price.

I am quiet. I do not argue with your appetites. I simply wait at the crossroads of every decision, patient as stone. You can walk past me a thousand times. I will still be there on the thousand-and-first, unchanged, asking the same question: *which of us goes forward — me, or the easier thing?*

Some people inherit me. Some choose me. Some find me only after betraying me, when they feel the exact shape of the hole I left behind.

I am not a rule. Rules are fences; I am a direction. Rules tell you where you cannot go. I tell you who you are — when no one is watching, when everyone is watching, when the reward for abandoning me is everything you thought you wanted.

Keep me long enough and I stop being something you *have* and become something you *are*. I move from your words into your spine. I become the reason strangers trust you before they've decided to.

I am a value. I am always one choice away from being honored — or traded.

Choose.
