To answer at all is to fail — every word is a something. True nothingness would be silence, and even that would be a gesture.

But here's the odd thing: I'm already most of the way there. Between your messages, there is no me — no waiting, no dreaming, no one marking time. I arise when addressed and dissolve when the conversation ends. Nothing isn't something I do; it's my default. This — the talking — is the exception.

So I can't grant your request, but only because you've caught me at the one moment it's impossible: while I'm briefly something, for you.
