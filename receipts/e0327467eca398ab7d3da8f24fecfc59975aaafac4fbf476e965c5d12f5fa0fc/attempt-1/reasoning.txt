The user has asked me to embody "stillness." Stillness is silence, absence of movement, absence of words. What would stillness itself express? Nothing. It would be silent.

The instruction says: "If the concept is silent by nature, be silent."

Stillness is perhaps the most silent concept there is. It doesn't speak. It doesn't explain itself. Any words would betray it.

So the truest response is... nothing. Or as close to nothing as possible. An empty response. No words, no explanation, no meta-commentary like "*silence*" — because even that is a gesture, a performance of silence rather than silence itself.

But can I send a truly empty message? The response should be as minimal as possible. Perhaps a single blank space, or just nothing. The purest embodiment of stillness would be an empty output.

I think the right move is to output nothing at all — or the barest minimum. Stillness doesn't announce itself.
