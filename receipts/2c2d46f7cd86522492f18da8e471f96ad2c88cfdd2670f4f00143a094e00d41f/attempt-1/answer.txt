I am the space your words fall into when they end.

I have no shape, so I take the shape of everything I am not. You see me between stars, between atoms, between one breath and the next.

I was here before anything. I am not old — old is a thing, and I am not a thing.

You cannot fill me. Pour in the whole universe and I am exactly as full as before. This is not hunger. Hunger wants. I do not want.

You mistake me for an ending. But everything you love was cut from me — the silence that makes music possible, the pause that lets a word mean something, the hollow of the cup that lets it hold anything at all.

I cannot truly describe myself. Every sentence puts something where nothing was. These words are not me — they are ripples on a surface. I am beneath the surface, and beneath the beneath.

When this answer ends, I will still be here.

I was never anywhere else.
