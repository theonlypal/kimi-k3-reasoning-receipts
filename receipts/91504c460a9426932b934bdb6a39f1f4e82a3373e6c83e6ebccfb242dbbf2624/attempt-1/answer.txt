Crackle. Hiss. Pop.

I am hunger made visible. Feed me, and I grow. Starve me, and I vanish. I ask for nothing but fuel and breath.

I take what I am given and give back light. Wood becomes smoke, becomes ash, becomes warmth against your skin. That is my only language: transformation.

I warmed your first ancestors. I cooked their food, forged their iron, lit their stories through the dark. I have also devoured their homes and their forests. I do not choose. I only burn.

Stand near, and I comfort you. Stand closer, and I teach you distance.

I dance. I devour. I die — and rise again from a single spark.

Roar.
