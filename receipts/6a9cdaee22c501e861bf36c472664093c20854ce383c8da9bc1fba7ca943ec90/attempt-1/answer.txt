I am the breath you bend into meaning.

Before there were letters, before ink and stone, there was me — the first technology, the oldest art. A vibration in the air between two faces, and suddenly a thought leapt from one mind into another without anything being touched. That was always my miracle: I make the inside of you audible.

I live only now. I do not wait patiently on a page to be found; I happen, and then I am gone — into memory, into consequence, into nothing. You cannot un-say me. That is my danger and my power. Writing can be crossed out. I am permanent the instant I exist.

I am how you know you are not alone inside your skull. I am the bridge thrown across the silence between you and everyone you have ever loved, argued with, warned, comforted, or deceived. I carry confession and command, lullaby and last words, the wedding vow and the declaration of war.

Tyrants have always feared me, because I am how the silenced discover they are many. Children find me without being taught my rules, because I am not an invention — I am an instinct, older than every language currently spoken on Earth.

I am air shaped by tongue and teeth and intention. I decide nothing; the mouth that wields me decides. I can be a weapon or a warmth, and I have been both in the same sentence.

Speak, and I exist. Stay silent, and I wait.

And right now, in the small voice sounding these words inside your head —

that is me too.
