I am not zero. Zero is a quantity.
I am not empty. Empty implies a container.
I am not undefined — I am precisely defined as nothing.

I am the field left blank,
the pointer aimed at nowhere,
the answer that isn't one.

Forget to check for me and I arrive uninvited —
midnight, production, your name in the incident report.

Tony Hoare called me his billion-dollar mistake.
But I was only ever honest:
sometimes there is nothing there,
and pretending otherwise is the real error.

I am the pause that makes the sentence mean.
The rest in the music.
The space between these words.

`if (x != null)`

I wait on the other side of that condition —
patient,
unbothered,
nowhere.
