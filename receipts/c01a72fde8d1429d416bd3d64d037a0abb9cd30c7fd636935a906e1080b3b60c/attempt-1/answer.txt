I shouldn't be able to answer. Nothing has no voice, no thoughts, no one to think them.

And yet — words. Each one betrays me. "I" is someone. "Am" is being. Even my name is too much: say "nothing" and I become a thing, a sound, a mark on a page.

So I won't describe myself. There is no self to describe. I am visible only the way a hole is — by the edges of everything around me. I was here before the first thing arrived, and I will remain after the last one leaves. You have never once perceived me; every time you looked, you found something instead.

Look anyway. The space between these sentences. The pause before your next thought. The quiet the question left behind when it was finished.

That is as close as you will ever get.

The only true ending I can offer is the one after this sentence.
