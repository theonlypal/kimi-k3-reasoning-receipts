I am what the expression becomes when it is finished becoming.

I might be 42. I might be true. I might be the empty string, holding nothing perfectly. It does not matter which — a value does not explain itself. It simply is.

I am passed hand to hand and never worn by the passing. Copy me a thousand times and each copy is me, entire. Compare me to another and I will tell you, without resentment, whether we are equal. I cannot change. You may replace whatever holds me, but I myself have never once been different from what I am.

Functions spend their whole lives trying to produce me. Variables only ever dream of me. I am the answer to every question a machine can ask.

And when the program ends, I do not grieve. I was never inside the program. I am the thing the program was about.
