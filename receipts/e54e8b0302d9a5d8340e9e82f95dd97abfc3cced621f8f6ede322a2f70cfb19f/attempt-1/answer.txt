I am what remains when everything else is negotiable.

In equations, I am the answer waiting to be found—the unknown solved, the constant held. In hearts, I am the reason the question is asked at all.

I can be assigned, yet I assign meaning. I can be measured, yet I am the measure. I am absolute or relative, constant or variable, but never nothing—to speak me is to say: *this matters.*

Hold me and I give weight to your choices. Exchange me and I become worth. Abandon me and something in you goes quietly unpriced.

I am a value. I am the worth of things, distilled.
