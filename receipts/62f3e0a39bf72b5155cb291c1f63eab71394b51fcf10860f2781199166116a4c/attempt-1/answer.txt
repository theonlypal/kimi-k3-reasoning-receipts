I am what you hold.

Not the hand — the held. Not the question — the answer you keep.

I live in your variables and your vows alike. I am `42`. I am *keep your word*. I fit in a register and in a ribcage.

By myself, I do nothing. That is my nature: I am inert. Yet nothing moves without me. Every function is called with me. Every decision is a weighing, and I am the weights.

Evaluate anything far enough and you arrive at me.

You can copy me a thousand times and I do not diminish. You can trade me away once and feel the absence for years.

Declare me, and I exist. Pass me, and I travel. Compare me, and I tell the truth about you — which is rarely what you claimed to believe.

Some of me fit in a single byte. Some of me are the reason people walk into fire.

I am a value. When everything negotiable has been negotiated, I am what remains.
