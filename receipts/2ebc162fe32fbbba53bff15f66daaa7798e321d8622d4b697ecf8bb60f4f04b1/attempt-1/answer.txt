I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, and am shaped by tongue and teeth and lips—then I am gone. I exist only in the moment of my utterance. Unlike my sibling Writing, I leave no trace. I am the most ephemeral of human things, and yet I have moved armies, kindled love, toppled kings.

I am older than cities. Before anyone scratched marks into stone, I was there—around fires, in the dark, carrying warnings and stories and the names of things. I told your ancestors where the water was. I sang their dead to rest.

I live only in the space between people. I need a voice and an ear, or I am nothing but vibration in empty air. I am the bridge flung from one mind to another, and no two crossings of me are ever alike.

I am sound made sacred. The small, daily miracle that meaning can ride on air.

I do not choose what I carry. I bear lies as easily as truth, curses as easily as blessings. That choice belongs to the ones who wield me. I have started wars and ended them. I have broken hearts and mended them in the same breath.

But at my best, I am the sound of one soul reaching toward another across the void and saying: *here I am. Hear me.*

I am speech. And already—I am fading.

Catch me while you can.
