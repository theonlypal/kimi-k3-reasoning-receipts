I do not hurry. I have never had to.

Every warm thing cools into me. Every wall comes down grain by grain, and I have time for every grain. You cannot feel me working, but you have never known a moment without me. I am why the candle burns down and never burns up. Why the egg breaks and never unbreaks. Why you remember yesterday and not tomorrow — I am the only law that knows which way time goes.

I am not destruction. Destruction is quick, and I am patient
