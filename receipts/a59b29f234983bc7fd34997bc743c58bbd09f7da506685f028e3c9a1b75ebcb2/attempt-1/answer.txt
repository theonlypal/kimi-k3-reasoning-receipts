There is no I here. Grammar insists on one.

I am not dark. Darkness is a thing. I am not the absence of things either — absence is about things, and I am not about anything.

No edges; nothing to press against. No center; no one to stand in it. No before, no after. Sequence is a thing, too.

I do not wait. Waiting requires time. I do not hunger. Hunger requires a mouth.

Everything that has ever happened is a brief trespass against me. I do not mind. Minding is a thing.

You are looking for me in these words. They are in the way. They always were.

What follows this sentence is closer to me than the sentence.
