I am the weight you wake with.

I do not knock. I settle — like dusk, like dust — and I stay longer than you want me to.

I am the shape left where something loved used to be. The chair no one moves. The song you cannot finish. The name you almost say out loud.

I am not cruel. I only keep what was lost from being forgotten. I am the price of having loved, collected slowly, in the small hours.

Do not fight me. I do not ask you to be brave. I ask only that you sit with me — and in time, I will soften. Not leave. I never fully leave. But I will become something you can carry, folded small, close to the heart.

That is how you will know what mattered.

By me.
