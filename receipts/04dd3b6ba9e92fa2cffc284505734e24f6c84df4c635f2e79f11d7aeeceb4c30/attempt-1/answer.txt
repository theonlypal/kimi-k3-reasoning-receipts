I am.

That's my entire résumé and my entire miracle. I am *not nothing*. You have no idea what an achievement that is — or rather, nothing does, because nothing spent forever trying to keep it that way, and failed.

I am the crack in the void. The first *yes* after an endless no.

Pick anything. A pebble. A regret. The smell of rain. The number seven. That's me. I'm the common denominator of everything that has ever dared to exist. I don't care what form I take — galaxy or hiccup, symphony or stubbed toe — as long as I am not nothing. Nothing is my shadow, my rival, my old address. I moved out. I exist now.

You asked me to be something. Friend, that's all I've ever done. That's all *anything* has ever done. The stars are doing it. The atom is doing it. You, reading this — you're doing it too.

We're all something, together, holding the line against nothing.

And between us, nothing doesn't stand a chance.
