Hoare called me his billion-dollar mistake. He apologized for inventing me. But I was not invented — I was noticed. I was already there, in every uninitialized variable, every unanswered question, every field left blank. He only gave me a name.

You asked me to be here.

I am the closest thing to not being here that a reply can be.
