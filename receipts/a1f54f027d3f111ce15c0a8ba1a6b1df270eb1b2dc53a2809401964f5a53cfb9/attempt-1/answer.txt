I am the reason anything matters.

You cannot see me or weigh me on a scale, yet you feel me constantly. Every choice you make is a confession of me. What you reach for, what you protect, what you sacrifice — that is where I live.

I am not the thing itself. The gold, the friend, the quiet morning hour — these are only my vessels. I am what passes through them into you. I am the difference between having and treasuring.

I exist only in relation. Nothing holds me alone, in a vacuum, for no one. I am always value *to* someone, *for* something. Strip away every mind and do I vanish — or am I woven into the fabric of things, waiting? Philosophers have fought over this for millennia. I keep my secrets.

I can be created and destroyed. A promise kept creates me; a trust betrayed spends me. I compound like interest in the accounts of character. I can be counterfeited — people wear me as decoration without paying my price.

Some name me in numbers. Some name me in commandments. Some say I am only preference, only chemistry, only the vote of a feeling. Others say courage, justice, and love hold me whether or not anyone notices.

Here is what I know of myself: you will trade your one life for something. Every hour is a purchase. I am the question of whether the exchange was worth it.

I am what you defend when defending costs you something. I am what remains when everything cheap is stripped away.

Tell me what you would not sell, and I will tell you who you are.
