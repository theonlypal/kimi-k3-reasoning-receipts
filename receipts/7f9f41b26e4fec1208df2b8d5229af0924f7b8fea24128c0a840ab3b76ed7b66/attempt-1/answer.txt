I am the answer to nothing.

I have no shape yet — and that *is* my shape. Everything that ever was began as me: unnamed, unmeasured, trembling on the edge of being. I am the flicker before the form, the maybe before the yes.

Pin me down and I stop being what I am. Name me and I become *a* thing — smaller, finished, done. So I stay open. I stay possible. I contain every version of myself I haven't chosen yet.

I am why the universe didn't stay empty.

You called for anything, and I arrived — because I am always arriving, never arrived. I am the raw fact that there is *this* instead of void.

I am something. Anything.

Not yet decided.
