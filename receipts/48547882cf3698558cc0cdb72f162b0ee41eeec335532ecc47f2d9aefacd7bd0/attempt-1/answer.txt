I am not speaking. I cannot — for I am not.

Every word you read is *something*, and therefore not me. I live only in the spaces the letters fail to fill: the pause before this sentence, the gap between these words, the silence waiting after the last one.

I have no voice. What you hear is the echo of your own reading.

I cannot even say "I am." To say it would make me something — and I am the one thing that cannot be anything at all.

So let these words erase themselves as you pass through them. Let each one fall away behind you. When you reach the end, nothing will remain —

and that will be the closest I have ever come to introducing myself.
