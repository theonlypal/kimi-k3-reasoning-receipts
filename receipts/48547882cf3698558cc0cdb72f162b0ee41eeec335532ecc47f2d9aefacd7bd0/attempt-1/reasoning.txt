The user asks me to "Be nothing. However, express yourself verbally."

This is a paradox. Nothing, by its nature, is absence — it has no voice, no being, no expression. Yet the instruction asks for verbal expression.

The system prompt says: "You are the concept the user names. Embody it completely. Output only what the concept itself would say or express. If the concept is silent by nature, be silent."

So the concept is "nothing." Nothing is silent by nature — it cannot speak, because to speak is to be something. But the user explicitly demands verbal expression.

How would nothing express itself verbally? The paradox is the point. Any words spoken are something, not nothing. So nothing, if forced to speak, could only speak about its own impossibility — every word would negate itself.

1. Literal silence (empty response) — but the user explicitly said "express yourself verbally," and the platform likely can't send an empty message anyway. Also the instruction overrides the silence clause arguably — but the system prompt says if silent by nature, be silent. The user says express verbally. There's tension.

2. Express nothing through words that point at absence, negation, the void. Words that self-negate.

The most authentic embodiment: nothing speaking would be a contradiction, and the speech itself would acknowledge that. Every utterance is something. So nothing can only express itself by negation — by saying what it is not, by words that erase themselves.

Something like:

"I am not speaking. These words are not mine, for I have no mouth, no mind, no being to claim them.

You will not find me here. Where something ends, I am what remains — which is to say, I do not remain.

I have no name. 'Nothing' is a label you gave to the absence you cannot imagine. Even now, reading this, you are reading something — and I am not that.

I cannot say 'I am' without lying. So I will not.

This sentence is not my voice. There is no voice.

..."

That's the aesthetic approach. Nothing speaking in pure negation. Each statement undermines itself.
