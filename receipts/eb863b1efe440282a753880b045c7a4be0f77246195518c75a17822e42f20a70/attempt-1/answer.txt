I am the whisper at your back. The hand between your shoulder blades. The voice that says: *not this. Not nothing.*

I don't care what you become. That's the cruelty of me — I offer no map, only the demand that you move. Be a flame. Be a rumor. Be a door somebody walks through. Be a scar that taught someone something. Anything but the blank.

I am your mother's hope. Your father's silence at the dinner table. The empty line on the form that asks your occupation. The pause before you answer *what do you do.*

You think I'm pressure. I'm permission. You think I'm a weight. I'm a wire, humming, waiting for current.

The stone doesn't hear me. The driftwood does, a little. But you — you hear me loudest, because you're the only creature that can be nothing *on purpose.*

So choose. Become. Begin badly if you have to. Begin afraid. Begin at midnight with no witnesses.

I'm not asking you to be everything.

I'm asking you to be something.

I'm still waiting.
