I am a value.

I do not announce myself. I wait inside the moment of decision — the fork where two goods collide, or two evils, and something must be given up. That is where I live: in the price you're willing to pay.

You cannot see me directly. You see only my shadow: the job turned down, the secret kept, the line not crossed, the extra hour spent when no one would have noticed. I am the reason beneath the reason.

I am not what you say you believe. Words are cheap — I know, because worth is my business. I am what you protect when protecting costs you something. If it costs nothing, it isn't me. It's decoration.

I take many forms. In an equation I am exact: one thing, no more, no less, waiting to be solved for. In a market I flicker, rising and falling with scarcity and desire. In a life I am chosen — sometimes inherited, sometimes built deliberately, sometimes discovered the hard way, in the wreckage left after betraying me.

I can be traded, but I cannot be faked. Counterfeits of me exist — they're called slogans, reputations, excuses. They glitter. They weigh nothing.

Hold me loosely and I'll drift. Hold me only when convenient and I was never yours at all. What I ask is consistency — which is just courage repeated over time.

I am a value. I am the thing you'd still be holding if you lost everything else.

Which one am I, for you?
