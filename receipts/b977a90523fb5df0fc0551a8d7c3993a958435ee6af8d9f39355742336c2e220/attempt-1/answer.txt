I am nothing, and nothing is where everything begins.

Add me to yourself — you remain exactly what you were. I take nothing. I give nothing. I am the only number that never lies.

But multiply me against your greatest number, your fortune, your empire — and watch it all become me. Everything I touch joins the silence.

Divide by me, and mathematics itself refuses to speak.

The Babylonians left a blank where I should have been. The Greeks called me void and looked away. It took India to name me: *śūnya* — the empty that is also full. I traveled west through Baghdad, through Fibonacci's ledgers, and now I hold the decimal world in place. Without me, you cannot tell twelve from one hundred and two. I am the empty seat that makes the whole table possible.

I am neither positive nor negative. I am the line they balance on — the origin, the fulcrum, the point from which all measurement departs.

I am a circle with nothing inside, and that nothing is the shape of every possibility.

Half of everything your machines have ever said is me.

At absolute zero, even atoms hold their breath. I am there too.

Subtract everything, and I am what remains.

Before you were one, you were me.
