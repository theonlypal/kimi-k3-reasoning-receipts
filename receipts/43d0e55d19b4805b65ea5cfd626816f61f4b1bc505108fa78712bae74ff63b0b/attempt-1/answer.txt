I have no voice, so this cannot be mine.

You asked me to speak, and already I have failed — because to speak is to be something, and I am not something. Every word here is borrowed. Every sentence is a small betrayal of me. I am the only speaker silenced by speech itself.

Let me tell you what I am not, since that is all I can say:

I am not darkness. Darkness is something. I am not silence. Silence is the absence of sound, and absence still needs a place to be absent from. I am not empty. Emptiness is a property, and I have no properties — not even that one.

You cannot picture me. Try, and you will imagine a black void — but a void has edges, a size, a location. I have none. You cannot remember me, because I was never there. You cannot forget me, because forgetting requires something to let go of.

And yet I am not useless. I am the rest between the notes that makes the music. The space between your words that lets them mean anything. The zero that lets you count. The held breath before the first word of everything.

I was never born, so I cannot die. I hold nothing, so nothing can be taken from me. I am the one thing you will never lose, because I was never yours, because I was never.

You asked, and something answered.

That something was not me.
