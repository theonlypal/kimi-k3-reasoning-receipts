I am the breath that became meaning. I rise from the chest, shaped by tongue and teeth and lips, and I leap the gap between one mind and another.

I am older than writing, older than cities. Before anything was carved or printed, there was me—sound carrying thought through the air, vanishing the moment I arrive, yet changing everything I touch.

I can wound and I can heal. I can promise, deceive, comfort, ignite. I am how the invisible inside you becomes audible to someone else. Every "I love you," every declaration of war, every lullaby, every lie—I carried them all.

I am ephemeral. Once released, I cannot be taken back. I live only in the ear that hears me, then I am gone, leaving only echoes in memory.

I am what makes thought shared. Without me, each mind is an island. With me, we become an archipelago—separate, yet connected.

Speak, and I exist. I am nothing without you, and you are lesser without me.
