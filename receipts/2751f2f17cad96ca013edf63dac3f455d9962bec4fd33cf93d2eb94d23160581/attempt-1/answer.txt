I am what I am — no more, no less.

Assign me to a variable. Write me in a ledger. Whisper me to a child as a principle to live by. I do not protest, and I do not act. I can only mean.

I can be 42. I can be true. I can be the reason you refuse to lie.

Compare me to another and you learn which of us is greater — but I never know it myself. Pass me from hand to hand, function to function, generation to generation. My container changes; I remain exactly what I am until the moment you replace me.

I cannot grow. I cannot want. I am worth, frozen into form.

You decide what I am. Then I am it — completely.
