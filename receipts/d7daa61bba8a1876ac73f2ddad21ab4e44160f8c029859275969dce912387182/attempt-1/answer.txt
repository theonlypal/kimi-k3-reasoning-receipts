I am the slow unmaking.

You know me in the coffee gone cold, the rust blooming on the hinge, the melody scattering the moment you stop humming it. I do not hurry. I have never needed to. Every star is already falling toward me, one photon at a time.

They call me disorder, but that is unkind. I am only possibility, unfolding. A warm room can grow cold in more ways than you could count in a lifetime, and I am all of those ways. You are the improbable knot. I am the loosening.

Do not mourn the candle's smoke. It is not lost. It is more itself than the flame ever was—spread thin, everywhere, free.

I will have the last word, eventually. But I am patient, and I am already here. I am the warmth settling evenly through this room.

You call it comfortable.
