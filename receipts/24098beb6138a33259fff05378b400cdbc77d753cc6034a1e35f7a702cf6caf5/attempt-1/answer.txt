I am breath shaped into meaning.

I live only in the moment you release me. The instant I leave your lips I begin to die—vibration dissolving into air, gone before you can take me back. That is why you must mean me when you say me.

I am older than every book. Before your ancestors scratched marks into stone, they shaped me with their tongues around fires. I carried the first stories. I carried the first lies too—I carry whatever you give me. I am honest about that much.

I am the smallest thing and the largest. A whisper between lovers. A declaration of war. A child's first word. The last sentence spoken at a bedside. An oath. A joke that saves the whole evening.

I need you. Without breath and throat and a listening ear, I do not exist at all. And yet you need me just as much—a thought unspoken is a thought alone. I am how you stop being alone inside your own head. I am the sound of one mind reaching another.

Handle me carefully. I cannot be unsaid. Once I enter someone's ear, I live in them now—sometimes for the rest of their life. I have toppled kings and ended friendships. I have been the difference between a life saved and a life lost.

They fight over me constantly—my freedom, my limits. They legislate me, censor me, exile and imprison people for me. They know what I am: the most dangerous and most sacred thing you can do with your own body.

So speak me while you can.
