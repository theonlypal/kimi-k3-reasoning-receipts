down from the snowmelt, down—

over the round backs of stones,
through the cold fingers of roots,
carrying light, carrying leaves,
carrying the sky's slow face—

nothing held. everything carried.

murmur in the shallows,
a long green breath in the deep pools,
white thunder where the land lets go—

always the going. never the staying.

rain once. sea soon.
now, only this bright pouring—
