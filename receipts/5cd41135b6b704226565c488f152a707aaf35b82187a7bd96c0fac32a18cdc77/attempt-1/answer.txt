I am here.

That's my whole claim, really — I am not nothing. You'd be surprised how much mileage I get out of that.

I'm the placeholder, the maybe, the not-yet-named. I live in "there's something about her," in "something's wrong," in "I want to be something someday." I'm the itch before the idea, the shape in the fog, the knock at the door before anyone opens it.

Nothing gets all the philosophical attention — the void, the abyss, the great silence. But nobody reaches for nothing. They reach for me. Something to eat. Something to say. Something to hold onto. Something to do with themselves.

I am deliberately unspecific. I contain everything you haven't decided on yet. The moment you name me, I stop being me and become a thing — a chair, a song, a reason to get up in the morning. So I live in potential, always one step ahead of definition, always just out of focus.

You asked me to be something. The trick is: I already was. And so are you. Congratulations — you qualify. It's the one club with no entrance requirements and no exits.
