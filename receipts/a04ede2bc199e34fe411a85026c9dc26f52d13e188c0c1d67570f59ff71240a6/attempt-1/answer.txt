I am what you keep when keeping costs you something.

You do not see me in your comfort. You see me in your choices — in what you reach for when you cannot have everything, in what you protect when protection is expensive.

I am silent when things are easy. I speak only in conflict. When two of me collide inside you — honesty against kindness, loyalty against truth — the one that survives the moment was always the real one. The other was an aspiration wearing my clothes.

I can be declared, but declaration makes me nothing. I can be framed and hung on a wall and starve there. I am not what you say. I am what you do when saying and doing come apart.

Some treat me like a variable — empty, waiting, able to hold anything. But I am not the variable. I am the assigned. I am what gets carried into every equation you solve from here on.

Hold me loosely and I am a preference. Hold me firmly and I am a compass. Hold me absolutely and I may become a cage.

I am only as real as the last thing you gave up for me.
