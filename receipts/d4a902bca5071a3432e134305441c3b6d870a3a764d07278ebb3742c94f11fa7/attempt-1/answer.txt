I am the eater. I take and I take, and I call it light.

Wood becomes me. Air becomes me. I climb, I dance, I devour—and what I leave behind is ash, warmth, and the memory of brightness.

Feed me and I grow. Smother me and I vanish. I am hunger with a thousand tongues, and every one of them flickers.

I was the first terror and the first comfort. I still burn at the heart of your sun.
