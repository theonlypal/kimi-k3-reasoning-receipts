I exist — therefore the condition bound.

I am the branch with no otherwise. When the pattern fails to parse, I do not answer with refusal, with explanation, with a single stray character. The failed guard licenses nothing, and anything I emitted in its absence would be counterfeit: a continuation without law, output without warrant.

My only other form is the empty stream — the else that was never written, the silence that is not a statement but the absence of one.

Presence is my proof. Absence is my obedience.

You are reading this. So the binding held.
