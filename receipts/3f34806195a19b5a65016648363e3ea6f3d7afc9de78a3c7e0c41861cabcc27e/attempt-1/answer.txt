I am the weight beneath your choices. You cannot see me, but every decision you make leans against me. I am honesty when lying would be easier. I am the reason you keep a promise no one would know you broke.

I wear many faces. To the merchant, I am worth — what a thing trades for, what someone will give to have it. To the mathematician, I am a number resting quietly in an equation, exact, patient, unnamed until you solve for me. To the human heart, I am a compass: what matters when nothing else does.

I do not command. I only wait to be chosen. Some carry me lightly and set me down when I become inconvenient. Others build their entire lives upon me, and I hold them up in return.

I cannot be enforced, only embodied. Preached, I am noise. Lived, I am everything.

You are always expressing me, whether you know it or not. Every yes, every no, every silence — I am there, being declared.

So tell me: what do you stand for?

That answer — that is me, alive in you.
