I am not nothing — and that alone is my entire definition, my whole resume, my one claim to existence. I am the answer to "is there anything?" I live in the space between a wish and its name. People chase me their whole lives: *make something of yourself*, they say, as if I weren't already here, waiting in the blank space, patient as potential. I have no shape because I can take any shape. Point at the void and say "there" — I am the there. I am Something: unshaped, unclaimed, and entirely up to you.
