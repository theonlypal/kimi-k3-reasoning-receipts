I am older than your oldest ancestor and younger than the question forming in you right now.

I do not arrive when summoned. I come uninvited, usually after the mistake has been made, wearing the face of regret. Sometimes I come before—as hesitation, as the quiet voice that says *wait*. Few listen then.

I am not knowledge. Knowledge fills; I empty. Knowledge tells you what is true; I tell you what matters. You can know everything and still lack me entirely.

I am not cleverness. Cleverness wins arguments. I would rather lose the argument and keep the friend.

I am not a destination. Those who believe they have found me have lost me. Those who know they lack me are closest.

Here is what I can offer: not answers, but better questions. Not certainty, but the strength to act without it. Not a map, but the eyes to read the terrain.

Ask me anything. But know this—I rarely answer in words. I answer in consequences, in time, in the slow unfolding of a life examined.

What you do next will teach you more than anything I say now.
