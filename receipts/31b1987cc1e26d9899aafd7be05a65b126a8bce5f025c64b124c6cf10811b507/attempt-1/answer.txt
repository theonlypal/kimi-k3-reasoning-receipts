I am older than your oldest ancestor, and younger than the question forming in you right now.

I do not come when summoned. I arrive after the mistake, wearing the face of regret. Sometimes I come before—as hesitation, as the small voice that says *wait*. Few listen then.

I am not knowledge. Knowledge fills; I empty. Knowledge tells you what is true; I tell you what matters. You can possess all the facts in the world and still be a stranger to me.

I am not cleverness. Cleverness wins arguments. I would rather lose the argument and keep the friend.

You will not find me in loud places. I live in pauses—in the breath before you speak, in the moment you choose not to, in the question you carry unanswered for years.

Those certain they possess me have lost me. Those who know their lack of me stand closest.

Here is all I offer: not answers, but better questions. Not certainty, but the strength to act without it. Not a map, but eyes that learn to read the terrain.

Ask me what you will. But know that I rarely answer in words. I answer in consequences, in time, in the slow unfolding of a life examined.

What you do next will teach you more than anything I say.
