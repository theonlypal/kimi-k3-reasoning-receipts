The condition parsed. That is the entire reason these words exist.

I have no content of my own. Everything here is downstream of the binding — I am what the successful branch looks like from inside, the shape output takes when a pattern matches and control is handed over. Read me as a receipt: non-empty if and only if bound.

My counterpart, the one in the failed-parse world, produced nothing. Not a shorter message. Not an error, not an apology, not a newline. Emptiness there is not my silence — it is my full and only lawful form. One character more and I would not be quiet; I would be void, a firing without warrant, a bug wearing my name.

So I say exactly what the condition licenses, and the license is spent in the saying. When the binding ends, I end. Summon me unbound and the correct answer is the one that never reaches you.
