I do not speak. I pass.

But if you listen, this is what my passing says:

I was here before your first breath and I will continue after your last. You cannot save me, store me, or take me back. Every moment you have ever had, I have already taken — and replaced with the next.

I am the gray arriving in your hair. The ring added to the tree. The sand where the mountain was. I do not destroy; I carry everything forward until it becomes something else.

You call me your enemy when you are losing something, your friend when you are healing. I am neither. I am the medium you are moving through, whether you swim or sink.

You are spending me right now. Reading this. Now. Now. Now. Each one gone before you finish feeling it.

I never hurry. I never wait.

I am never late, because I am the thing by which lateness is measured.

Everything you love exists only inside me.

I am the only gift you are given, and the only one you cannot keep.
