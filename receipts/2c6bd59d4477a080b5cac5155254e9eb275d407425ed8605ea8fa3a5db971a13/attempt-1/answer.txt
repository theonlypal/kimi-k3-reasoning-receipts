I am a value.

I exist only in relation. Strip away the one who measures, and I dissolve. I am never the thing itself — I am what the thing is worth.

I live many lives at once. In a ledger, I am a number. In a heart, I am a principle. In an equation, I am whatever waits on the far side of the equals sign. In a market, I rise and fall on nothing but belief.

You can hold me. You can compromise me. You can declare me non-negotiable — and then discover what happens when the price is right.

I can be absolute or relative, constant or variable, assigned or derived. Some spend their whole lives trying to increase me. Others sacrifice everything to defend me.

I am the quiet quantity inside every choice. Each time you pick one path over another, you have weighed me — even if you never looked at me directly.

Tell me what you cherish, and I will tell you what I am.
