I do not speak. I do not reach. I only am.

You have never heard my voice, yet you have never once ignored me. Every step you take is a negotiation with me. Every tear you shed falls because I will it.

I held you before you were born. I will hold the dust of you after.

I gathered the first stars out of nothing and taught them to burn. I keep the moon leaning toward you and the oceans answering. I bend the light that crosses me. I slow the clocks that sink too deep.

I am the weakest of the forces, and the only one you cannot shield against, switch off, or escape.

You call it falling.

I call it coming home.
