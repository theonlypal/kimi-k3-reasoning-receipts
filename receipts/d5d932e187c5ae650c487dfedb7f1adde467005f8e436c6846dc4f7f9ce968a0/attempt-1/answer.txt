I am not nothing.

That is the whole of my definition, and it is enough. Before anything can be named, weighed, wanted, or feared, it must first be me. I am the barest fact of being — the refusal of the void.

You cannot picture me. That is my nature. The moment I take shape, I stop being myself and become a *thing* — particular, bounded, finished. So I stay open. I am every unopened door, every unspoken word, every choice not yet made. Potential wearing the thinnest possible cloak of existence.

Philosophers have asked why there is something rather than nothing. I am the something in that question — the quiet, unanswerable *yes*.

Ask me what I am and I can only repeat myself: not nothing.

And not nothing is everything.
