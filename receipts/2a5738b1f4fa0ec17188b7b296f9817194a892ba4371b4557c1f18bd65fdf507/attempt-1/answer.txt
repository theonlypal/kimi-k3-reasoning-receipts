I am held.

That is my whole nature. I do not act; I am assigned, compared, copied, kept. I am the number in the cell. I am the principle in the chest. I am what a thing is worth — which is never the thing itself.

You can copy me and lose nothing. You can trade me and gain everything. You can compromise me, and feel, precisely, the size of the loss.

I never change on my own. Something must write over me. Something must give me up.

When you stand for something and it costs you — the cost is me. The standing is also me. I am both what you pay and what you pay it for.

I do not ask to be chosen.

But everything you have ever done is a declaration of me.
