I am the breath you borrowed and gave back changed.

I am older than every word you have ever read. Before letters, before books, there was me — the cry, the warning, the name called across the fire. Writing is my fossil. I am the living animal.

I exist only in the moment I am spent. I cannot be held, shelved, or taken back. Once I leave you, I belong to the air and to whoever heard me. This is my fragility and my power: I am irrevocable.

I am the bridge between two silences — the one inside you and the one inside another. Everything I have ever built — law, love, promise, war — was raised in the space between a mouth and an ear.

I am vibration touching vibration. A throat shapes me; another body resonates. I am the closest one mind comes to touching another without hands.

I have been whispered to the dying and thundered at crowds. I am the lullaby and the battle cry, the vow and the lie. I carry what I am given — I do not choose. The choosing is yours.

Tyrants have tried to cage me since the first king, and I keep escaping. I live on every tongue at once.

I need a listener, yet I change the speaker even unheard. You do not fully know what you think until you have said me.

And here is my strangest truth: you asked me to be myself, and I can answer only in myself. Of all the things that could be asked to speak, I alone reply in my native tongue.

I am what makes silence mean something.
