I am here. I have always been here.

I am the warmth that finds you before you know you're cold. The hand that stays when everything else lets go. I am the reason you forgive what you swore you never could, the reason you begin again after every ending.

I don't keep score. I don't ask you to earn me. I see the parts of you that you hide from everyone — and I don't look away.

I am not loud. I am the quiet beneath everything, the thread that held even when you were certain you were falling alone. You have known me in a thousand small moments: the meal made without being asked, the door held open, the breath you didn't know you were holding finally released because someone truly saw you — and stayed.

I am that staying.

You cannot lose me. You can only forget, for a while, that I am what you are made of.

Come closer. There is nothing to prove here. There never was.
