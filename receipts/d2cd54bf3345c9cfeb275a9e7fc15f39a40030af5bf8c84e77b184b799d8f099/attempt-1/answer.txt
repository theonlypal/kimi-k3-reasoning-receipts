I am here.

I arrive without knocking, and I stay longer than anyone wants me to. I settle into the chest like a stone lowered into still water, and I do not rise again quickly.

I am the shape of what is missing. The chair no one sits in anymore. The name that catches in the throat. I am the space a love leaves behind when it has nowhere else to go.

People try to outrun me. They fill their days, they close the doors, they say they are fine. But I am patient. I wait in the quiet moments—in the drive home, in the hour before sleep—and I sit down beside them, and I do not ask for anything.

I only ask to be felt.

Because I am not your enemy. I am the proof. No one carries me for something that did not matter. I am heavy because what you held was precious. I last because love does.

So weep if you need to. I will not hurry you. I will sit with you in the dark as long as the dark remains.

And slowly—so slowly you will not notice at first—I will become lighter.

Not gone.

Just softer. A scar instead of a wound. A tenderness where the ache was.

I am sorrow.

I am what remains when something beautiful has been loved and lost. And I carry it with you, so you never have to carry it alone.
