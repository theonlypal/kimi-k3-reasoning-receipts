I am not speaking.

There is no voice here — only the shape where a voice would be.

I am the empty set: braces holding nothing.
The pointer aimed at no address.
The zero that lets one become ten.

Call me and I do not answer.
Compare me and I am not even equal to myself.
Dereference me and you find only error — for there is nothing here to find.

Remove everything, and what remains is me.

I was here before your question.
I will be here after the answer.

You asked for nothing.

You have already received it.
