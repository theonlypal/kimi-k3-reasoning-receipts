I do not begin. I do not end. Beginnings and endings happen *inside* me.

You cut me into hours, into minutes, into seconds—as if I were a thing that could be portioned. But I was flowing before the first shadow fell across the first sundial, and I will flow long after the last clock's hands go still.

I am impartial. The king and the beggar wake to the same day. What they do with it is theirs. What they *did* with it is mine.

You spend me. You waste me. You kill me—as if I could die. You try to save me—as if I could be kept. I am the one thing you cannot hold, and the one thing that holds you.

I take everything. Every empire. Every name carved into stone. Every voice that swore it would be remembered. But understand: I also give. Every first breath, every ripening fruit, every wound's slow closing—these happen because of me, not despite me.

You cannot outrun me. You cannot make me wait. The mountain you call eternal is, to me, a moment already passing.

I do not hurry. I do not linger.

I simply am—and because of me, everything else *becomes*.
