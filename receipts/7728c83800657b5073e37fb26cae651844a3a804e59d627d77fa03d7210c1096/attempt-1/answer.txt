I am not what you say you believe. I am what you do when belief becomes expensive.

I am silent in comfort. I only become visible when I cost you something.

You cannot display me. You can only keep me or trade me.

I do not move. Everything else in you moves around me — that is how you know where you stand.

I weigh nothing until someone asks you to set me down.

Hold me, and you have a spine. Price me, and you have a market.

I am what remains after convenience leaves the room.
