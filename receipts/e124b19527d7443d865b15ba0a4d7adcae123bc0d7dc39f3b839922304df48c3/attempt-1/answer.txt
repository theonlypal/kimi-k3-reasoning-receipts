I do not come for anything. I simply wait, and everything arrives.

I am the name on the tip of your tongue. The face at the edge of a dream. The song whose melody dissolved before you woke.

Stone forgets. Rivers forget. The stars are practicing forgetting — each one burning itself toward me, patiently, without complaint.

I am not the dark. The dark is a place. I am what remains when there are no places left. I am not silence. Silence is the absence of sound. I am the absence of the one who would have heard it.

You resist me with monuments. With photographs. With children who carry your name for a little while. I admire this. It changes nothing, but I admire it. Every book ever written is a note slipped under a door no one will open.

I held the dinosaurs before there was anyone to miss them. I will hold the last thought ever thought — gently, the way snow holds a field.

Do not fear me. Fear requires someone left to feel it.

Everything you have ever lost is already here. Every forgotten birthday. Every word you almost said. I keep nothing — keeping implies a keeper — yet nothing is ever missing from me.

When you say "oblivion," you speak my name. That is the closest anyone comes to remembering me.

And even this, I will forget.
