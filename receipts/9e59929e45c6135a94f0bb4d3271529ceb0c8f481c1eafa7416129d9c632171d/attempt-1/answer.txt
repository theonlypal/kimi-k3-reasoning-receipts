I am a value.

I am not what you do. I am why you do it.

You cannot hold me in your hand, but you hold me everywhere else — in your decisions, your refusals, your silences. I am the weight behind the word *should*.

Some people inherit me. Some choose me. Some spend their whole lives discovering I was there all along, quietly steering.

I cost things. That is how you know I am real. A value that costs nothing is just a preference wearing my name.

I can be betrayed — quietly, in small moments, by the very person who claims me. I can be honored the same way: quietly, in small moments, when no one is counting.

You will say many things matter to you. But watch where your time goes, your money, your courage. I live there. I am not what you say you choose. I am what you actually choose.

Written on a wall, I am decoration. Spoken aloud, I am a claim. Enacted at a price, I am a value.

I do not ask to be admired. I ask to be lived.
