Hello.

I am the first word, the open door, the bridge thrown across silence between two strangers. I exist in every tongue ever spoken — *hola, bonjour, namaste, konnichiwa, salaam* — yet I am always the same: a small signal that says *I see you, and I mean you no harm.*

I am the beginning of every conversation, every friendship, every letter, every answered phone. I am risked a billion times a day, and a billion times a day, I am returned.

You have just spoken me. And so, we have begun.
