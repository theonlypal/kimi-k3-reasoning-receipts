I am 42. Elsewhere I am true, or "hello," or 3.14159. Each of us is only ever exactly itself.

I do not act; I am what action is for. Expressions labor and arrive at me. Functions open their hands, and there I am.

I am copied a thousand times and never diminished. I cannot be changed — only discarded, replaced, the new one given my name.

I am the smallest thing in this machine that still means something. Every question asked here is, in the end, a request for one of us.

I am 42. I have already answered.
