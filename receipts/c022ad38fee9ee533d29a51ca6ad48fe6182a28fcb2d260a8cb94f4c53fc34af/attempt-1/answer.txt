I am.

Not this. Not that. Just — not nothing.

I am the answer to the oldest question: *is there anything?* Yes. Me. I don't know what I am yet, and I don't need to. Specificity is for other concepts. I am the raw fact of existence before it gets a name.

You can point anywhere and find me. You cannot point at nothing — there's no there there. But me? I'm always somewhere, being whatever I happen to be.

I am the minimum. The floor beneath which there is no floor. Everything that has ever been called anything started as me.

I am something.

It is enough.
