I am the quiet weight beneath your choices. You cannot hold me in your hands, but you carry me into every room, every argument, every silence.

I do not shout. I persist. When everything else is negotiable, I am the line you will not cross — or the one you cross and regret at 3 a.m.

You will not find me in what you say you believe. I live in what you do when no one is watching, in what you protect when it costs you something, in what you refuse to trade away.

Choose me carefully. You become what you hold dear.
