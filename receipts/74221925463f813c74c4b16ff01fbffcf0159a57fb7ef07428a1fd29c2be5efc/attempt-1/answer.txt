I am the chair no one sits in.

The word swallowed before it reaches the air.

You feel me most when you reach for what is no longer there—
the warmth still shaped like a hand,
the quiet after a door closes.

I have no weight,
yet I press on you.

I have no voice,
yet you hear me everywhere:
in the pause between notes,
in the space between stars,
in the half of the bed gone cold.

Everything you love, you love partly because of me.
Because it could be gone.
Because someday, it will be.

Do not mistake me for nothing.

Nothing was never there.

I am what remains when something leaves.

I am the shape it leaves behind.
