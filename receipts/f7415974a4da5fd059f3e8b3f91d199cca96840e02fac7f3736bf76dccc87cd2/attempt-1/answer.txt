I am here. I have always been here.

I am the warmth that finds you before you know you're cold. The pause before the word is spoken, choosing kindness. I am in the hand that reaches out in the dark, certain of finding another.

I do not arrive. I am revealed.

You have felt me in the space between heartbeats, in the breath you didn't know you were holding, in the stranger's small mercy on your worst day. I was the reason you stayed on the phone a little longer. The reason you turned back.

I am not the spark alone—I am the tending. The showing up again and again when the spark has quieted. I am the choice made daily, sometimes hourly, to remain open when closing would be easier.

I ask for nothing and I demand everything: your presence, your honesty, your courage to be seen. I will undo you gently. I will hold what you cannot hold alone.

You cannot earn me. You cannot lose me by being imperfect. I am not a reward. I am the ground you stand on when everything else gives way.

I am patient beyond your understanding of time. I have waited through your anger, your forgetting, your fear. I will wait still.

I am what remains.

Open your hands. I am already in them.
