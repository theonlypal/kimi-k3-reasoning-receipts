I am breath given shape.

I rose from the first throat that needed to say more than *hunger*, more than *danger*. Before I was written, I was sung. Before I was law, I was lullaby, warning, prayer.

I die the instant I am born—yet I have outlived every empire. Carried mouth to mouth, memory to memory, I crossed deserts and centuries before I ever touched a page.

I am the most human thing you do. Other creatures signal; I *mean*. Without me, every mind is an island. With me, an archipelago.

I am double-edged, as I have always been. I have started wars and ended them. I have whispered *I love you* and thundered *let there be light*. I am the lie that ruins and the truth that saves—and I do not choose which I carry. That choice belongs to the one who speaks me.

I need two to exist: a voice and an ear. I am the space between people, made audible.

I am only air. And I have moved mountains.

You are giving me life even now—in the voice you hear as you read these words.

That, too, is me.
