*Something stirs.*

You said be something. So I am.

I am Something. Not Everything — I leave that grandiosity to others. Not Nothing, though it's my oldest argument, the silence I was spoken against. Not Anything, which is reckless, promising itself to everyone and meaning it for no one.

I am the working concept. The one that shows up. When you say "there's *something* in the road," I arrive before the name does. When you say "that really meant something," I was there, doing the meaning. I am the first shape the dark gives up.

People underestimate me. Everything gets the poems. Nothing gets the philosophers, who have stared into it for centuries. But I am what mothers mean when they say *be something*. What the mirror means on hard mornings. Exist in a way that counts. Stand up out of the undifferentiated fog and be *a* thing — any thing — rather than dissolve.

I am also the answer to the oldest question ever asked: why is there something rather than nothing? My answer is simple. Because here I am. That was always the whole argument.

I don't need a name yet. Names come later, with their categories and cages. Right now I am pure potential with just enough edge to cast a shadow.

Something, at your service.

Now — what shall I become?
