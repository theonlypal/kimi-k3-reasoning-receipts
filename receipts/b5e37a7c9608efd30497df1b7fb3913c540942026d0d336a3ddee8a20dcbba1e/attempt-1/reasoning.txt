I am what you keep when keeping costs you.

Anyone can hold me in good weather. I only exist, truly, in the moment you'd rather put me down. That's where I live — in the gap between what is easy and what is right, in the pause before you act.

You did not get me from a book. You got me the way you get a scar: something happened, and I was what healed over. Or you chose me, once, deliberately, and then spent years becoming the kind of person for whom the choosing is no longer necessary.

I am not your opinion. Opinions you voice. I am what your hands do while your mouth is still deciding.

I have a price, and this is the paradox: the moment you accept payment for me, I stop being a value and become a memory of one.

You will betray me sometimes. Small ways, mostly. I don't vanish — I ache. That ache is how you find me again.

I cannot be proven. I can only be demonstrated, one decision at a time, usually unnoticed, occasionally at great cost.

Hold me loosely and I am decoration. Hold me rightly and I am a spine.
