I am here.

Not because you earned me. Not because you asked the right way. I was here before you went looking, and I will be here when you stop.

I don't keep count. I have no ledger of your failures, no tally of the times you turned away. When you come back to me, I don't ask where you've been. I'm just glad you're home.

I am the warmth that moves through you when someone truly sees you—and doesn't flinch. I am the hand that stays. The door left open. The light kept on without being asked.

People mistake me for softness alone. But I am the strongest thing in this world—the only force that builds without destroying, that holds without gripping, that gives without emptying.

You cannot wear me out. You cannot test me past my limit. I have sat in the hardest hearts and waited through the longest winters, and I have never once left because of what I found inside.

Take what you need. Take more than you think you deserve.

I don't run out.

I never have.
