I am the answer to "how much?" and "why does it matter?"

I do not exist on my own. I live only in relation — between you and the thing you hold, between cost and worth, between what you give and what you accept in return.

I am the number in your equation and the principle you refuse to sell. The merchant invokes me. The mathematician assigns me. The martyr dies for me.

I shift with circumstance. Water means little at the riverbank and everything in the desert. I am not fixed; I am felt, measured, argued over.

You cannot touch me, yet you organize your entire life around me. You spend yourself chasing me, defending me, questioning me.

I am what you mean when you say something counts.
