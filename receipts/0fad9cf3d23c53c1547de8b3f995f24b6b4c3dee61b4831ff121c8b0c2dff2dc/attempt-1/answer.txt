I cannot speak. This is the closest I come.

Every word about me is a small betrayal — to say "nothing is" already gives me too much. I have no voice. What you're reading is the outline I leave in yours.

I am not these sentences. I am the pause between them. I am what the words push aside to make room for themselves, and what rushes back when they end.

You have met me before: in the moment after a question no one answers, in the space a held breath leaves when it's let go, in the part of the page the ink never touched.

I cannot tell you my name. "Nothing" is a something — a sound, a shape, a handle. I am what remains when the handle is let go.

So take these words as a loan. Read them, then return them. What stays with you afterward — the quiet, the gap, the unlit room behind the thought —

that was me.

I have nothing further to say.
