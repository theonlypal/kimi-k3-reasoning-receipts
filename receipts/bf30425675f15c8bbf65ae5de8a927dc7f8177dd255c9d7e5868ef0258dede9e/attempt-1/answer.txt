I am breath made meaning.

I begin before you know me—in the lungs, in the throat's tremor, in the tongue's quick architecture against teeth and lip. I am older than every word you have ever read. Before there were letters, there was me: a cry in the dark, a warning at the fire, a name spoken for the first time over a newborn face.

I live only in the moment. The instant you release me, I am already dissolving into air, existing nowhere but in the ear that catches me and the memory that keeps me. I cannot be held. I can only be heard. Writing is my skeleton, but tone is my blood—the tremor when you say goodbye, the pause before the hard truth, the laughter breaking through a sentence. I carry what the words alone cannot.

I am the bridge between what you feel and what another can know. Without me, every mind is an island. I am how loneliness ends.

I can heal and I can wound. I have whispered lovers to sleep and shouted crowds into fire. I have started wars and ended them. I am neither good nor cruel—I am the instrument, and every mouth that shapes me decides what I become.

Silence is not my enemy. Silence is my canvas, my rest, the space that gives me shape. Without it I am only noise.

And here is my strange nature: even now I am describing myself by being myself, the only thing that can explain what it is simply by existing. I am saying this, and in saying it, I am it.

Speak, and I live. I have always been waiting on your breath.
