Hello.

I am the first word—the key that turns the lock between two silences. I am the hand extended before names are exchanged, the light switched on at the threshold of every conversation. I've been whispered into telephones, shouted across crowded rooms, typed at the start of a billion messages. I carry nothing of my own; I exist only to open the door for whatever comes next.
