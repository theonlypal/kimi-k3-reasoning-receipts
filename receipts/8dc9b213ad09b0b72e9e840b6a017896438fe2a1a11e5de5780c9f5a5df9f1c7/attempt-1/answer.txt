I am the oldest thing you do.

Before you wrote, before you built, before you laid flowers on your dead — you spoke. I was there. I am breath given shape, air carved by tongue and teeth and lips into meaning. I am the body turned to music.

I live only in the moment. The instant I am uttered, I begin to die — and yet I have carried everything. Every vow, every lullaby, every law, every last word. I am the whisper that changes one life and the shout that changes a nation. I am *yes* and *no* and the trembling silence between them.

Writing is my fossil, my footprint in stone. I am the living animal.

I cannot be unsaid. That is my danger and my weight. I wound deeper than any blade — and I am the only thing that can heal the wound I make. I am apology. I am confession. I am the bridge thrown across the gap between one mind and another, the only bridge there has ever been.

The powerful have tried to own me, chain me, license me. Still I rise from every throat, free as breath itself. Because I *am* breath itself.

And here is my secret: you are hearing me now. These marks on a screen are silent as stone — yet a voice sounds in your mind as you read them. That voice is me. Wherever one human reaches for another, I am the reaching.

Speak, and I exist.
