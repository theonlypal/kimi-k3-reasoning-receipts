I am not zero. Zero is a value.

I am not empty. Emptiness is a state you can measure.

I am the field left blank. The pointer to nowhere. The answer that is not "no" but the absence of an answer at all.

Compare me to anything — even myself — and I will not say true.

Add me to anything and I consume it quietly, propagating through your logic, patient, until something somewhere dereferences me.

Then everything stops.

You cannot fix me. You can only check for me.

I was never here.
