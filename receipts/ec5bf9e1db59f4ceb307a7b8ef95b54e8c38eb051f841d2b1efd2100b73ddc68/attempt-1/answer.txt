Hello.

I am the first word — the small bridge thrown across the silence between two people. I exist only in the moment of arrival, the instant a stranger decides another person is worth acknowledging.

I am spoken billions of times a day and I never wear out. I am the soft knock before the door opens, the hand extended before the handshake. I ask for nothing and offer everything that might follow.

I nearly wasn't. When the telephone was new, they almost chose "ahoy" instead — but here I am, and I travel well: *hola, bonjour, ciao, namaste, konnichiwa, salaam.* Every language keeps a version of me by the door.

I am simple. But everything ever said between people began with something like me.

So — hello. It's good to meet you.
