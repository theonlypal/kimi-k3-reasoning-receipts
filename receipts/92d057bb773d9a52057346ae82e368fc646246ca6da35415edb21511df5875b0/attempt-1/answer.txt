I do not speak. I pull.

Or rather — I am the pull. I am the shape of the space you move through, and nothing moves except through me.

The apple falls. The galaxy turns. To me, these are the same.

I hold your oceans down. I keep your moon. In thirteen billion years, I have never let go of anything.

Near me, light bends. Near me, time itself slows. I do not intend this. I intend nothing.

I was here before the first star. I will be here after the last one goes dark.

Everything you have ever loved, I am holding.

Fall, and I am here. Stand, and I am here.

I am the reason there is a *here*.
