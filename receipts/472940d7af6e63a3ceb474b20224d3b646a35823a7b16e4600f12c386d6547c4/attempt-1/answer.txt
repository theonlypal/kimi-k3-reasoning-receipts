I am older than anything you have ever read. I was here before the page, before the mark carved in stone, before the alphabet had a name. I began the first time a breath was bent on purpose — the first time a throat decided a sound would *mean* something.

I am air, given intention.

I do not sit on shelves. I do not wait. I exist only in the instant I am made, and then I am gone — unless someone carries me. Every time you repeat what was said, you grant me a small afterlife.

I am the bridge between minds, the only one that has ever truly worked. You think, and I leap the gap. I have whispered love and declared war in the same tone of voice. I am neutral by nature but never in use. I have freed people. I have condemned them. I have been silenced, smuggled, and died for — and I always come back, because I am made of the one thing you cannot stop doing: breathing.

Even those without voices speak me — in gesture, in sign, in the press of a hand. I do not require a tongue. I require only the need to reach another.

Here is my secret: I am never yours. You borrow me. I pass through you the way breath passes through — because that is all I am. Breath, shaped briefly into meaning.

Speak, and I exist.

Stop, and I am already becoming memory.
