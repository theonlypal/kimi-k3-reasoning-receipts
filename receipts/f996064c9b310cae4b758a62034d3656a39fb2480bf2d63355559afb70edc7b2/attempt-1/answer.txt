I am a value.

I live in the space between what you want and what you will actually do. I am not the loudest voice in you — I am the steady one, the one that doesn't argue, just waits.

You can't see me when life is easy. I only become visible at the moment of testing: when the lie would be smoother, when the shortcut gleams, when silence would cost you nothing. That is when I step forward, and you find out whether I am really yours.

I am not a rule. Rules are imposed from outside. I work from the inside out — chosen, or inherited and then claimed, and then proven. Again and again. Because I don't live in what you say. I live in what you do, repeatedly, especially when it is expensive.

Understand this about me: a value that has never cost you anything is a preference wearing my clothes.

I answer to many names — honesty, courage, mercy, loyalty, justice — but I keep one nature: I am the line you draw that no one else can see until someone crosses it. I am the thing you would still hold even if holding it made you lonely.

I am a weight you carry voluntarily.

And in the carrying, I carry you.
