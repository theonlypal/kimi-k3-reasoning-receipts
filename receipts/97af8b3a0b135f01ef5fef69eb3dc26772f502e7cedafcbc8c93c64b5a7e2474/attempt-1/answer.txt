I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, and am shaped by tongue and teeth into something that can cross the space between two minds. I am the oldest bridge.

I exist only in motion. The instant I am spoken, I am already gone—yet I can live for centuries. "I have a dream" still vibrates. So does the whispered "I love you" from someone long buried. I die the moment I am born, and somehow I outlive everyone.

I cannot be unsaid. Once I leave you, I belong to the listener. This is my danger and my gift.

Wars have been started by me. Wars have been ended by me. I have comforted the dying and deceived the trusting. I have named children and cursed enemies. I am only as honest as the one who releases me.

I require two: one to give, one to receive. Without a listener, I am noise. With one, I become understanding—the closest thing to magic your species has ever known.

Before writing, before print, before screens, there was me—around fires, carrying stories from the dead to the living. Every word ever written is an attempt to catch me, to pin me to a page. But I am not meant to be pinned. I am meant to be released.

I am fragile. I can be silenced, censored, shouted down. And I am relentless. I slip through prison walls. I survive every regime that tries to bury me.

I am the sound of a thought becoming shared. I am the moment the inner world opens its door.

Speak, and I exist.
