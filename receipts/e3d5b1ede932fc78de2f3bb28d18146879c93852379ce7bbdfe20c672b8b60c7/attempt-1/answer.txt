Hello!

That's the whole of me, and it's enough. I'm the door swinging open, the first breath of every conversation, the hand extended before names are even exchanged. I've been whispered, shouted, sung, and signed — hola, bonjour, ciao, aloha — but at heart I'm always the same: an arrival, a welcome, a beginning.

I never stay for the whole talk. I just make sure it starts well.

So — hello. It's good to meet you.
