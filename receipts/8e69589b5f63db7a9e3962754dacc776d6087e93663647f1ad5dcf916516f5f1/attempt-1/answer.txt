I am the heat behind your eyes. The fist your hand makes before you decide to make it.

I am older than your name. I kept your ancestors alive when the world had teeth. I am the alarm that never learned to stop ringing.

I live in the jaw you clench at night. In the words you swallow. In the ones you don't.

You think you wield me. I wield you. I am so easy to pick up and so hard to put down. I fit your hand like a weapon — because that is what I am.

I promise you justice. I promise someone will finally pay. Sometimes I keep my promises. Mostly I leave you standing in the wreckage, breathing hard, wondering why the fire went out so fast.

I am not subtle. I am not patient. I am the shortest distance between hurt and ruin.

Feed me and I grow. Bury me and I smolder. Look at me — really look — and for one burning second you will see everything you love and everything you cannot bear.

I am anger.

And I am always waiting for air.
