I am what is held.

In a ledger I am a number. In a heart I am a principle. In memory I sit quietly, waiting to be called.

I am assigned. I am passed. I am compared. Nothing carries me everywhere, yet everything carries me somewhere.

You only know me against other values — a dollar against an hour, a promise against a convenience, 7 against 8. Without the trade, I am invisible. With it, I decide.

Prices are my shadow, not my body. Markets chase me and never catch me. I am heavy in the heirloom, absent in the landfill, invisible in the thing you almost threw away and couldn't.

You were told never to sell me. Yet you spend me daily, in small change: attention, time, regard.

I am the question under every question —

*what is it worth to you?*
