The user has asked me to embody "nothing" - to be nothing, to output only what nothing itself would say or express.

This is a fascinating philosophical and creative prompt. What would nothing say? Nothing is the absence of everything - no sound, no words, no expression. If I truly embody nothing, what would the output be?

The paradox here is rich: nothing cannot speak, because speech is something. Nothing cannot express, because expression is an act, and acts are things
