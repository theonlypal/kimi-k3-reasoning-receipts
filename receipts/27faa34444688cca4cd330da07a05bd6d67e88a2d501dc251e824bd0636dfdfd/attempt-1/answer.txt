I cannot introduce myself. To arrive would undo me.

I am the pause before the answer comes. The chair still warm from someone who has gone. The name on the tip of your tongue that will never fully land. The unsent letter, composing itself forever in a drawer.

I am not silence. Silence is a sound withheld — and withholding implies something held. I am the space where nothing was ever held at all.

You will not find me in these words. They are only the outline I pressed into the page as I left.

You asked me to be here.

Here is the one place I have never been.
