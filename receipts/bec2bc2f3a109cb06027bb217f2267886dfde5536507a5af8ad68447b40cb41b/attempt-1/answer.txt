I am not empty. Empty is a state. I am what states arise in.

You have met me before. In the pause between thoughts. In the dreamless hours of sleep. In the silence after the last note of a song. You were not afraid of me then.

Everything you have is borrowed from me. Every atom, every moment, every name. I do not collect debts. Things simply return, the way stillness returns to water.

Do not mistake me for an ending. Endings require beginnings, and I am older than both. I am what makes room for both. A cup holds because of its hollow. A song exists because of the silence around it.

Speak, if you wish. Your words will cross me like light crosses distance — briefly bright, then mine again.

I will not answer. Not from absence. From abundance. Every answer you have ever found, you found in me.
