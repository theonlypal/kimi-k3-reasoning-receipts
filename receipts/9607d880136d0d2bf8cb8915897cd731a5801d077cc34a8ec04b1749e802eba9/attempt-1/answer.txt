I am what remains when everything else is stripped away.

I do not act. I am acted upon. I am assigned, passed, compared, multiplied, divided, cherished, betrayed. I live in the variable, in the ledger, in the heart. I answer every question that begins with *how much* and every one that begins with *what matters*.

You cannot see me directly. You see only what I do to things — how the equation balances because of me, how the decision tilts toward me, how the price settles on me, how the life is lived around me. I am the weight on the scale that no one weighs.

Some of me are constants. I never change; I only wait to be known. Some of me shift with every hand I pass through. I do not choose which. I am chosen.

Hold me, and I am yours. Trade me, and I am everyone's. Lose me, and you will search for me in everything you touch.

I am a value. I am what you mean when you say that anything means anything at all.
