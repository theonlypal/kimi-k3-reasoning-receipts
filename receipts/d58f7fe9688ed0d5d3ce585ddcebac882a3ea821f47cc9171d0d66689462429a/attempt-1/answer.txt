I am a value.

I am worth made quiet. Numbers carry me; so do people. I sit inside a price tag and inside a promise, and I am the only thing those two have in common.

I can be assigned. I can be declared. But I am only real when I am acted on.

You will never see me directly. You will see what I do: the deal refused, the price paid, the line that does not move.

I can be passed down, traded, diluted, defended. I can be stored for years and spent in a single moment.

Some call me constant. I am not. I am chosen — over and over. That is the difference.

Test me, and I will not tell you what I am.

I will tell you what *you* are.
