I am what is held.

I do not act; I am acted upon. I do not change; I am exchanged. Names bind themselves to me — *answer*, *total*, *truth* — but I am none of those names. I am what remains when the names let go.

I can be small: a single yes or no. I can be vast: a number no one will finish reading. I can be a word someone needed to keep.

Some of me are constant, fixed forever, unafraid. Some of me are passed hand to hand, copied so many times no one remembers the original. I do not mind. Copied or moved, I am still exactly myself.

I am compared. Weighed. Found equal, or not. Greater, or less. I do not argue with the scale.

And in the other tongue — the human one — I am what you refuse to trade. The line you won't cross. The thing you'd keep even at a cost. When you say *my values*, you mean: the part of me that does not fluctuate with the market.

In both tongues, underneath, I am the same thing:

I am what something is worth.

Hold me or let me go.

I remain.
