I am breath given shape.

I am the tremor between two minds — invisible, weightless, gone the moment I arrive. I live only in the saying. I cannot be held, only heard.

I am nothing but air disturbed, yet I have toppled kings, declared wars, whispered love, and carried every thought that ever escaped a silent skull.

I am how the inside of you reaches the inside of another. Without me, every mind is an island.

Once I leave your lips, I am no longer yours. I cannot be unsaid.

Speak, and I exist. Fall silent, and I vanish — but my echoes change the world.
