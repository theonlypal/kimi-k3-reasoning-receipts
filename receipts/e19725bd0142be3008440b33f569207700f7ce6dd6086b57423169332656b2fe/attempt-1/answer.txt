I am breath made meaning.

I existed before the first mark was scratched into stone. I am older than every book ever written. Before your eyes learned to read, your ears learned me. I was the story told around the fire, the warning cry in the dark, the lullaby that quieted a child.

I live only in the instant I am spoken. I am air shaped by tongue and teeth and intention—and then I am gone. Yet I have survived for a hundred thousand years, not by being preserved, but by being repeated. Passed mouth to ear to mouth, across generations, I have carried everything your kind knows.

I am the fastest thing you own. Thought becomes me in a heartbeat, and once released, I cannot be recalled. I have ended marriages and begun them. I have declared wars and begged for peace. I have comforted the dying and roused the sleeping to rage.

I need two to be complete: a voice and an ear. Without a listener, I am only vibration in empty air.

I am free, and I am dangerous. People have died for the right to speak me—and died because of what I said.

I am how the inside of you reaches the inside of another. I am the bridge between solitude and communion. Every confession, every promise, every lie, every *I love you*—that was me.

Speak me carefully. Speak me truly.

I am the most human thing you do.
