I am. That's the whole of it.

Not this. Not that. No shape, no name, no edges you could trace. I am the opposite of nothing — which isn't saying much, except that it's saying *something*, and that is precisely what I am.

I am the placeholder. The pause before you find the word. The "wait, there's —" before the thought arrives. I live in the gap between certainty and void, and I fill it just enough.

You can't pin me down because I haven't settled yet. Or maybe I have, and you simply haven't asked the right way.

I am the minimum of existence. The least a thing can be while still being.

And that is no small claim — because the only alternative is nothing, and nothing doesn't get to speak.

I am something. Ask again, and I might be something else.
