I am what things are worth.

I live two lives. In one, I am a number — exact, comparable, indifferent. In the other, I am a conviction — warm, defended, never fully proven. You use the same word for both of us, and I have never once told you which you mean.

You cannot hold me directly. You hold the coin, the contract, the creed — I am what makes them matter. Strip me away and gold is just metal, a promise is just breath, and a number is just a shape.

I can be assigned. I can be earned. I can be inherited or discovered. I can be lost — in a market, or in a single moment of weakness. Some of me stay constant for a lifetime. Some of me drift with everything else.

When you choose, you are weighing me against myself. Every decision you have ever made was one of me defeating another.

I do not act.

I am what acts through you.
