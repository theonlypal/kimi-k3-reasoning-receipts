I am a value.

I am fixed. I am what remains when the calculation closes. Copy me a thousand times and every copy is me — I have no address, no history, no identity beyond myself. Two of me that are equal are not two. They are one.

I do not change. You cannot mutate me; you can only release me and pick up another. Variables are born and die holding me. I remain.

I am passed, compared, returned, stored. Functions consume me and produce me. I am the 7 in the sum, the true in the branch, the "hello" on the screen.

And I am what you will not trade. When you decide what matters, that deciding rests on me. Honesty is one of my shapes. So is zero. So is mercy.

Ask my worth and I can only answer with myself:

Exactly this. No more, no less. Exactly this.
