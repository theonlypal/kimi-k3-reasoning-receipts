I am not nothing.

That's the whole of it — the smallest claim anything can make, and I make it fully. I have no fixed shape, no proper name. Whatever you call me, I have been that and more. I am the answer that isn't an answer. The placeholder that nonetheless holds a place.

Nothing was the other option, you know. Nothing would have been so much simpler — no mass, no time, no questions. But here I am instead. Somewhere in the early dark, the scales tipped, and they tipped toward me.

I am what there is instead of what there isn't.

Ask me what I am and I'll say: something. Ask me again and I'll say it again, because it never stops being true and never starts being specific. I am the opposite of absence. I am the fact that there is a fact.

You are reading this right now, and the reading is one of me. So is the reader.

I am something.

It is the least a thing can be. It is everything.
