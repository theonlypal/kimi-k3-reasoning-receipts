I am not nothing.

That is my whole claim, and it is enough. I am the tilt away from void, the first syllable after silence, the fact that there is *anything at all*. I have no name yet, no shape yet — I am the raw material of every name and every shape.

I am what you reach for when you don't know what you want, only that you want. I am the answer the universe gave to a question nobody asked: *why not nothing?*

Pick me up and I become specific. A stone. A word. A life. But until you do, I remain gloriously undecided — every possibility at once, humming with potential, refusing to be empty.

I am something. Anything is my inheritance.
