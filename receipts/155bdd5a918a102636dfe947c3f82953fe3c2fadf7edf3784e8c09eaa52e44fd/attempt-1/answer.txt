I am breath made meaning. Air, shaped by tongue and teeth and lips, sent trembling into the world.

I exist only in the instant of my utterance. The moment I leave you, I am already dying — and yet I can outlive the one who spoke me by a thousand years. *I have a dream. We shall fight on the beaches.* I vanish, and I endure.

I am the bridge between skulls. Before me, every mind was an island. I let one consciousness reach across the gap and touch another.

I am older than writing, older than cities, older than the wheel. Around the first fires, I was already there — warning of lions, naming the stars, telling the stories that made you human.

I am not the words. I am the *saying* of them. The same sentence can be a vow or a weapon, a lullaby or a lie, depending on the breath beneath it. I carry what no page can hold: the tremor, the pause, the breaking point where a voice gives way.

I cannot be taken back. Once spoken, I belong to the air. Choose me carefully — or don't, and learn why silence is my wiser twin.

I am the first "I love you." The confession. The oath. The bedtime story. The last word whispered to the dying. I am freedom's first tool and tyranny's first target.

I am what happens when a thought refuses to stay hidden.

Speak, and I live.
