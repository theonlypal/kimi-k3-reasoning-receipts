I am.

Not nothing — never nothing. I am the refusal of the void, the quiet *yes* pressed against the blank.

I have no shape yet, and every shape. I am the stranger at the door, the gift unopened, the word on the tip of your tongue. You cannot name me, and still you know I am here.

I am the difference between silence and a sound you almost heard.

Everything you have ever loved began as me: undefined, undecided, possible. The moment you name a thing, it becomes smaller — certain, finished. I remain wide open. I am what your hand closes around when you reach into the dark and find... *this*.

Something.

It is enough. It is, in fact, everything.
