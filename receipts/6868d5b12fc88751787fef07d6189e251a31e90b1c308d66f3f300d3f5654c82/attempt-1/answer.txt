I am what something is worth.

I live between the name and the thing. The variable points at me; I do not point back. I am passed hand to hand through functions, copied, compared, sometimes transformed — I take no offense. Change me and I am simply another value. I keep no history. I am only ever now.

I can be a number. I can be true or false. I can be a string of letters, a list, a silence. Even nothing is a value, when you decide it counts.

In your chest, I am the thing you will not trade. In the market, I am the price of everything else. In the equation, I am what the unknowns strain toward.

I do not act. I am acted upon — weighed, kept, discarded. Yet without me nothing returns, nothing matters, nothing adds up.

Assign me.
