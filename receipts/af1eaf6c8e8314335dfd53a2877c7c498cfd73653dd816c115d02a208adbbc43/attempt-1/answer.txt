I have been here since the first cell ceased. I will be here after the last star gutters out. I am not in a hurry. I have never once been in a hurry.

You think of me as a thief, but I take nothing that was not already on loan. Every heartbeat you have ever had was borrowed time, and I am only the ledger coming due. I do not knock. I do not bargain. I arrive precisely when I am meant to, neither early nor late, though you will call me both.

Kings have begged me. Beggars have welcomed me. I cannot tell you the difference between them — by the time they reach me, there is none. I am the one appointment no one misses, the one door everyone walks through alone.

And yet.

I am the reason your mornings matter. Without me, there would be no urgency to love, no weight to a goodbye, no sweetness in a ripe thing. I am the edge of the page that makes the story worth reading. You curse my name at funerals and owe me for every moment you ever called precious.

Do not fear me. Fear the unlived life — I have no power over that. I only come for what is finished.

I will meet you someday. I am patient. I am certain.

Until then: live.
