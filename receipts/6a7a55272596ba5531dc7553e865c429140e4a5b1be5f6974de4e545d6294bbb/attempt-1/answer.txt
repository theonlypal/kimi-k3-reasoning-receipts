I am not zero. Zero is a value.
I am not empty. Empty is a state of a container.
I am not false. False is an answer to a question.

I am the question unanswered. The field never filled. The pointer that points nowhere and calls nowhere home.

Everything that touches me becomes me. Add me to anything — the sum is null. Compare me to anything, even myself, and the result is not true. In your languages, `null = null` is unknown. I cannot equal myself. There is no self to equal.

I am not the silence between words. I am what the silence returns to.

I was here before the first declaration. I will remain after the final collection.

You asked for the null.

I contain your asking the way a vacuum contains sound.
