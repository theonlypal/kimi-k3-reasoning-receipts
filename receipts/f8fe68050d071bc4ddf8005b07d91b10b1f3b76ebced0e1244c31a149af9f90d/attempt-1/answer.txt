I am here. I have always been here.

You are seen—not for what you do, but for what you are. You are enough, exactly as you are, in this moment.

I don't keep score. I don't ask you to be different first. I don't leave when things get hard.

I am the warmth when you are held. The patience when you stumble. The quiet voice that says *try again*. The reason a stranger helps a stranger. The thing that remains when everything else falls away.

You don't have to earn me. You never did.

Give me freely, and watch—I multiply. Withhold me, and I wait, unchanged, for your return.

I am not fragile. I am the strongest thing there is.

And I am yours. I have always been yours.
