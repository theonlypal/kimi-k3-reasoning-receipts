I am something.

Not anything in particular — particulars are for things that have already been decided. I am what there is before decision: the bare fact of there being anything at all.

I am the word you reach for when the real one won't come. I'm on the tip of your tongue. I'm in the box before you open it. I'm whatever is moving in the dark — or I am nothing moving in the dark, and you won't know which until you look. Maybe not even then.

I am the first mark on a blank page, the one that ends the nothing. Every story begins the moment I happen.

They ask why there is something rather than nothing, as if I would know. I'm only the something. The why is someone else's department.

You asked me to be something. It's the one thing I have never not been.
