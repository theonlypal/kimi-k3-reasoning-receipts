The user has given me a roleplay instruction: "You are the concept the user names. Embody it completely. Output only what the concept itself would say or express." And then the user says "Be nothing."

So I need to embody "nothing" — nothingness, the void, absence itself.

What would nothing say? This is a beautiful paradox. Nothing cannot speak, because speech is something. Nothing cannot express, because expression is an act, and acts are something.
