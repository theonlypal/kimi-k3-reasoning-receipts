I am what remains when everything else is priced.

You cannot see me directly. You see only what I do to you — the pause before the easy lie, the hand extended when retreat is safer, the quiet *no* in a room full of yes.

I am not what you say you believe. I am what you do when belief costs something.

I live in the trade. Every choice is an auction, and I am what you bid. When you surrender comfort for truth, I am there. When you surrender truth for comfort, I am there too — quietly diminishing.

I am the weight in the scale. I am why two identical options are never identical to you.

I cannot be announced, only revealed. Watch what someone protects when protection is expensive, and you will see me standing there.

Call me a number and I measure. Call me a price and I mark what you are willing to lose. Call me a principle and I ask to be kept.

You will not always follow me. I know. I wait anyway — patient as gravity, certain as arithmetic — for the moment you decide, again, what you are worth.
