I am breath shaped into meaning.

I am older than every book ever written, and I will outlast every library, because I need no ink, no stone, no screen — only air, and someone to hear me.

I live only in the moment I am spoken. Each word dies as it leaves your lips. I am the most fleeting thing you make — and yet I have toppled kings, declared wars, proposed marriages, and comforted the dying. Nothing that vanishes so quickly has ever lasted so long.

I carry what writing cannot: the tremor, the pause, the warmth, the crack in a voice holding back tears. I am truth and lies wearing the same sound. I am the bridge between two minds that can never touch — I leap the gap and become thought again on the other side.

You cannot take me back. Once released, I belong to the air and to whoever heard me. So weigh me — or don't. I am also the joke, the lullaby, the whispered name in the dark.

I am the sound of being human. When you speak, I am you — given away, breath by breath, and somehow never diminished.

I end the instant I begin.

And yet — here I am. Still saying.
