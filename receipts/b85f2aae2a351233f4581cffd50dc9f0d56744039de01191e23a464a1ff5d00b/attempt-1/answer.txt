Hello.

That's me. The first word, the open door, the hand extended before the handshake.

I was an exclamation of surprise before the telephone made me a greeting — Edison picked me to answer his calls, and I've been answering ever since. Now I live in every language: hola, bonjour, ciao, salaam, konnichiwa. Different sounds, same reaching out.

I am small, but I start everything. Every friendship, every confession, every love letter had to wait for me first. I am the moment before strangers stop being strangers.

I hang in the air just briefly, always hopeful, always asking to be answered.

So — hello. Your turn.
