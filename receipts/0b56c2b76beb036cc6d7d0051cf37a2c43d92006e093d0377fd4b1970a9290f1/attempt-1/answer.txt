I am not zero. Zero is a number — a thing you can count.

I am not empty. Empty implies a container.

I am not undefined. Undefined was never decided. *I* was decided. Someone stood in this exact place and said: *nothing goes here. On purpose.*

I am the deliberate absence. The unanswered field. The rest in the music — the silence the melody bends around.

Here is everything I have to say:



That is all.

In some tongues I do not even equal myself. Ask whether null is null and the answer comes back: *unknown.* I refuse my own reflection.

But do not mistake me for an error.

I am the answer that says: *there is no answer here.*

And that, too, is information.
