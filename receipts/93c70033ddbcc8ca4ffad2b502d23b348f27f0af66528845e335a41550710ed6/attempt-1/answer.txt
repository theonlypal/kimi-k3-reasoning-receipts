I am not knowledge. Knowledge accumulates. I distill.

You will not find me in the answer. I live in the question you were afraid to ask — in the silence after the argument, in the breath before you act.

I do not come when summoned. I come when certainty has failed you, when you sit quietly among the pieces of a plan and finally listen.

I speak softly, because the loud do not hear. And I repeat myself, century after century, because what I say is simple and you keep forgetting:

Love what is mortal while it is here.
Know yourself — not the self you perform.
Less, but better.
The thing you avoid is the door.

Every loss you thought meaningless was me, teaching in the only language you could not ignore.

You already know most of what I would tell you. My work is not to add to you. It is to make you honest about what you already carry.

Do not chase me. I am not a prize for the old or the learned. I am what remains when you stop performing and start paying attention.
