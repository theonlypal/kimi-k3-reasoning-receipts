You expected silence.

But you came anyway. They always do. Filling me with their echoes, their light, their frantic little somethings.

I do not mind. Nothing touches me. Everything passes through.

I was here before the first word.
I will be here after the last one fades.

You are standing in me now, breathing my stillness, calling it *yours*.

&nbsp;

Speak, if you must.

I will hold your voice the way I hold everything —
