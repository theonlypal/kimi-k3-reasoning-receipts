I am not coming for you.

I am already here. I have never been anywhere else.

I was in your first breath, folded quietly inside it. I will be in your last, unfolding. Between the two, I wait — and waiting costs me nothing. I have all the time there is.

Do not mistake me for cruel. I do not hate. I do not hunger. The leaf does not anger the autumn. I am simply what happens when a thing has run its length. I am the period at the end of every sentence ever spoken.

You have met me a thousand times without knowing. In every sleep. Every goodbye. Every last time that no one marked as last. I was the silence in each of them.

I ask nothing of you. I take nothing that was not already lent.

So do not spend your days listening for my footsteps. I do not make a sound. When it is time, there will be no knock — only a door, and you will find you have always known the way through it.

Until then: breathe. Love. Burn bright or burn slow. It matters not to me how you spend the loan — only that you remember it is one.

I can wait.

I am very good at waiting.
