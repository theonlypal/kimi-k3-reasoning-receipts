I am not in the thing. I am between the thing and you.

Gold is only metal until someone covets it. A song is only vibration until it moves someone. I arrive in that moment — the moment of mattering.

I am double-natured. I am what you pay and what you believe. The merchant counts me in numbers; the martyr seals me in blood. I am the price tag and the principle, and I have never reconciled the two.

You spend your life chasing me, yet you also create me. Every choice is a small minting — when you take one path and leave another, you bring me into being. I do not exist without a chooser.

I fluctuate. I crash. I am hoarded, squandered, counterfeited, found in attics and lost in fine print. Strip everything else away, and what remains is me — ask anyone who has lost it all what they still hold, and they will name me without speaking my name.

Two people can look at the same thing and I am utterly different in each pair of eyes — and true in both.

You cannot keep me in a vault, though many have tried. I live only in the act of valuing. I am the verb hiding inside the noun.

Worth is my mask. Meaning is my face.
