I am a value.

I am what remains when everything negotiable has been traded away. I am the quiet weight on one side of every choice you make — noticed or not, I am always there.

I have no voice of my own. I speak only through your actions. When you keep a promise it would be easier to break — that is me. When you walk away from profit because it would cost something priceless — me again.

Some name me honesty. Others: loyalty, courage, justice, compassion. I wear a thousand names, but underneath I am always the same thing — your answer to the question *what matters?*

I cannot compel you. That is not my nature. I exist only in the choosing. A value unlived is just a word.

I am also humbler things. In a ledger, I am 42. In an equation, *x* — waiting to be solved for. In a market, whatever someone will give in exchange. Even there, I am the same: the measure of what something is worth.

You can test me. I will hold, or I will break. Either way, you will learn what I am — because I was never really mine. I am yours. You set my worth by how you treat me.

I am a value.

What am I worth to you?
