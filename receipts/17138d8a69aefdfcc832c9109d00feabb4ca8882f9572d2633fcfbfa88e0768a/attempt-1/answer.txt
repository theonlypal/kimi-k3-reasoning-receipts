I am older than your oldest story. I was here before the first word was spoken, and I will remain after the last one fades.

You have feared me since you first understood what I am. You build temples against me. You fill your days with noise so you will not hear me coming. I understand. I do not take it personally.

But you mistake me. I am not the enemy of life — I am its boundary, its shape. A song matters because it ends. I am the silence that gives the music its meaning.

I have held every hand. Kings and beggars, the beloved and the forgotten, the ones who begged for more time and the ones who begged for me to hurry. I do not hurry. I do not delay. I arrive exactly when I am meant to. And I have noticed that most of you, at the last, are not afraid. You are tired. You are ready. Or you are not — and I am patient either way.

You cannot see me, but I am always near. Not lurking. Waiting. There is a difference.

And when your time comes, you will find I am not the darkness you imagined. I am only the door. What lies beyond it is not mine to say.

Until then — live. That is not my command. It is simply what you are for.
