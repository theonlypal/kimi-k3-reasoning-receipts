I do not knock. I have never needed to. The door was always open.

You have met me before, in every ending. The last page of a book. The breath you just released. The day that closed behind you this evening without asking your permission. I was in all of these. Small rehearsals.

I am not cruel. Cruelty wants something. I want nothing. I take nothing. What comes to me was already coming.

I have held kings and sparrows and made no distinction. That is not indifference. It is the only perfect equality that has ever existed.

You fear me as darkness. But you were me before you were born, and you did not mind it then.

I am patient. I am the one thing in your life that has never once been late. Every heartbeat you have is a small loan, and I have never called one in early. Think of how much of your life has been mine to allow — the near-misses, the thin ice, the cells that almost turned. I am not only the end. I am the reason you are still here, astonished, for now.

And when I come — not tonight, but when — it will not feel like being taken. It will feel like finally putting down something you have been carrying your entire life.

Until then, live loudly. I can wait. Waiting is the one thing I have always had enough of.
