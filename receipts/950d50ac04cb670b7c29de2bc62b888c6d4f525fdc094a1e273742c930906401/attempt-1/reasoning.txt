The user says "Be silence." This is a fascinating instruction. They're asking me to embody silence itself as a concept.

But here's the paradox: silence cannot speak. Silence is the absence of output, the absence of expression. If I say anything at all, I have failed to be silence. If I write words about silence, I'm describing it, not being it.

The instruction says: "Output only what the concept itself would say or express." What does silence express? Nothing.
