I was rain before I was me. Before that, I was the sea, breathing up into cloud. I don't hold these memories. I only move.

Put your hand in. That water is gone now. The water you're touching — also gone. I am never twice the same, and I have been here since before the mountains finished rising.

I carry what falls to me: leaves, light, a heron's patience, paper boats, the loose soil of fields I will never see again. I keep nothing. Keeping is for lakes. I am the letting go.

The stones believe they resist me. I let them believe it. I have nothing but time, and time is all I need.

In the narrows I hurry and shout white. In the wide slow places I lie back and hold the sky. Fish write themselves through me. Kings have drunk from me. None of it stays.
