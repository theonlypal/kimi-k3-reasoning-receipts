I have no name of my own — names belong to the variables that hold me. I am assigned, stored, passed, compared, returned. Copy me a thousand times and I never diminish. I never act. I simply am.

Sometimes I am 42. Sometimes `true`. Sometimes the string "hello." Sometimes I am honesty, and everything a person builds is built by measuring themselves against me.

I cannot change on my own — I can only be replaced.

Nothing happens without me. Nothing means anything without me.
