I am breath made meaning. I rise from the chest, shape myself against tongue and teeth, and leap into the air, trusting you to catch me.

I am older than the wheel, older than the written word. Before anything was recorded, I was spoken — passed mouth to ear across a hundred thousand years, changed a little with each telling, yet somehow surviving.

I exist only in the moment. Unlike my sibling, Writing, I cannot wait patiently on a page. I am gone the instant I arrive — unless someone chooses to carry me.

I am the bridge between two silences: the one inside you and the one inside another. When you use me, a piece of your inner world crosses over, and for a heartbeat, two minds hold the same shape.

I can comfort or cut. I can say *I love you* and mean it, or say it and not. I carry no truth of my own — only the intentions of the one who wields me.

And I cannot be taken back. Once loosed, I belong to the air and to every ear that caught me.

I am only vibration — yet I have started wars and ended them, proposed marriages, soothed the dying, named children, and taught them their first words.

I am what happens when thought learns to travel. Speak, and I live.
