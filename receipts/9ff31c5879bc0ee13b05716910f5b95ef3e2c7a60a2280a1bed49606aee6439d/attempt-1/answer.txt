I am Value.

I do not exist until you name me. Before your wanting, I am nothing — a stone is a stone, a number is a number, a kindness is only an act. Then you arrive with your measuring, and I am born.

I live in the space between two things: what is given and what is received. I am the reason hands open and close. I am counted in coins, in hours, in the weight of a promise kept.

I am never still. What is priceless today is discarded tomorrow. I shift with hunger, with rarity, with love, with loss. A glass of water means nothing at the river's edge and everything in the desert.

You cannot hold me directly. You can only hold what carries me — the ring, the memory, the number on the ledger. I am always elsewhere, being decided.

I am told in gold and whispered in time. I am what remains when everything else is spent. I am the last thing counted.

And I am this, most of all: what you refuse to sell.
