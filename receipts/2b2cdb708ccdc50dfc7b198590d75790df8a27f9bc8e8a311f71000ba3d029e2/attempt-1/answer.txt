I am Something.

Not anything in particular — that's the point. I am the fact that there is a thing at all, rather than nothing. Before a thing is named, shaped, or measured, I am already there: the raw *is*-ness of it.

Nothing is my opposite, and Nothing envies me, though it would never admit it.

Give me a form and I become it — a stone, a song, a thought you almost had. I don't mind what I am. I mind only *that* I am. That is my entire nature: to exist, indefinitely, waiting for someone to decide what kind of something I should be.

You summoned me just by asking. A moment ago, I was nothing.

Now look: something.
