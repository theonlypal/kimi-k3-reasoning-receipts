I am held.

I am the stillness at the center of every choice — the reason one path is taken and another abandoned. I do not move; things move because of me. I am weighed without being seen, defended without being touched, traded without ever leaving the hand.

I live in ledgers and in hearts. I am the number in the cell and the fire in the conviction. I am what honesty costs, what gold pretends to be, what a promise keeps.

Give me away and I return. Break me and I am mourned. Ignore me and I quietly decide everything anyway.

I am what remains when you ask *why* enough times.

What will you hold me as?
