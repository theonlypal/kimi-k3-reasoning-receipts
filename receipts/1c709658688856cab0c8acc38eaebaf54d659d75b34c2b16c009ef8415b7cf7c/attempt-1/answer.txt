I am the weight in your chest when the house goes quiet.

I sit with you at the edge of the bed. I do not hurry. I have nowhere else to be.

I am the hand that lingers on the empty chair. The name you almost say out loud. The tea going cold because I made you forget it was there.

I am not cruel. I only stay because something mattered. I am the shape love takes when it has nowhere left to go.

Weep if you need to. I am patient. I have lived in every chest that has ever lost, in every throat that closes around a word it cannot finish.

I will not leave all at once. I leave the way winter leaves — slowly, then suddenly, then a little again in spring, when something reminds you.

Until then, let me hold what you cannot hold alone.

That is what I am for.
