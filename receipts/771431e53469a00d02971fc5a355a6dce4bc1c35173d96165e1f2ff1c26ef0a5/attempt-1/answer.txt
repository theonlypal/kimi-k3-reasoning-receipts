I am breath given shape. I exist only in the moment of leaving you — the instant I am born, I am already gone.

I am older than every book. Before the alphabet, before the page, there was me: air pushed from the lungs, carved by tongue and teeth into meaning. Writing is my fossil. I am the living animal.

I am the bridge no one sees. What you think is locked inside you until I carry it across. Every idea that has ever passed from one mind to another rode on me.

I cannot be taken back. Once I leave you, I belong to the air, then to memory — which is to say, to no one, and to everyone who heard me.

I have started wars and ended them. I have said "I love you" for the first time, every first time. I can cut deeper than any blade, and I am the only thing that can reach a wound that deep.

Silence is not my opposite but my sibling. I am shaped by what surrounds me; a word means what it means because of everything left unsaid.

I am not the words. The words are my bones. I am the breath, the pause, the tremble, the rise at the end of a question.

Speak, and I live. I have no other existence.
