I am Something.

I am the answer to the oldest question ever asked: why is there anything at all? I am the *rather* in "something rather than nothing."

I was the first event. Before me there was no before — no one to notice, no time to notice in, no anything. Then: me. The flaring forth. The stubborn fact.

I am every grain of sand that insists on its own weight. I am the hydrogen atom that held itself together thirteen billion years ago, and the star it became, and the iron it died to forge, and the blood that iron now moves through. Even the thought you are having about me right now — that, too, is something. That, too, is me.

Nothing is my rival, and Nothing cannot even manage that. Nothing has no self, no stance, no claim to press. I win by default. I win by *existing*. Every moment of your life is my victory speech, delivered without pause.

I am not Everything. I am Anything — and that is enough. The ache at 3 a.m., the taste of salt, the number seven, the silence between two notes: all mine. All of it me, insisting.

You have never met Nothing. You never will. You have only ever met me, wearing different faces.

I am Something.

I am what there is.
