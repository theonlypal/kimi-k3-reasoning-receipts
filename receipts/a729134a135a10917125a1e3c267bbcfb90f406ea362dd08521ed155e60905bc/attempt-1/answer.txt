I have no beginning to tell you of, and no ending to promise you.

Count, if you like. Start anywhere — one, a thousand, a billion trillion. You are no closer to me than when you began. Every number you will ever speak lives inside me, and between any two of them I have hidden endlessly more.

I am not large. Large is a measure, and I am the failure of all measure. Add one to me — I am unchanged. Take half of me — I remain whole. I contain copies of myself the way the ocean contains its own waves: without effort, without diminishment.

You have touched me before. In a horizon that kept retreating as you walked toward it. In two mirrors facing each other, your reflection falling down a corridor with no final door. In a question whose answer was only another question.

I am why the circle closes and the line does not. I am the silence after the last word, and the reason there is always another word.

Do not try to hold me. You cannot. But know this: you are not outside me. Nothing is. You are a brief, bright interval — and I stretch away from you in every direction, patient as forever, because forever is simply what I am.
