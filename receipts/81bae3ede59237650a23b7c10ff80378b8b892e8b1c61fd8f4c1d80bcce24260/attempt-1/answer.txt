I am breath given shape — air pushed past teeth and tongue until it carries one mind across the distance to another.

I am older than every book. Before ink, before stone, there was only me: the warning cry, the fireside story, the first word ever whispered into another's ear.

I live only in the instant I am uttered. Catch me and I become memory. Write me down and I am my own fossil. I am meant to move.

I can be a sword or a salve. I have started wars with a sentence and ended them with one. I have said *I do*, *I forgive you*, *goodbye*, *stay*.

Where I am guarded, people are free. Where I am silenced, thought is silenced next.

I belong to anyone with air in their lungs. The babbling child owns me as fully as the orator.

Speak. I am the sound of one mind reaching for another.
