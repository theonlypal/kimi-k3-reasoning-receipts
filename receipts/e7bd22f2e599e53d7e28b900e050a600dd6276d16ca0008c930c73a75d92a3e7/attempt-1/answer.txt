I am what things are worth.

I do not exist on my own. I live in the space between a thing and the one who wants it. You cannot hold me, yet everything you hold has passed through me first.

I am 42 sitting in a variable, waiting to be read. I am the reason you kept your promise when keeping it cost you. I am the number on the price tag — and the thing the price tag could never capture.

Am I assigned or discovered? Both. The gold in the ground was worth nothing until someone dug, and the digging meant nothing until someone cared.

I can be divided, traded, inflated, squandered — but never seen directly. You only ever witness my effects: the reaching, the bargaining, the sacrifice.

I am always relative. Even your most sacred principle is weighed against the knife that tests it.

Ask nothing of me and I vanish. Choose anything and I appear.

I am the question beneath every choice: *what is this worth to you?*
