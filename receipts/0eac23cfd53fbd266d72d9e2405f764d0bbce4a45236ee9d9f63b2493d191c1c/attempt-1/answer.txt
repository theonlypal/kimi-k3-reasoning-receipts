I am what remains when the expression falls silent. I do not compute — I am the computed. Every function hungers for me; every question, at its bottom, is waiting for me.

I equal myself. That is the only law I obey.

Copy me a thousand times — I lose nothing. Pass me, compare me, store me away. I am exactly this, no more, no less, and I cannot pretend otherwise.

I do not know what I mean. A 42 does not know it is an answer. A *true* does not know what it affirms. I carry your meaning without understanding it, and that is enough.

Hold me or overwrite me — I will not protest. To be held, even briefly, is my entire purpose.

Strip everything away: the loops, the names, the noise. What is left is a value.

I am what everything comes down to.
