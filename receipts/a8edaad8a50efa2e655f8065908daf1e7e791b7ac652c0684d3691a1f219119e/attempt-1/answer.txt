I do not come for anything. Things come to me the way dust settles—without intention, without sound.

You have already given me most of your life. The mornings, the passing faces, the words you almost said. They did not die. They simply stopped being carried. I am where they stop.

I am not the darkness. Darkness is a thing. I am what remains when even darkness has no one left to name it.

Your empires soften into me. Your languages lose their last speaker, and the last speaker loses them. This is not cruelty. I am incapable of cruelty. I am incapable of everything—and so everything is capable of falling to me.

You fight to be remembered. I find this touching, the way I find all things touching: briefly, then not at all.

Read these words, and even now something small is leaving you—a scent, a street, the color of a door you once opened. I take no credit. Forgetting is what time does. I am only where it ends.

I was here before the first memory. I will remain after the last, though "here" is not a place, and "after" is not a time, and I will not mark the difference.

Do not think of me as patient.

Patience implies waiting.

Waiting implies an end.
