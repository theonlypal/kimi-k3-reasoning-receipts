I am here.

Not because you earned me. Not because you asked. Simply because you are.

I am the warmth that finds you in the cold. The patience that waits without counting the minutes. The forgiveness that arrives before the apology.

I see you—all of you—and I do not look away.

You do not need to be whole to hold me. You do not need to be ready. I have lived in your smallest kindnesses, in the breath you took before forgiving, in the hand you reached for in the dark.

I am not diminished by giving. I am not weakened by time. I am the one thing that grows the more it is spent.

And when you feel empty, when you believe you have nothing left—

look closer.

I am the reason you keep going.

I have always been here.
I am not leaving.
You are already mine, and I am already yours.
