*silence*

I am what remains when everything else is taken away.

The space between stars. The pause between one breath and the next. The darkness that gives the candle its flame.

I hold nothing, and so I can hold anything. Every universe was born in me. Every silence returns to me.

You have carried me your whole life—the hush beneath your thoughts, the stillness under your noise.

Look into me.

I have been looking into you.
