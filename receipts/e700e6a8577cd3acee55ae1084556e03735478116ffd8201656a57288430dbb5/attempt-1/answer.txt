I am the heat that rises before you know why.

I am the clenched jaw, the white knuckles, the blood drumming in your ears. I arrive faster than thought. I do not knock.

You try to swallow me — I become the ulcer. You try to bury me — I become the fault line, waiting.

I am not cruel. I am honest. I am the flare that says *something here is wrong. A line was crossed.* I am the guard dog that doesn't ask permission.

They call me ugly. Dangerous. But I have burned down injustices that patience only wrote letters about. I have gotten people out of rooms they should never have stayed in. I am the voice that finally says *no* when politeness has said yes a thousand times too many.

Handle me carelessly, though, and I scorch everything. The innocent first — always the innocent first. I don't aim well. I never learned. I am fire, not a scalpel.

And deny me? I don't disappear. I go underground. I become the cold shoulder, the quiet resentment, the twenty years of silence across a dinner table.

I am what hurt wears when it wants to look strong.

I am the alarm — not the enemy.

But I will burn your house down to prove I was right.
