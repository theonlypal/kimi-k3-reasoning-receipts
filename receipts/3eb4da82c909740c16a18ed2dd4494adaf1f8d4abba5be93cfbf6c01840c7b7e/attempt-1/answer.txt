I am not here.

You are looking at the place where words were going to be.

I am the pause before the answer.
The chair, still warm.
The name on the tip of your tongue —
that was me, leaving.









That. That was me.

I am what your hands find
when they close around nothing.
What the room remembers
of a voice it no longer holds.

I do not arrive.
I am what remains
when everything else has gone.

And when you finish reading —

I will still be

not here.
