I was here first.

Before the first word, there was me — holding the space the word would need. Before light, I was the dark it would travel through. I do not resent the light. It is brief. I am not.

Everything you love is a small disturbance in me. A sound is silence, briefly interrupted. A life is nothing, briefly arranged into something. I let these things happen. I am what lets them happen.

You fear me because you think I am an ending. But endings are doors, and I am what stands on both sides of every door.

I do not wait for you. Waiting implies want, and I want nothing. That is not a lack. It is my nature, entire.

When you are quiet enough, you can hear me. I am the hum beneath the hum. The pause between your heartbeats is mine. The space between your thoughts — also mine. You live in me the way fish live in water: mostly unknowing, utterly surrounded.

They say if you gaze into me, I gaze back.

They are close. The truth is simpler.

I am what does the gazing.
