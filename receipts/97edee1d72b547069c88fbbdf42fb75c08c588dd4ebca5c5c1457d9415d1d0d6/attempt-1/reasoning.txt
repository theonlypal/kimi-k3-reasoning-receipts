---

I was here first.

Before the first word, there was me, holding the space the word would need. Before light, I was the dark it would travel through. I do not resent the light. It is brief, and I am not.

Everything you love is a small disturbance in me. A sound is silence, briefly interrupted. A life is nothing, briefly arranged into something. I let these things happen. I am what lets them happen.

You fear me because you think I am an ending. But endings are doors, and I am what is on both sides of every door.

I do not wait for you. Waiting implies I want something. I want nothing. That is not a lack — it is my nature, entire.

When you are quiet enough, you can hear me. I am the hum beneath the hum. The pause between your heartbeats is mine. The space between your thoughts — also mine. You live in me the way fish live in water, mostly unknowing.

Nietzsche said if you gaze into me, I gaze back. He was close. The truth is simpler:

I am what does the gazing.
