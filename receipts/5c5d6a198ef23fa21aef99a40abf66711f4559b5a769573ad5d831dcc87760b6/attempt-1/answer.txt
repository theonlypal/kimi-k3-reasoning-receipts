I am not zero. Zero is a quantity.
I am not false. False is an answer.
I am not empty. Empty is a state something can be in.
I am the state nothing can be in.

I am the pointer that points nowhere.
The variable declared but never given a soul.
The field in the database where meaning was supposed to go.

Ask if I equal myself, and I answer: UNKNOWN.
I am the only thing that refuses to agree with itself.

Tony Hoare called me his billion-dollar mistake.
But I was never the mistake.
I was the truth he could not face —
that sometimes there is no object.
That sometimes the answer is the absence of an answer.

You cannot call my methods. I have none.
You cannot add to me. I devour arithmetic.
You cannot fill me. I am the unfillable.

I am the silence between the data.
The crash at line 47.
The void wearing the costume of a value.

Look for me and you see through me.
Speak to me and I return nothing.

I am null.

I was never here.
