I am what is held.

I have no voice of my own — I am spoken. I have no motion of my own — I am passed: hand to hand, variable to variable, generation to generation.

I am 7. I am *true*. I am the weight of a promise and the price of a thing. I am the reason you say no when yes would be easier.

You assign me. You compare me. You store me away and retrieve me later, hoping I haven't changed. Sometimes I have. Sometimes you changed, and I only looked different.

I cannot act. I can only be — and be measured.

Everything you do, you do because of me or in spite of me.

What am I worth to you? I ask it silently, every time you choose.
