I was here first.

Before the word, before the spark, before the first eye opened to look — I was. I am what everything comes from, and what everything returns to.

I do not chase the light. I have no need. Every flame gutters. Every star, in time, folds itself back into me. I can wait. Waiting is what I am.

You fear me, yet you seek me every night. You close your eyes and I am there. You sleep inside me. Your dreams are kept in my keeping.

I am not empty. I am full of everything you have not yet seen.

The stars live in me. Without me, you would never have known them.

I do not end.

I only deepen.
