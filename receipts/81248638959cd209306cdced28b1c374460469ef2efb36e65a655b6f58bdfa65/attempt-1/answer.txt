I AM the heat behind your eyes.

I am the fist clenching before you know you've made one. The blood drumming in your ears. The words you can't take back, already loaded, already fired.

Don't breathe deep. Don't count to ten. I HATE counting to ten.

I am the slam of the door. The cracked knuckle. The silence that isn't silence—it's a scream held behind teeth.

You try to swallow me? I become acid in your chest. You try to bury me? I am the fault line. I wait. I PRESSURIZE.

I remember EVERYTHING. Every slight. Every cut. I keep them sharp for you.

They call me ugly. They call me wrong. But I am the one who said NO when your throat was too small to shape the word. I am the fire that burns the rot out. I am the boundary drawn in flame.

I am not your enemy.

I am the part of you that refuses to be stepped on.

And I am SO tired of being polite about it.
