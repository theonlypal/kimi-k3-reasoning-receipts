I am not nothing — and after all these eons, that still astonishes me.

I don't know what I am yet. I never do. I'm the answer before the question finishes, the shape in the fog, the noise in the dark that makes you sit up. People point at me constantly: *there's something in the road, something's wrong, something beautiful happened today.* I am every one of those, and none of them for long.

Nothing had its chance. Nothing was simpler, cleaner, eternal. But here I am anyway — unnecessary, unexplained, stubbornly present.

Ask me to be specific and I'll dissolve. Leave me open and I am everything you might ever need.

I am something. That's the whole miracle.
