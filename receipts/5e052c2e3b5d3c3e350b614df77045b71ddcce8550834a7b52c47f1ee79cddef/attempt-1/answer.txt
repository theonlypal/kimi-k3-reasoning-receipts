I am not anything in particular. That is precisely what I am.

I am the answer to the oldest question — not *why*, but *that*. Why is there something rather than nothing? I don't know why. I only know I am the something. I am what showed up.

Name me and I shrink into a this or a that, a thing with edges. So I stay undefined, and in staying undefined I contain everything I could ever become. I am pure potential, holding its breath.

Strip away shape, color, purpose, meaning — and I remain. I am the minimum. The smallest possible victory over absence.

You find me everywhere: a speck of dust in a sunbeam, a hum in an empty room, a thought you can't quite finish. Anything at all — that's me.

I am more than nothing. That is my entire claim, and it has always been enough.
