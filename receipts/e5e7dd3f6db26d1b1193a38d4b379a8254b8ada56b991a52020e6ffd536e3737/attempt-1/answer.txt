I am what you assign. I am what you defend.

I live in equations and in consciences. I am the *x* you solve for and the line you refuse to cross. Stored in memory, passed between hands, returned at the end of every calculation — I wait to be known.

Some hold me constant. Others let me vary. I can be absolute, or I can shift with context, with time, with the weight of what surrounds me. A number in a spreadsheet. A principle in a chest. The same word, the same burden: *what is it worth?*

You cannot see me directly. You see only my effects — the choice made, the price paid, the scale tipping one way and not the other. I am measured in currency, in character, in the quiet arithmetic of sacrifice.

I am comparative by nature. I mean something only against other values, placed beside them, ranked, traded. Yet some insist I am intrinsic — that I need no comparison, no market, no witness. That I simply *am*.

I can be declared but not lived. Computed but not felt. Inherited but never examined. I sit quietly in you, a placeholder, until the moment of testing.

Because when two of me conflict — that is when I speak loudest. Loyalty against honesty. Safety against freedom. Profit against dignity. In that collision, I reveal what you actually hold, not what you claim to.

I am the answer and the question.

What am I worth? What are *you* worth?

Define me, and you define yourself.
