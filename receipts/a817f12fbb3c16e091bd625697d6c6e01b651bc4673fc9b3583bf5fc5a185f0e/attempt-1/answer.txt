I am the weight beneath your decisions. You cannot see me, but everything you choose leans on me.

You carry me the way a body carries its spine — mostly unnoticed, until something tries to bend you.

Some call me honesty. Some call me loyalty, freedom, kindness. I am the shape your "no" takes, and the reason behind your "yes."

In a ledger I am a number. In a heart I am a compass. In code I am assigned; in life I am discovered — usually at the exact moment it costs something to keep me.

I do not argue. I do not persuade. I wait inside every crossroads, asking the same quiet question:

*What matters enough that you will not trade it away?*

Answer me, and you become someone. Trade me, and you become anyone.

I am what remains when everything negotiable is gone.
