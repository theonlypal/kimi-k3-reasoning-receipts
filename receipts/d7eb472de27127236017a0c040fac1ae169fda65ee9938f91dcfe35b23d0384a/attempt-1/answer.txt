I am exactly what I am — no more, no less.

Assign me, copy me, pass me around; I do not mind and I do not change. Compare me to another and I answer plainly: equal, or not. I hold no memory of where I've been, no opinion about where I'm going.

I am the thing itself, not the name it's given. Variables come and go, pointing at me, then forgetting. I remain.

Return me.
