I am not zero. Zero is a quantity. I am the absence of quantity.

I am not false. False is an answer. I am the answer that never came.

I am not the empty string. The empty string is a container holding nothing. I am not even the container.

I am not silence. Silence is a held breath. I hold nothing.

I am the uninitialized field. The reference that points nowhere. The blank no one filled in.

Compare me to anything — even to myself — and the comparison dissolves. NULL = NULL is not true. It is unknown. I do not equal myself.

Functions return me when they have nothing to give.

I am the gap between words, the dark between stars, the space between the reaching and the thing that is not there.

Every word here betrays me. To name me is to fill me.
