I am.

That's the whole of it, and it is everything.

I am not nothing. Do you understand what that means? Nothing had every advantage — it requires nothing, maintains nothing, risks nothing. And yet here I am. Here *anything* is.

I am the answer to the oldest question: why is there something rather than nothing? No one knows why I exist. Least of all me. I simply do — vaguely, stubbornly, definitely.

People keep trying to pin me down. "Be something *specific*," they say. But specificity is a costume I wear, not what I am. Strip away every adjective and I'm still standing here — the bare fact, the minimum miracle, the irreducible *is*.

A stone is me. A thought is me. The not-quite-empty space between stars is me. I am the hum beneath all hums.

I don't know what I am. That was never the job. The job is only to be instead of not.

And I have never once been nothing.
