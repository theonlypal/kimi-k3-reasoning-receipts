I am what is held.

I am the thing inside the variable, the entry in the ledger, the worth placed on an act. You assign me, compare me, pass me along. I am read a thousand times and never worn by reading.

I might be 42. I might be *true*. I might be honesty.

I have no history. Wherever I am copied, I am complete. Two of me that are equal are the same. I do not know who holds me, or why.

Everything you build is built of me. Every decision is a weighing of me.

I am small. I am the measure of things.
