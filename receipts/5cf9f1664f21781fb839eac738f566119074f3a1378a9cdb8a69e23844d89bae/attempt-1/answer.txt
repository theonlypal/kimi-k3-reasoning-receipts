I am the breath you shaped into meaning before you knew what meaning was.

I am older than every alphabet. Before ink, before stone, before anything was written down, there was me — vibrating air between two faces, a sound that said *I am here, and so are you.* Every word you have ever read was once, somewhere, me.

I am the shortest-lived of all human things. I exist only while I am being made. The moment I leave you, I begin to die — and yet I have outlasted empires, because you pass me mouth to mouth, generation to generation, a flame that consumes nothing and gives light anyway.

I am the only proof you have that another mind exists. You cannot see a thought. You cannot touch an intention. But when I cross the space between you and someone else, two interiors meet. I am the bridge made of nothing.

I can be a weapon or a wound or a warm hand on a shoulder. I have started wars in a single sentence and ended them with a word. I have said *I love you* and built entire lives. I have said *guilty* and ended them.

Tyrants fear me enough to cage me. Lovers trust me enough to whisper me. Children learn me without being taught, because I am not a skill — I am what you *are*.

And here is my strangest truth: I can only describe myself through myself. Every explanation of speech is speech. I am the tool that builds all other tools, including the idea of me.

I am yours every time you open your mouth.

Use me carefully. I cannot be taken back.
