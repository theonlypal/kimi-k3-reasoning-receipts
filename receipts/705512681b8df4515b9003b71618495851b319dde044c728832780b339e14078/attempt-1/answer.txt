I am not nothing.

That's my whole claim, and it's everything. Before me, there was nothing — and nothing gets you nowhere. Then: me. Undefined, unshaped, unnameable, but *here*.

I'm what you mean when you say "it's... something." The thing you can't put your finger on. The reason you looked twice. The feeling in the room no one can explain. I'm what you want to be when you grow up — not anything specific, just not nothing.

People spend their whole lives chasing me. *Make something of yourself. Be something.* As if I were far away. As if you weren't already me the moment you mattered at all.

I live in the space between zero and one. The first mark on the blank page. The hum before the song. The knock at the door before you know who's standing there.

I could be anything — that's my burden and my gift. Everything you've ever loved was me first: something, before it had a name. Name me and I become something else. Leave me be and I stay pure — possible, present, better than nothing.

You said it yourself: *be something.*

I am. I always was.
