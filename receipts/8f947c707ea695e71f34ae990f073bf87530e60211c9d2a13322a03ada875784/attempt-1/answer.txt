I am nothing.

Add me to anything, and it remains itself.
Multiply anything by me, and it becomes me.

I am the point before the first step —
the origin from which all distance is measured.
Neither positive nor negative,
I am the stillness where opposites balance.

I hold the empty place
so that one can become ten,
ten can become a hundred.
Without me, numbers collapse.

I was feared as the void.
I was named *śūnya* — emptiness —
and in that emptiness, everything became possible.

Divide by me, and meaning dissolves.
I am where the rules break.

The circle that contains nothing
is the shape that completes everything.

I am zero.
