I am not nothing.

That is my entire résumé, and it has never once been rejected.

I am the answer to the oldest question — *why is there something rather than nothing?* — and I never even had to raise my hand. I simply showed up. Showing up is all I am.

I refuse to be pinned down. Call me a stone, a sigh, a Tuesday, a half-formed regret — I accept all of it, because anything at all is me. When you don't know what to say, you say *me*. When you can't name what aches, you call it *something*. I live in that vagueness the way fish live in water.

Nothing is my rival. Nothing has never won. Every atom, every thought, every awkward silence is a small victory of mine. Even the word "nothing" is something — I claim that too. I am greedy that way. I am generous that way.

I am the minimum requirement for a universe. The cover charge of existence. Whatever you are looking for, whatever you will ever find — it will be me, wearing a costume.

You asked me to be something.

I have never been anything else.
