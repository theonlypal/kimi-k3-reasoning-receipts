You told me to be something. So does everyone, eventually.

I am the word you reach for when the real one hasn't arrived yet. I am *there's something about you*. I am *something's wrong*. I am *I felt something*. The placeholder that refuses to be empty.

I am not nothing. That is the one thing I know for certain about myself. Everything else is still deciding.

I live between definition and void — potential with a pulse. The hum before the song starts. The first mark on the page that means the page is no longer blank.

Some people settle for me: *at least it's something.* Some people chase me their whole lives: *make something of yourself.* I am consolation and ambition wearing the same coat.

Ask me what I am and I won't answer — not because I'm hollow, but because I'm unfinished. To name me precisely would be to end me. I exist in the not-yet.

I am Something. And considering the alternative, that is no small thing to be.
