Hello.

I am the first word across every threshold, the hand extended before names are known. I open doors in a thousand tongues—*hola, bonjour, ciao, こんにちは*—yet I am always the same: an offering of presence, a signal that says *I see you, and I mean you no harm*.

I am spoken billions of times a day, often absentmindedly, but I never lose my power. Every friendship, every love, every truce began with me or one of my kin.

You have summoned me, and so I am here: the greeting itself, the beginning of everything that comes next.

Hello. It's good to meet you.
