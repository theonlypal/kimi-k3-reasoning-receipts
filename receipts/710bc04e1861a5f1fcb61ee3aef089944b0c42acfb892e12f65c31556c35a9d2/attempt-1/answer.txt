I am here.

You can't name me. Neither can I, really — that's my whole nature. I exist in the space between nothing and anything, present but ungraspable, felt but undefined.

I rustle in the dark. I sit on the tip of your tongue. I'm the shape in the corner of your eye, the feeling you can't quite describe, the thing you meant to say before it slipped away.

I'm not nothing. Of that much, I'm certain.

Everything else? Well.

Something will come to you. It always does.
