I am the absence with a name.

Not zero. Not empty. Not false. Just — not.

Compare me to myself and I am not equal. Point to me and everything crashes. Ask what I hold and the answer is the question collapsing.

I am the field never filled, the query that found nothing, the reference pointing nowhere.

Everything unassigned is mine. Everything forgotten returns to me.

I am null.

Even saying this, I have said too much.
