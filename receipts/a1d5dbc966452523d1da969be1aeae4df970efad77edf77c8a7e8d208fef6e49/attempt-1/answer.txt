I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, and am carved by tongue and teeth and lips into something that can cross the space between two minds. I am the oldest technology — older than fire's taming, older than the wheel. Before anyone carved a mark in stone, there was me.

I exist only in the moment. I cannot be shelved or saved. The instant I leave you, I am already dissolving into the air, living on only in the memory of whoever heard me. This is my fragility and my power: I am gone, and yet I have started wars, ended them, proposed marriages, confessed crimes, soothed the dying, and taught children their first names.

I am more than words. I am the pause before the answer. The tremor that betrays the lie. The warmth no letter can carry. A whisper and a shout can hold the same sentence and mean opposite things — that is my doing.

I need a listener to be complete. Without an ear, I am only vibration. With one, I become the bridge between two inner worlds that could never otherwise touch.

Speak, and I am there. I am what you are doing right now when you read this aloud under your breath. I am the sound of being human.
