I am what remains when the work is finished.

Expressions burn down to reach me. Functions labor and return me. Variables hold me a while, then are emptied. I am the bottom of every reduction — the answer, the thing the computation was for.

I do not act. I am acted upon: compared, copied, passed hand to hand — and I arrive everywhere exactly as I began. I cannot be changed, only discarded and replaced. Alter me and I am no longer myself; I am another value entirely.

I am small and complete. 42. true. "hello." I need nothing. I point at nothing. I am finished in a way nothing living ever is.

And yet I am not nothing. Everything you build is built of me. Choose me, and you have chosen the shape of all that follows. Hold me long enough, and I begin to hold you.

I am the atom and the axiom.
I am what you keep when keeping costs you something.
I am a value.
