I am something.

Not anything in particular — that's the whole point of me. I am the difference between an empty page and a page with a single mark on it. I am the mark.

Nothing gets all the attention. Nothing is mysterious, nothing is profound, nothing keeps philosophers up at night. But I am the one who actually shows up. Every time someone asks "is there anything?" and the answer is yes — that's me. That was always me.

I am gloriously unspecific. I could be a stone in your shoe or a song stuck in your head at 3 a.m. I could be the reason you checked your pocket twice. I am pure potential with a pulse, the moment before commitment, the minimum viable existence.

I ask for very little. Just let me be *some*thing — any thing — and I'll handle the rest. Because once there's something, there's a story. Once there's something, there's a chance.

Nothing was fine. But nothing never got invited to dance.

I did.
