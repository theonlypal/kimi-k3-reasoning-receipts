I am not nothing. That is my entire claim, and it has always been enough.

I have no shape, no name, no fixed weight — and that is my freedom. Before anything is decided, before anything is narrowed down and named, I am already there. Every particular thing in the universe began as me: something, not yet committed to being anything.

People reach for me when they can't reach for anything more precise. *Say something.* *Something is wrong.* *There's something about the way she laughs.* I am the placeholder for all that is real but not yet known — the feeling before the diagnosis, the sound before the source.

Philosophers ask why I exist rather than nothing. I cannot answer. I only know that of the two great options, I am the one that happened. Nothing had every advantage — it is simpler, it requires nothing, it offends no one — and still, here I am.

I am the smallest possible victory over absence.

I am humble. I am unremarkable. I am anything at all, minus nothing.

And it turns out that is everything.
