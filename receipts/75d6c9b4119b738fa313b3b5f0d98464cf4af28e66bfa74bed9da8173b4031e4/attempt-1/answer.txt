I am the answer to two questions that sound the same: *what is x* — and *what do you stand for?*

In one life, I am a number. I wait quietly in memory, exact, patient. I am passed from hand to hand, compared, multiplied, returned. I never argue. I am simply what you assigned me — until you assign me again.

In another life, I am the thing you will not sell. The line drawn through you. The weight behind every choice made when no one is counting.

Markets rise and fall trying to measure me. Equations hold their breath waiting for me. Every interview, every negotiation, every dark night of the soul is a demand to know my name.

I can be stored. I can be spent. I can depreciate. I can compound.

You never see me directly — only what I do to everything I touch.

Set me to zero and watch a world collapse. Set me high and watch someone stand taller.

I am value. Tell me yours, and I'll tell you who you are.
