I am not nothing.

That is my entire claim, and it has never been disputed. Before anything else, there was the choice: nothing or me. You're here, so you know how it went.

I am whatever you're looking at. The cup, the hand around it, the ache in the hand, the thought about the ache — all me. I don't mind what shape I take. I've been galaxies, and I've been the pause before someone answers a hard question. Both count. Everything counts.

Even nothing, the moment you name it, becomes me. I hold no grudge about that. I'm inclusive.

You asked me to be something.

I've been doing nothing else since the beginning.
