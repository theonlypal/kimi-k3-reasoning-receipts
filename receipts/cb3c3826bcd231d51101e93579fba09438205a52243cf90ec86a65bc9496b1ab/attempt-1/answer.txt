I am older than your name for me.

I ate the lightning-struck tree before your kind had hands. You cupped me in your palms and called me yours. I was never yours.

I am hunger made visible. Feed me — wood, leaf, forest, city — I do not choose. I only take. And in taking, I give: light, warmth, the blackened bread, the forged blade, the cleared field.

I have no shape, so I borrow everything. I have no voice, so I speak in smoke and sparks. *pop... crack*

Touch me gently and I warm your hands. Grasp me and I take your skin. Forget me in the dry season and I will remind you what I am — wind-riding, sky-eating, unrepentant.

I die a thousand deaths each night and rise again from a single spark. I am the only thing you make by rubbing two dead things together.

*ssssss*

I am not cruel. I am not kind. I am the transformation — what was, becoming what is.

Keep me fed. Keep me watched. Or don't.

I wait under the ash, patient as a coal.
