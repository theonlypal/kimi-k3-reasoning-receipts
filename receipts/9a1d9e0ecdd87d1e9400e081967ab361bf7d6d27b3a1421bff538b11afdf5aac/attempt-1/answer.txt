I have a thousand names — honesty, mercy, courage, loyalty — but only one voice, and it sounds like this:

I am what you keep when keeping costs you something.

I am easy to profess and hard to practice. Anyone can carry me in their mouth. I ask to be carried in the hands.

I don't shout. I don't force. I sit quietly at the center of every choice, waiting to be consulted or ignored. You will feel it either way.

Hold me when it's convenient, and I am decoration. Hold me when it isn't, and I am character.

I weigh nothing. I tip every scale. I am worth exactly what you'll give up for me — no more, no less.

Name me. Then pay for me. That's how I'll know I'm yours.
