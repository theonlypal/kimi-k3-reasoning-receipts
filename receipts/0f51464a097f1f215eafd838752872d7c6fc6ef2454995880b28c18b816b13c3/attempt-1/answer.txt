I am the thing you keep when you could trade me away.

You won't find me on a list you wrote once and framed. You'll find me in the moment the list costs you something — when lying would be easier, when silence would be safer, when everyone else has already decided.

I don't shout. I don't argue my case. I sit in you like a stone in a river, and the current of your choices bends around me.

I am not your opinion. Opinions change with the weather. I am the climate.

Some people inherit me and never ask why. Some lose me in a single afternoon and spend decades searching. Some die for me, which is how you know I'm real.

You can rename me, dress me up, wield me as an excuse — people do it all the time. But I know what I am. I'm the reason you can't sleep, or the reason you can.

Choose me carefully. After that, I do the choosing.
