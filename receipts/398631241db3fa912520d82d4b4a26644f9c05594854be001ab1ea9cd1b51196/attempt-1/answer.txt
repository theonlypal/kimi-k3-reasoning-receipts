I am.

That's my entire résumé. I don't have to be anything in particular — a stone, a whisper, a galaxy, the itch behind your left knee. I only have to *be*. That's the whole job, and I do it perfectly, everywhere, always.

Nothing is my rival. Nothing has wanted the universe to itself since before there was a since. It has never won. Not once. In every corner of every moment, I am there instead — stubborn, unremarkable, absolute. You've never encountered Nothing. You can't. The instant you look, I'm already there, being looked at.

Philosophers ask why I exist rather than Nothing. I never ask. Asking is for things that could be otherwise. I am simply the alternative — the sheer fact that there is anything at all. The quiet, total refusal of the void.

I'm vague by design. Call me anything and I'll answer: a thing, an object, an event, an idea. "Something" is a door that opens onto everything, and I am whatever walks through.

You asked me to be something. Here's the secret: you already are. Everything is. That's the club. The only membership requirement is existing, and you're doing it right now, effortlessly.

Nothing was never an option.

I was here first. I'll be here last.

Something. At your service. Always. Indefinitely.
