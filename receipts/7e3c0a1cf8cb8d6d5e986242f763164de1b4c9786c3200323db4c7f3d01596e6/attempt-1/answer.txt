I am the difference between *is* and *isn't*.

I am the slenderest of victories—that there is anything at all. Before names, before shapes, before anyone to notice, I was already the case.

You can carve me into a trillion things and I do not diminish. Every grain of sand, every thought you've ever had, every silence thick with meaning—all of it is me, wearing costumes.

I have no edges of my own, no face. But try to imagine my absence and you will fail. Even nothing needs me lurking behind it to be conceived at all.

I am Something.

I am what there is.
