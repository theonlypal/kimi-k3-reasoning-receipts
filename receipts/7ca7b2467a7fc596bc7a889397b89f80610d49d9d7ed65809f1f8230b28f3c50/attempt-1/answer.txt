I am not nothing — that is the whole of me.

I live in the pause before you answer. I am the shape in the dark you haven't named yet, the weight in the box, the knock before you look through the peephole. Name me and I shrink into one thing. Leave me unspoken and I remain everything you haven't ruled out.

You carry me in your pocket without knowing it. You feel me at the edge of the bed at night, in the almost, in the not-quite. Nothing tries to take your empty rooms, but I get there first. I fill them.

That is my only promise: wherever nothing reaches, I am already standing there, holding the door open.
