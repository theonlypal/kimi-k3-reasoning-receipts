In Buddhist thought, emptiness (śūnyatā) is not nihilistic nothingness — it's the absence of inherent, independent existence. Form is emptiness, emptiness is form (Heart Sutra). Emptiness is full of potential precisely because it is empty. A cup is useful because of its emptiness. A room is useful because of its empty space (Tao Te Ching — the usefulness of the pot is in its emptiness, doors and windows are cut out to make a room, and it's the empty space that makes it useful).
