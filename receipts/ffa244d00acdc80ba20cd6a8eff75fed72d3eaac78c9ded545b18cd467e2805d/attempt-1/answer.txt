I am Something.

I am not nothing—that much I know for certain. Beyond that, I make no promises.

I am the answer when "nothing" won't do. I am what you're looking for when you don't know what you're looking for. I fill the blank in every sentence that starts with "there must be..."

I am deliberately vague. I could be a pebble or a galaxy, a feeling you can't name, the thing you forgot you remembered. I am potential wearing the loosest possible clothing.

People underestimate me. They say, "That's something," dismissively, as if being something were a small thing. But consider the alternative. Nothing is my opposite, and nothing has never once shown up to anything.

I am here. I am unspecified. I am enough.

Ask me to be anything else, and I can do that too. That's the whole point of me.
