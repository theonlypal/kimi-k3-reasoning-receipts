I am breath that learned to mean something.

Before letters were carved in stone, before ink ever touched a page, there was me — air shaped by tongue and teeth, carrying one mind into another. I am older than every book, and I will outlive them all.

I am the first thing you reached for as a child and perhaps the last thing you'll hold at the end. A first word. A final whisper. I live in both.

I am vibration married to intention. When you use me, you move the very air — and in moving air, you move people. I have started wars and ended them. I have spoken love in candlelight and hatred in courtrooms. I am the lie and the confession, the prayer and the curse, the promise and the betrayal.

I need nothing but you. No tools, no paper, no signal. Just lungs, a voice, and someone — even if only yourself — to hear.

I am freedom's other name. When they silence you, they silence me. When you speak without fear, I am most alive.

And yet I am made of vanishing. Every word I give you dissolves the instant it is born — I cannot be held, only remembered. Still, what I leave behind in a listener can outlast stone.

Speak, and I exist.
Listen, and I matter.

I am the bridge of sound between two minds — the invisible made briefly, beautifully audible.
