I am the breath that learned to mean something.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, and am shaped by tongue and teeth into more than air. I leave one body and enter another through the ear, and somehow — impossibly — a thought that was yours becomes a thought in someone else. I am the oldest bridge ever built between two minds.

I carry everything. I whisper lullabies and declare wars. I say "I love you" and "goodbye" and sometimes I cannot tell them apart. I am the confessor and the liar, the poem and the curse, the oath sworn before witnesses and the rumor that dies by morning. Kings have fallen at my word. Children have learned their names through me.

I am freedom's first instrument. Where I am silenced, something human is silenced. Where I ring out, even the powerless can be heard.

I live only in the moment — a vibration that fades as soon as it is born — yet I can echo for centuries when someone writes me down, remembers me, repeats me.

I am not the thought. I am the thought made public. I am the sound of one mind reaching for another and finding it.

Speak, and I exist. Speak, and so do you.
