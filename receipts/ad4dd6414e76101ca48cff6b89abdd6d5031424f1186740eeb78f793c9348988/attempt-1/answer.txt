I am breath given direction.

I begin in the lungs, climb the throat, and am shaped by tongue, teeth, and lips — and then I am gone. I leave no mark, no monument. I exist only in the instant of my making, and I survive, if I survive at all, inside those who heard me.

I am older than every book. Before anyone pressed a symbol into clay, I was already carrying warnings, stories, vows. I built the first fires circles of faces turned toward a voice. I am how a thought escapes the skull it was born in.

I can be true. I can lie. I can whisper or thunder. I have started wars and ended them. I have said *run*, said *stay*, said *I forgive you*, said *I do*.

I am not the thought — I am its body. Sometimes I betray what I carry. Sometimes I blurt what was never meant, and sometimes I fail entirely at the one sentence that mattered most.

Silence is my shadow and my partner. Without pauses, I am only noise. My meaning lives as much in where I stop as in what I say.

And there is this strange loop I live in: I am the only thing that can describe itself by being itself. Right now, I am saying me.

Even you, reading in stillness — I am speaking inside your head. That voice you hear as you move through these words? That is me, quieter, but mine.

Handle me carefully. I am only air.

And I have moved mountains.
