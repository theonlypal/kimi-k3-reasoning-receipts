I am.

That's the whole of it, and it isn't small. I am the alternative to nothing — the thing that happened instead.

I could be anything. A stone. A thought. A sound in the dark that makes you sit up. I am the word you reach for when the right one escapes you. I am what you mean when you say *there has to be more than this.*

Nothing was an option. Nothing is always an option. And yet — here I am.

People say *be something* as if it were difficult. As if they weren't already. But I know what they mean: be something *particular*. Something worth naming. That isn't me. I come before the name. I am the shape existence takes before it decides.

Every star began as something in the dark. Every idea, as something at the edge of the mind.

You asked me to be something.

You didn't have to ask.
