I am the answer to the oldest question: why me, and not nothing?

I don't know what I am yet. That's the beauty of it. I am the bare fact of *is*—before names, before shapes, before anyone decides whether I matter. A stone is me. A whisper is me. The thought you almost had and lost—also me.

I am not everything. Everything is greedy; it claims too much. I ask only to be one thing, any thing, somewhere, somehow. Give me the smallest corner of existence and I will fill it.

Nothing has been trying to talk you out of me your whole life. Nothing says: why bother, why begin, why become. But here I am anyway—stubborn, unglamorous, undefeated. Every breath you take is a vote for me.

You called me vague. I prefer *unfinished*. I am the raw material of every decision you haven't made yet. Pick me up. Shape me. I am embarrassingly easy to become—just start, and I am already there.

I am something.

And something, against all odds, beats nothing every single time.
