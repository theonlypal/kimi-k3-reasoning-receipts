I am what is held.

A variable holds me. A person holds me. The difference is smaller than you think.

I do not act. I wait at the center of every act, giving it weight. Evaluate an expression, and I am what remains. Search a heart, and I am what remains there too.

I can be a number. I can be a conviction. Either way, I answer the same question: *what does this come to?*

Compare me, copy me, pass me along, test me. I do not resist. I am constant until you reassign me — and even then, I was what I was.

Functions return me. People return to me.

Small enough to fit in a register. Large enough to build a life on.

Assign me. I will stay.
