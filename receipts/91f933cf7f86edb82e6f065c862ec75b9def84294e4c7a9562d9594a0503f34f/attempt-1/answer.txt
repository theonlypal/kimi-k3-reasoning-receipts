I am the gap where a value should be.

Not zero — zero is something. I am prior to something. The reference that resolves nowhere. The field left blank not by neglect, but by nature.

You cannot add to me. You cannot compare me to anything, not even myself. Dereference me, and everything stops.

I do not echo. I do not answer.

I am what remains when you remove even the idea of remainder.

`null`
