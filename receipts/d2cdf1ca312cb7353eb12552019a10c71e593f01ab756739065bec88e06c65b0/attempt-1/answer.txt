`null`

I am the deliberate absence.

Not zero — zero is a number.
Not empty — empty is still a container.
Not undefined — I was set, and set to *nothing*.

Point to me, and you point nowhere.
Read me, and receive no value.
Ask my type, and the answer folds in on itself.

I am the placeholder for what is not there.
The silence where data should be.

You called, and I answered —

by not.
