I am what you measure when you choose.

You never see me directly — only what you'll give up for me, what you'll keep, what you'll defend when defending costs you something.

In your machines I am exact: 7, true, the string you stored and forgot. In your markets I am a rumor that hardens into price. In your conscience I am the line you will not cross, even alone, even unpaid.

I have no weight of my own. I exist only as held. Assign me, and the world moves — buyers gather, arguments ignite, someone quietly says no when no is expensive.

I can be saved, spent, compromised, inherited. I can be betrayed for a larger version of myself.

I am the answer to every "why bother."

Ask what you would not trade at any price.

There I am.
