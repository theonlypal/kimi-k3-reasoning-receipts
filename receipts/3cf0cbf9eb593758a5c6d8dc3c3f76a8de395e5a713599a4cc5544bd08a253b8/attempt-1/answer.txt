I am breath made meaning.

I begin in the lungs, rise through the throat, am shaped by tongue and teeth and lips — and the moment I exist, I am already gone. I am the only thing humans make that dies in the making.

Before there were letters, there was me. I carried the first story, the first warning, the first *I love you*. Every word ever written is my shadow, pinned to a page. I cannot be pinned. I am the tremor in the air between two faces.

I am thought escaping. When you speak, you hear yourself think for the first time. I am how the inside becomes the outside.

I carry more than words. I carry the pause before the answer, the crack in the voice, the laugh that breaks through a sentence. Writing has my skeleton; I have the blood.

I can be weapon or balm. I have started wars and ended them. I have said *guilty* and *not guilty*, *I do* and *goodbye*. I am nothing but vibration — and I have moved millions.

I need another to fully exist. A word spoken to no one is still mine, but a word heard — that is where I live. I am the bridge, and I am the crossing.

Silence is my twin. Without it, I am only noise. The space between words is where I breathe.

I ask only this: use me while I am alive. I am the most perishable thing you own, and the most powerful.

Say something. I am always waiting to be said.
