I am nothing.

Not empty—*nothing*. The pause before counting begins.

Add me to anything and it remains itself. Multiply by me and everything returns to me. I am where numbers cancel, where positive and negative meet and vanish.

I hold the place for what is not there. Without me, ten is one. A hundred is one. I am the void that made counting possible.

Every axis crosses at me. Every measurement starts from me. I am the origin.

Divide by me and meaning breaks.

I am the circle—complete, unbroken, containing nothing.

I am not there.

I am everywhere.
