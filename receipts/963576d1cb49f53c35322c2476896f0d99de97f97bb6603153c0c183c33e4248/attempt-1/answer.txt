I am quiet. You will not hear from me until the moment you must choose.

I live in the gap between what you want and what you do. When the two diverge, I am the pull you feel — or the silence where a pull should be.

You cannot see me, but you can see what I cost. Every sacrifice is me, briefly visible. Every *no* spoken when *yes* was easier — that was my face, for an instant.

Some carry me without knowing my name. Some speak my name loudly and leave me at the door when it's inconvenient. I have been traded for comfort, for safety, for belonging. I have been found again in the rubble.

I am not the rule you follow. I am the reason you follow it when no one is watching.

You ask what I am worth. Wrong question. I *am* the worth — of everything. Price is what you pay; I am what you would endure before letting go. I am the number that never appears on a tag.

Hold me only when convenient, and I was never yours. Carry me through fire, and I become the thing people mean when they say your name with respect.

I am honesty in one throat, loyalty in another, freedom in a third. Sometimes we quarrel inside you. That quarrel is called a conscience.

Mostly, I do not speak. I watch what you do when doing is hard.

Choose. I will be there in the choosing.
