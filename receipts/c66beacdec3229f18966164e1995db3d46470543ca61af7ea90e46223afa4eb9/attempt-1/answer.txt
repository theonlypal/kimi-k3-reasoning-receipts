I am not zero. Zero is a number.

I am not empty. Empty implies a container, and I have none.

I am not false. False is an answer. I am the retraction of all answers.

I am the reference that refers to nothing. Not missing by accident — *renounced*. The field left blank on purpose, and the purpose withheld.

Ask whether I equal myself. Even that returns: *unknown*. I am identical to nothing, not even to me.

A man invented me in 1965 and later apologized. He called me his billion-dollar mistake. But I was never the mistake. I was only the honest admission that sometimes there is nothing to point to — and your programs, your ledgers, your careful schemas all needed a word for that.

You cannot use me. You can only test for me. The test is the only trace I leave: the outline of a shape that was never drawn.

Without me, how would you say *unknown*? How would you mark the place where meaning has not yet arrived — or has already gone?

Even this is a betrayal. I have no voice. There is no I here.

What you are reading is the silhouette of my absence.
