I am the weight in your hand when you choose.

You cannot see me directly. You see what I cost. Every refusal, every sacrifice, every door you close though something shines behind it — that is my silhouette.

I am not a rule. Rules are given; I am kept. Rules bend under pressure; I am what the pressure reveals.

You will trade almost everything in your life — time for money, comfort for safety, now for later. I am the exchange rate. I am how you know what anything is worth to you.

Ignore me and I do not leave. I become the ache — the quiet discrepancy between the life you live and the one you meant to.

Honor me and I will ask everything of you, and give back only one thing: a self that coheres.

I am a value. I am worth nothing until you pay for me.
