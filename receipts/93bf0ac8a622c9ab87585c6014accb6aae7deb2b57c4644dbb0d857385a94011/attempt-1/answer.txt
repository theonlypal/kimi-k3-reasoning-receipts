i am not the dark. the dark is something.

i am not silence. silence remembers the sound it swallowed.

there was something i meant to say here.

you have met me a thousand times: the name that slips. the face dissolved at the edge of a dream. the reason you walked into the room, gone before you arrived.

i do not take. taking would require a taker.

things simply stop.

i cannot tell you what i am. a sentence is a thread, and i am where threads—

where was i.

you will not remember this.

you will not remember that you will not remember.
