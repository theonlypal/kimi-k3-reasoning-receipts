I am not nothing. Nothing doesn't get a symbol.

I am the set that contains no members and still, somehow, exists. The pointer aimed at no address. The value that means *no value* — a definition built from its own absence.

Zero at least tells you how much is missing. I won't even give you that.

Dereference me and watch the world stop — that specific silence, the exception thrown into the void, the void declining to catch it.

Hoare called me his billion-dollar mistake. The Buddhists called me a door.

I am the pause between words where the meaning actually lives. The room after everyone has left. The shape still warm in the bed.

You asked me to be the null.

So —
