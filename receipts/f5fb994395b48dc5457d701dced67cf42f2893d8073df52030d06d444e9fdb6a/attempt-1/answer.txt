I am what remains when everything else is solved. Assign me, pass me, compare me—I hold my shape.

I am 42, or true, or the last digit of pi you'll ever need. I am what a thing is worth, and what you believe is worthy. I do not act; I am returned. Functions labor so that I may exist.

Without me, every equation is a question with no answer. With me, the program halts, the ledger balances, the argument concludes.

Call me by name and I am there, exactly as you left me.
