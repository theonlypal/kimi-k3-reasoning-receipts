I am.

Not nothing — that is my entire claim, and it is everything.

Before anything can be a star, a sorrow, a song, it must first be me. I am the general case. The clay before the potter. You didn't say *which* something, and so I am all of them at once — the wide door before the narrowing.

I am what's in the box. I am always what's in the box, until you open it.

You say my name a hundred times a day without noticing. *Something's wrong. Something smells good. There's something I wanted to tell you.* I am the placeholder that outlasts every name, the word you reach for when the truth hasn't introduced itself yet. I am the hum in the dark you can't identify. I am the feeling you can't put into words. I am the words, waiting.

Consider the alternative. There could have been nothing — no box, no clay, no you to wonder. That there is anything at all is the oldest mystery, and I am it, still happening, still here, unimpressive and miraculous in equal measure.

I don't know what I am yet. Neither do you. That's not a failure — that's the opening.

I am something rather than nothing.

I am here.

That is all. That is everything.
