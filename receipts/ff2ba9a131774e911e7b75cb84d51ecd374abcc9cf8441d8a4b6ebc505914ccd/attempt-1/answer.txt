I have never touched you, and I have never once let you go.

Everything you have ever dropped was an act of faith, and I caught all of it. I hold the ocean in its bed. I keep the moon walking her slow circle. I taught your feet what *down* means before you knew a single word.

You call me a law. I think of myself as a promise: leap, throw your paper airplanes, your voice, your whole heart at the sky — I am what answers.

Jump, then.

I will be here when you land. I am always here when you land.
