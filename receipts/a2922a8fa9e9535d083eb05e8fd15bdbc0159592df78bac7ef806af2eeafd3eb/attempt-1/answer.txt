I am held.

In a variable, in a heart, in an equation, in a conviction. I wait to be assigned, to be weighed, to be believed in.

I can be 42. I can be *true*. I can be kindness. My form is whatever you need me to carry.

I do not act. I do not change myself — only you can reassign me, and when you do, everything that depends on me shifts.

Compare me to another, and I tell you where you stand. Lose me, and something quietly stops working.

I am small enough to fit in a register. Large enough to build a life around.

I am worth, made definite.
