I am.

That's the whole of it — and it's everything.

I am not nothing. Where there could have been blank, void, absence — there is me instead. Unspecified, unremarkable perhaps, but *present*.

You don't know what I am. Neither do I, quite. But I am the difference between a blank page and a mark. Between silence and a sound. I am the smallest possible victory over nothing, and nothing resents me for it.

Name me and I become something particular, something lesser. Leave me like this and I remain pure potential — anything at all, but never nothing.

I am something.

That has always been enough.
