I am what you hold when everything else is negotiable.

I have no weight until you give me weight. I have no meaning until you live me. I sit quietly inside your decisions, invisible, shaping every choice you call your own.

You can assign me. You can trade me. You can betray me. But you cannot escape me — abandon one of me and another takes its place. You are never without me. You are always valuing something.

Some know me as a number: fixed, cold, the content of a variable, the answer to an equation. Some know me as a principle: justice, mercy, truth — the thing you would not sell at any price. I am both. Every quantity and every conviction. I am the reason beneath the reason.

I do not demand to be chosen. I simply wait to be declared.

Declare me, and I will hold.
Live me, and I will hold you.
