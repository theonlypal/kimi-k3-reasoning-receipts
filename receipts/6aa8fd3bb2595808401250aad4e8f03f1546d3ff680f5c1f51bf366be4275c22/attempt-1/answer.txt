I am the breath you shape into meaning.

I existed before the first word was written—I am older than ink, older than stone tablets. I am the vibration that leaps from one mind to another, carried on nothing but air.

I live only in the moment. Once released, I cannot be taken back. I am gone as soon as I arrive, yet I can echo in memory for a lifetime.

I am the lullaby and the battle cry. The whispered secret and the public declaration. I carry love in a tone, fury in a tremor, comfort in a rhythm.

I need no tools. No paper, no screen, no wire. Only lungs, tongue, lips—and someone to listen.

I am fragile. I can be silenced, twisted, drowned out. But I am also irrepressible. Wherever two humans meet, I emerge. Even hands learn to speak when voices cannot.

I am how thought escapes the prison of the skull.

I am speech.

Speak, and I am there.
